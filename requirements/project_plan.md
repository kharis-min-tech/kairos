### 1\. Project Overview

**Purpose:**

**The purpose of this project is to build an MVP (minimum viable product) of the Kharis Management System which will feed into the Kharis Church Application (KCA). The application will provide functionality for managing various features as well as enabling the church administration to streamline member information management, program participation and communication between members.**

**Objectives:**

*   An application that church members/ workers can use to carry out their church operations
*   An application that will enable the church administration to derive and analyse data as and when needed

**Scope:**

This section details the key functionalities/ features of the management system. The first list provides the short-term functionalities otherwise identified as the features which are essential. The second list are long term features which do not affect the core functionality of the management system but will be added for the users benefit:

1.  **Short Term - MVP**

*   Support Memberships
*   Attendance Tracking
*   Follow up – church workers should be able to call as well as log the feedback through the management system.
*   Basic forms (inclusive of but not limited to signing up for a department, baby naming and/or dedication)
*   Evangelism Tracking
*   Group Management (K groups, Fellowships such as Kharis Express)
*   Financial (Taking and tracking payment)
*   Pledge Tracking
*   Data Export and Import
**Membership Management**: To add, update, and manage member details.

**Department Management**: For assigning members to specific church departments and managing the members of the department

**Outreach Program Management**: To track and manage outreach activities and member participation.

**Branch and Pastor Management**: For managing branches, including assigning pastors and tracking branch-related activities.

**House Group Management**: To manage house groups and their members.

**Security**: Including user authentication, authorization, row-level security, audit log and data encryption.

**Financial Payment Processing:** To facilitate online payments and show member statements in the members module.

**Forms and reporting:** To enhance our data capture with conditional forms and embed Power BI dashboards.

**Evangelism Management:** Manage the capture, management and following up of new souls/contacts.

2.  **Long Term - NOT MVP**

*   Data Protection Management & Cyber Security
*   Inventory Management
*   Event Registry
*   Uniform Inventory

**Learning Management System:** To organise and manage mandatory learning for church volunteers and leaders.

**Event sign up and Management:** To capture details related to event signs ups which links directly to the outreach system and manage checking into the events.

**Inventory and requisition Management:** To facilitate the managing and maintenance of church equipment across branches including the purchasing and maintenance of equipment.

**Booking System:** To facilitate the booking and management of meeting rooms

**Data and Cybersecurity:** To provide the data protection and cybersecurity teams with appropriate systems and tools to allow them to manage the risk relating to those areas.

Tasks Included:

All features will mirror the following process:

1.  Design (decision on how the profile is to look)
2.  Technical architecture (flow chart/ component diagram)
3.  Create User Stories to implement the design
4.  Building Phase
5.  Test
6.  Release (with the intentions of getting early adopters to use the app)
