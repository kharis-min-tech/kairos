# Church Health

"Church health" is not one number in Kairos. It is a handful of figures in a handful of places, and this page says which figure answers which question.

::: warning If you are looking for the old engagement score
Earlier versions of the dashboard carried a "Mission Summary" band with a single High / Medium / Low engagement score, and a "Mission Control Reports" strip of six mini-charts. Both have been removed. A single composite score hid more than it showed: two branches could score the same for completely different reasons, and neither reason was visible. What replaced it is a small set of figures you can actually act on, each in the place where you would act.
:::

## Where each question is answered

| The question | Where to look |
|--------------|---------------|
| How big are we, and how many are formally Members? | **Dashboard → Pulse** |
| Is anything wrong right now? | **Dashboard → Pulse warnings** and **Needs you** |
| Are people coming, and how often? | [Attendance Reports](/analytics/attendance-reports) |
| Who has stopped coming? | The heatmap and the Dormant band in [Attendance Reports](/analytics/attendance-reports) |
| Are we growing, or just churning? | [Growth Reports](/analytics/growth-reports) |
| Are new people arriving, and do they come back? | First-time versus returning, in [Attendance Reports](/analytics/attendance-reports#first-time-vs-returning) |
| Are converts being discipled? | [New Believers](/platform/new-believers) |
| Are regulars becoming Members? | [Membership Classes](/platform/membership) |
| Is anybody connected to nothing? | The no-group queue in [Follow-ups](/platform/follow-ups#no-group) |
| Is evangelism converting? | [Souls Dashboard](/platform/souls-dashboard) |

## The figures worth watching

### Confirmed members against total congregation

Both appear in Pulse. The gap between them is the size of your discipleship backlog: people who are part of the church and have not yet done the [membership class](/platform/membership). A gap that only grows means the class is not running often enough.

### Attendance rate against engaged members

An attendance rate is only meaningful once you know what it divides by. [Attendance Reports](/analytics/attendance-reports#the-engaged-member-denominator) divides by **engaged members**, meaning people who have attended at least once in a window you choose (3, 6, or 12 months), rather than by the whole roll. Dividing by the roll makes every branch look bad, because the roll includes people who moved away years ago.

### The Dormant band

In the attendance frequency breakdown, Dormant counts people on the active roll with **no** attendance in the whole window. This is the single most actionable health number in the system: it is a list of names, and it is the list the [no-group and due follow-up queues](/platform/follow-ups) exist to work through.

### Pulse warnings

Pulse surfaces these on its own, as links rather than as numbers to interpret:

- A service register that was never taken
- Members drifting out of attendance
- Branches falling behind
- A branch with no Main Pastor

### Follow-up coverage

Souls with no contact in seven days or more are counted as **Critical** on the [Souls Dashboard](/platform/souls-dashboard). Stalled New Believers enrolments, meaning no progress in seven days or more, are surfaced as a warning count on the [New Believers](/platform/new-believers) page.
