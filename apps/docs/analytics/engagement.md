# Engagement

Engagement asks a different question from attendance. Attendance asks whether somebody came. Engagement asks whether they are actually embedded in church life, which is what tends to decide whether they are still here next year.

::: warning The old engagement card has been removed
Earlier versions of the dashboard carried a **Member Engagement** card showing a single High / Medium / Low score. It is gone, along with the rest of the Mission Control strip. The measures below are what replaced it, and they are deliberately several numbers rather than one.
:::

## The honest denominator

Everything on this page rests on one idea: an **engaged member** is somebody who has attended at least once within a chosen window, which you set to 3, 6, or 12 months from the header of [Attendance Reports](/analytics/attendance-reports#the-engaged-member-denominator).

That window is the most consequential control in the reporting surface. Set it narrow and you are asking how you are doing with the people currently around. Set it wide and you are asking how many of everybody you have ever had are still coming. Both are real questions, and they give very different percentages for the same church.

## How often engaged members actually come

The attendance frequency breakdown splits the engaged roll into five bands over the chosen window:

| Band | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Weekly | Attended at least 75% of services |
| Biweekly | 40% to 74% |
| Monthly | 15% to 39% |
| Occasional | Under 15%, but at least once |
| Dormant | On the active roll, no attendance at all in the window |

Watch the shape, not any single bar. A church whose Weekly band is shrinking while Monthly grows is losing intensity before it loses people, which is the moment to act.

## Being embedded, not just present

Service attendance on its own is a weak signal. Somebody attending every week with no fellowship, no department, and no pastoral contact is connected to the building rather than to the church. Kairos surfaces each of those separately:

| Signal | Where to see it |
|--------|----------------|
| Attends services | The per-member heatmap in [Attendance Reports](/analytics/attendance-reports#attendance-heatmap) |
| In a fellowship | The no-group queue lists anybody who is not |
| In a department | The same queue; it requires both to be empty |
| Has been contacted | Follow-up history on the member's profile |
| Is progressing to Membership | [Membership Classes](/platform/membership) |
| Is progressing through discipleship | [New Believers](/platform/new-believers) |

## Tracking an individual

- **The heatmap** shows one row per member across the last 13 weeks, with a missing-streak count, so you can sort by who has been gone longest
- **My Attendance** gives the member their own rate, punctuality split, and streak
- **Follow-up history** on a member's profile shows every contact logged about them, across every context, with the welfare and safeguarding flags visible
- **Department and fellowship rosters** show whether they are active or stale in the group

## Improving engagement

The queues exist so this is work rather than guesswork:

- Work the **first-timer** queue within days, not weeks. It only holds people nobody has contacted at all.
- Work the **no-group** queue steadily. It clears when somebody actually joins something, which is the point.
- Work the **due** queue to keep the promises your own follow-ups made.
- Chase the **Dormant** band before those names become Occasional in a wider window.
- Run the membership class often enough that the gap between Total congregation and Confirmed members stops widening.
- Record fellowship attendance, so participation is visible at all. An unrecorded meeting looks identical to a meeting nobody came to.
