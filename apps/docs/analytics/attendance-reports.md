# Attendance Reports

Attendance reports give you a clear view of service and fellowship participation trends over time, helping you spot drops early and respond before they become patterns.

## Two places to look

| Where | What it is for |
|-------|----------------|
| **Reports → Attendance** | The headline trend: is attendance going up or down |
| **Attendance → Reports** | The detailed report surface: who is attending, how often, and who has stopped |

## The detailed report surface

From **Attendance**, open **Reports**. Everything on the page shares one control, and it is the most important one to understand.

### The engaged-member denominator

An attendance *rate* is only as meaningful as what you divide by. Dividing by the entire roll makes every branch look bad, because the roll includes people who moved away three years ago.

So the denominator here is **engaged members**: people who have attended at least once within a window you choose. Set it to **3, 6, or 12 months** from the control in the page header, and every rate, bucket, and heatmap row on the page recomputes against that choice.

A narrow window asks "how are we doing with the people who are currently around". A wide one asks "how many of the people we have ever had are still coming". Both are real questions; they give different numbers, and the window tells you which one you asked.

### Attendance heatmap

One row per engaged member, one column per service, across the last 13 weeks, oldest on the left. Each cell is colour-coded: present, virtual, late, or missed.

This is the view that finds individuals rather than trends. A horizontal band of colour that stops abruptly is somebody who dropped off, and the missing-streak column counts their consecutive recent absences so the list can be sorted by who has been gone longest. Sort instead by attendance percentage or by name as needed.

The heatmap is per branch: pick a branch and it appears.

### Attendance frequency

How the engaged roll divides by how often people actually come, over the chosen window:

| Band | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Weekly | Attended at least 75% of services |
| Biweekly | 40% to 74% |
| Monthly | 15% to 39% |
| Occasional | Under 15%, but at least once |
| Dormant | On the active roll, no attendance at all in the window |

Dormant is the band to watch. These are people the church still counts who have not been seen in the whole window.

### First-time vs returning

Weekly counts of first-time attendees against returning attendees, over the last 12 weeks. Read together these two lines tell you which of two very different problems you have: a flat first-time line means nobody new is arriving, while a healthy first-time line with a flat returning line means people arrive once and do not come back.

### Filters

Narrow any of the above by branch, by department, or by fellowship, so a department lead can see their own team's pattern rather than the branch's.

## Accessing the headline trend

Go to **Reports** and the page opens on the **Attendance** tab by default.

## Service attendance

### Weekly Attendance Rate chart

A line chart showing the average attendance rate (percentage of active members present) over the last 8 weeks. Each data point represents one week.

- A flat line near 80%+ is healthy
- A consistent downward slope needs attention
- A sharp drop on a specific date may indicate a special event or service cancellation

### Trend indicator

The chart header shows a trend direction badge:
- **Trending up**: attendance improving
- **Trending down**: attendance declining (shown in red as a warning)

### Monthly Highlight

A plain-language summary on the right side of the chart. For example:

> "Attendance needs attention. Average rate is 80% over the last 8 weeks"

Click **View breakdown** to drill into the detail.

## Fellowship attendance

### Top Fellowships Attended

A bar chart ranking fellowships by total meeting attendance. The bar length represents the number of members who attended meetings in the period. Click any bar to see that fellowship's full meeting history.

This helps you identify which fellowships are thriving and which might need pastoral support or schedule adjustments.

## My Attendance (individual view)

Every member has a personal attendance report on their **My Attendance** page:

- 12-week attendance rate
- Punctuality breakdown (Present, Late, Virtual, Missed) as a bar chart
- Current attendance streak (consecutive services attended)
- Full service history for the window, oldest first

This is private: only the member, their pastor, and admins can see it.

## Service types in reports

Attendance data includes all service types:
- Sunday Service
- Midweek Service
- Special Service
- Prayer Meeting

Filter by service type when you want to isolate Sunday attendance vs. midweek engagement.

## Exporting attendance data

Use **Export CSV** on the Attendance page to download a full attendance dataset for the current filter. Date range and branch filters apply to the export.

## Role-based scope

| Who | What they see |
|-----|--------------|
| Admin | All branches, all services |
| Branch grant | Their branch only |
| Group leader | Their fellowship or department only |
| No grants | Own attendance only (My Attendance page) |
