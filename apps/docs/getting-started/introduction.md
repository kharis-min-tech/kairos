# Introduction

Welcome to the Kairos documentation. This guide covers everything you need to know about using the Kairos church administration system, whether you're a church member, a department leader, a pastor, or a system administrator.

## Who is this for?

What you see in Kairos follows from the permissions on your account, not from a job title. In practice almost everybody's experience falls into one of four shapes:

- **Members**: View your own profile, attendance, fellowships, class progress, and submit forms.
- **Leaders**: Manage your specific department or fellowship, log follow-ups, and record attendance.
- **Pastors**: Full visibility and control over everything within your assigned branch.
- **Admins**: Complete access across all branches, regions, and system settings.

These are shorthand, used throughout this documentation. They are not roles you can be assigned. Kairos stores only two system roles, `admin` and `member`, and everything else is built from **grants**: a named role pinned to one branch, one fellowship, one department, or the whole church. [Roles & Permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions) explains how the shorthand maps onto the real thing.

## What Kairos covers

| Module | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| Branches & Regions | Manage church locations and geographic groupings |
| Members | Full member directory, profiles, approvals, CSV import/export |
| Membership Classes | The four-week class, its interest pool, cohorts, marking, and graduation |
| Fellowships | K-Groups, Kharis Express, New Breeds, and more, with map view |
| Departments | Ministry teams with rosters, rotas, and recruitment pipelines |
| Attendance | Service check-ins, self check-in by QR code, and fellowship meeting rolls |
| Follow-ups & Concerns | Pastoral contact records, the first-timer and no-group queues, and the welfare and safeguarding inboxes |
| Outreach | Evangelism programs, soul capture, and follow-up tracking |
| New Believers | Discipleship pipeline from altar call to department integration |
| Forms | First-time visitor, baptism, testimony, baby naming, and more |
| Souls Dashboard | Analytics on the souls pipeline and conversion funnel |
| Reports & Analytics | Growth, attendance, engagement, and outreach metrics |
| Profile & Settings | Personal information, security, display preferences |
