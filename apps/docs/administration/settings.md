# Settings

App Settings let you manage your account security, display preferences, notifications, and privacy. Every user has access to their own settings.

Access settings from your **Profile** page. Click **Settings** in the top-right corner, or use the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Account & Security

### Change password

Update your login password. You'll need to provide your current password before setting a new one.

### Change email

Move your account to a new email address. Both the old and new address receive a notification when the change is made.

### Recent sign-ins

View a history of sign-ins, password changes, email changes, linked sign-in providers, and grant updates on your account. Useful for spotting unauthorised access.

## Display

### Theme

Choose between **Light**, **Dark**, or match your device's system preference. The dark mode toggle is also available directly from the bottom of the sidebar.

### Language

Currently English only. Additional languages are coming in a future release.

## Notifications

### Email preferences

Manage which email categories reach your inbox and how often.

## Privacy & Data

### Legal & consent

Review the Terms & Conditions, the Privacy Notice, the Acceptable Use Policy, and the Confidentiality Undertaking, see which versions you have accepted, and manage your marketing preferences. When a policy is reissued you are asked to accept the new version before continuing.

### Export my data

Download a copy of your personal data, in exercise of the GDPR right to portability. The export is generated on request and covers your own record only.

### Delete my account

Withdraw consent and delete your account. You will be asked for your current password to confirm, because this cannot be undone from inside the app.

## Log Out

The **Log Out** button appears at the bottom of the Settings page. Clicking it ends your session immediately and returns you to the login screen.
