# Di Mayn Tin Dɛn

If yu ɔndastand dɛn siks tin ya, ɔl di ɔda dɔkyumɛnteshɔn go izi fɔ fɔlo.

## 1. Branch dɛn

Wan **branch** na wan chɔch ples. Ɛvri infɔmeshɔn insay Kairos (mɛmba dɛn, dipatmɛnt dɛn, fɛloship dɛn, awtrich program dɛn, atɛndans rɛkɔd dɛn) bilɔng to wan branch. Branch na di mayn we aw di tin dɛn arenj.

Ɛvri branch gɛt:
- Wan nem, wan tayp (Mayn, Satelayt, Kampɔs, ɛn ɔda dɛn), wan siti, ɛn kɔntakt infɔmeshɔn
- Wan om rijɔn
- Wan Mayn Pasto ɛn Minista dɛn if dɛn de
- In yon mɛmba dɛn, fɛloship dɛn, ɛn dipatmɛnt dɛn

Branch dɛn nɔ kin si dɛn kɔmpin in data pas if na Admin yu de.

## 2. Pipul dɛn, ɛn wetin «Mɛmba» min

Ɔlman insay Kairos de pan wan lis. Wan pɔsin gɛt wan om branch, ɛn i kin bilɔng to wan fɛloship ɛn te tu dipatmɛnt insay dat branch.

Di wɔd *Mɛmba* na im nid mɔ ɛksplaneshɔn. Insay dis chɔch, Mɛmba na pɔsin we **dɔn finish di fo-wik mɛmbaship klas**. If yu de kam fɔ ia dɛn, if yu de sav na dipatmɛnt, ɔ if yu de lid wan fɛloship sef, dat nɔ mek yu Mɛmba insay di fɔmal we. Si [Mɛmbaship Klas dɛn](/kri/platform/membership).

So ɛvri pɔsin kɛr tu difrɛn lebul, ɛn i gud fɔ nɔ miks dɛn:

**Wetin kayn pɔsin i de:**
- **Mɛmba**: i dɔn finish di mɛmbaship klas
- **Ritɔna**: i de kam bɔt i nɔ du di klas yet. Na dɛn plɛnti pas insay di chɔch.
- **Vizitɔ**: i de kam sɔm tɛm, ɛn na di Fɔs-Tɛm Vizitɔ fɔm kin mek am
- **Pikin**: dɛn de fɔlo dɛn sɛpret, wit dɛn yon prɔtɛkshɔn

**If i kin sayn in:**
- **De wet**: i rejista bɔt dɛn nɔ aprov am yet
- **Dɛn gri / Aktiv**: i gɛt ful akses
- **Nɔ aktiv**: dɛn pul am sɔf sɔf, bɔt di istri de de

Vizitɔ ɛn pikin dɛn de stil sho pan di dairɛktri ɛn insay di atɛndans nɔmba dɛn, bikɔs dɛn na pat pan di chɔch ɛn wi fɔ kawnt dɛn. Wi jɔs lebul dɛn so dat di difrɛns klia.

## 3. Rol ɛn Grant dɛn

Kairos de yuz tu sistem rol (`admin` ɛn `member`) bɔt di rial pamishɔn dɛn kɔmɔt frɔm **fɔnkshɔnal grant dɛn**. Wan grant na wan rol bɔndul we dɛn nem, we dɛn tay to wan spɛshal ples, ɛn fo kayn ples de: wan **branch**, wan **fɛloship**, wan **dipatmɛnt**, ɔ di ol **chɔch**.

Fɔ ɛgzampul, wan mɛmba we gɛt `FellowshipLeader` grant fɔ «Grace K-Group» kin ron dat fɛloship bɔt natin pas dat. Di sem mɛmba wit `DepartmentLeader` grant fɔ «Kwaya na Kharis London» kin ron dat dipatmɛnt sɛpret.

Di chɔch ples de fɔ di tu tin dɛn we nɔto fɔ wan branch nɔmɔ: di mɛmbaship klas program, we in koɔt dɛn de pul pipul frɔm ɛvri branch, ɛn di Sefgadin Ed, bikɔs sɔm tɛm wan wɔri we kam ɔp na wan branch fɔ kɔmɔt na dat branch kliin kliin. Wan chɔch grant de rich ɛvri branch. Di ɔda we rawnd nɔ de wok: if yu gɛt branch grant na ɛvri branch sef, dat nɔ sem lɛk chɔch grant.

Si [Rol ɛn Pamishɔn dɛn](/kri/getting-started/roles-permissions) fɔ di ful tin.

## 4. Di Sol Paiplain

Kairos de fɔlo di jɔni fɔ pipul dɛn we wi rich tru ɛvanjelizm. Wan **sol** na pɔsin we dɛn kach durin wan awtrich ivɛnt. I de muv tru wan paiplain:

**Nyu → Wi de fɔlo ɔp → I want → I dɔn kɔnvat**

Wan tɛm i kɔnvat, dɛn kin rejista am na di **Nyu Biliva** disaypulship trak, we gɛt fo sɛshɔn bifo i jɔyn wan dipatmɛnt. No se fɔ finish Nyu Biliva nɔto di sem tin lɛk fɔ finish di mɛmbaship klas: di fɔs wan na disaypulship fɔ pɔsin we jɔs kɔnvat, di sɛkɔn wan na wetin mek pɔsin Mɛmba fɔ tru.

## 5. Fɔlo ɔp ɛn Wɔri dɛn

Wan **fɔlo ɔp** na rɛkɔd fɔ pastɔral kɔntakt: udat tɔk to am, aw, wetin apin, ɛn wetin fɔ apin nɛks. Ɛvri fɔlo ɔp de nem di ples usay i apin: wan fɛloship, wan dipatmɛnt, ɔ jɔs di branch. Di branch opshɔn impɔtant: i min se pɔsin we nɔ bilɔng to ɛni grup at ɔl kin stil gɛt fɔlo ɔp.

Ɛni fɔlo ɔp kin mak as **wɛlfɛa** wɔri ɔ **sefgadin** wɔri, wan wan. Dɛn tu mak de go na tu difrɛn inbɔks, we difrɛn pipul de rid pɔposli. Si [Fɔlo ɔp dɛn](/kri/platform/follow-ups) ɛn [Wɔri dɛn](/kri/platform/concerns).

## 6. Sɔf Dilit

Natin nɔ de dilit fɔ gud insay Kairos. If yu dɔn tɔn ɔf wan mɛmba, fɛloship, ɔ dipatmɛnt, ɔl di istri de de. Atɛndans rɛkɔd dɛn, fɔlo ɔp not dɛn, ɛn rol asaynmɛnt dɛn nɔ de lɔs. Dis impɔtant fɔ ripɔt ɛn fɔ akawntabiliti.
