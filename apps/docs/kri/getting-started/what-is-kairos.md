# Wetin na Kairos?

Kairos na wan chɔch adminstreshɔn sistem we dɛn bil fɔ Kharis Church. I tek di ples fɔ spredshit dɛn, WhatsApp grup dɛn, ɛn tul dɛn we nɔ de wok togɛda, ɛn i gi wan platfɔm we ɛvri rol insay di chɔch kin yuz, frɔm di mɛmba we jɔs kam fɔs tɛm sote go na di chɔch adminstretɔ we de ron plɛnti branch.

## Di prɔblɛm we i de sɔlv

If yu de ron wan chɔch we gɛt plɛnti branch, data prɔblɛm dɛn kin kam: mɛmba rɛkɔd dɛn skata na difrɛn branch, nɔbɔdi nɔ no udat de fɔlo ɔp di nyu sol dɛn, i at fɔ si wetin dipatmɛnt nid atɛnshɔn, ɛn nɔ rɛkɔd nɔ de fɔ di aproval dɛn. Kairos de bring ɔl dat kam na wan say.

> «Bɔt mek ɔltin du di rayt we ɛn insay ɔda.» (1 Kɔrintians 14:40)

Na dat na di tinkin bihayn Kairos.

## Wetin mek i difrɛn

**I no yu rol frɔm di stat.** Ɛvri pej, ɛvri akshɔn, ɛvri infɔmeshɔn de filta to wetin na yu rispɔnsibiliti fɔ tru. Wan fɛloship lida de si in fɛloship. Wan pasto de si in branch. Wan admin de si ɔltin.

**Wan sayn in, ɛn ɔltin de na in ples.** Nɔ «pik yu rol» step nɔ de afta yu sayn in. Yu pamishɔn dɛn de insay yu tokin wan tɛm yu sayn in. Di UI de sho ɛgzakli wetin yu grant dɛn alaw: nɔ mɔ, nɔ lɛs.

Sayn in wit imel ɛn paswɔd, ɔ wit **Google**, **Microsoft**, ɔ **Apple**. Ɔl fo de kam na di sem akawnt, so pɔsin we bin rejista wit paswɔd kin sayn in wit Google leta ɛn i stil na di sem pɔsin, wit di sem istri.

**Branch ayzoleshɔn de insay di sistem.** Data nɔ de lik frɔm wan branch go na ɔda bay mistek. Wan pasto na Lɔndɔn nɔ kin si mɛmba rɛkɔd dɛn frɔm Akra. Admin dɛn gɛt klia akses fɔ go akrɔs branch.

## Di chɔch we dɛn bil am fɔ

Dɛn bil Kairos rawnd Kharis Church in strɔkchɔ:

- Plɛnti branch na difrɛn kɔntri (UK, Gana, Salon)
- Branch dɛn we dɛn grup insay rijɔn bay ples
- Ɛvri branch gɛt in yon Mayn Pasto, Minista dɛn if dɛn de, dipatmɛnt dɛn, ɛn fɛloship dɛn
- Mɛmba dɛn kin go na ɛni branch bɔt dɛn gɛt wan om branch
- Wan smɔl admin tim de ron di ol nɛtwɔk
