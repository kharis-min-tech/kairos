# Yu Fɔs Chɔch

<SectionClip slug="getting-started/your-first-church" />

Dis gayd de kɛr Admin tru di sɛt ɔp fɔ Kairos fɔ nyu chɔch frɔm skrach. If yu chɔch dɔn sɛt ɔp, jɔmp go na [Platfɔm](/kri/platform/dashboard).

## Stɛp 1: Mek Rijɔn

Rijɔn na jiɔgrafik grup fɔ yu branch dɛn. Stat ya.

Go na **Rijɔn dɛn** insay di saydba ɛn klik **+ Nyu Rijɔn**. Put di rijɔn nem ɛn di kɔntri.

Ɛgzampul frɔm Kharis Church: `Greater Accra (Ghana)`, `United Kingdom`, `Western Area (Sierra Leone)`.

## Stɛp 2: Mek yu fɔs Branch

Go na **Branch dɛn** ɛn klik **+ Nyu Branch**. Ful:

- Branch nem (lɛk "Kharis London Central")
- Branch tayp (Mayn, Satɛlayt, Kampɔs, Sɛl, ɔ Ɔnlayn)
- Rijɔn
- Siti, adrɛs, fon, ɛn imel

## Stɛp 3: Put Pasto

We yu branch dɔn de, opin am frɔm **Mi Branch** ɛn yuz di **Lidaship Naw** sɛkshɔn fɔ put Mayn Pasto. Na mɛmba dɛn we de insay di sistem nɔmɔ yu kin put.

## Stɛp 4: Ad Mɛmba dɛn

Yu kin ad mɛmba wan bay wan tru **Mɛmba dɛn → + Ad Mɛmba**, ɔ yu kin impɔt plɛnti wan tɛm tru **Impɔt CSV**. Mɛmba fɔ gɛt branch we yu de mek dɛn.

Afta yu ad mɛmba, ɛnibɔdi we rɛjista insɛf go sho insay di **Aproval Kyu** ɔnda di Mɛmba pej ɛda. Aprov dɛn fɔ gi dɛn akses.

## Stɛp 5: Sɛt ɔp Fɛloship dɛn

Go na **Fɛloship dɛn → + Nyu Fɛloship** ɛn mek yu fɔs smɔl grup. Put lida, pik di fɛloship tayp (K-Groups, Kharis Express, New Breeds, ɛn so on), ɛn ad mitin plan.

## Stɛp 7: Gi pamishɔn

Nɔbɔdi nɔ gɛt ɔtoriti te yu gi am. Fɛn awt udat de ron ɛvri branch ɛn gi am **Branch Sistem Admin** grant frɔm in mɛmba prɔfayl; dat pɔsin kin gi di rɛst insay in yon branch. Nem ɛvri fɛloship in lida pan di fɛloship ɛn ɛvri dipatmɛnt in lida pan di dipatmɛnt, ɛn dat de mek dɛn grant fɔ yu.

Tu grant na chɔch-wayd ɛn i gud fɔ disayd dɛn kwik: **Mɛmbaship Admin**, we de ron di mɛmbaship klas program, ɛn **Sefgadin Ɛd**. Put ɛvri branch in **Mayn Pasto** bak insay **Mi Branch → Lidaship Naw**, bikɔs na dat apɔyntmɛnt de gi am say pan in branch in sefgadin kyu.

Si [Yuza Pamishɔn dɛn](/kri/administration/permissions).

## Stɛp 8: Plan yu fɔs mɛmbaship klas

Fɔ de pan di rol nɔto di sem tin lɛk fɔ bi Mɛmba. Di fɔmal stɛp na di fɔ-wik klas, so sɛt ɔp kɔhɔt kwik: go na **Mɛmbaship**, mek kɔhɔt, plan in fɔ sɛshɔn, ɛn lɛ pipul sho se dɛn want. Yu go admit dɛn frɔm di wetlis we yu redi.

Si [Mɛmbaship Klas](/kri/platform/membership).

## Stɛp 9: Yu redi

Wit branch, mɛmba, fɛloship, dipatmɛnt, pamishɔn, ɛn wan klas we yu plan, yu tim kin stat fɔ rɛkɔd atɛndans, wok di fɔlo ɔp kyu, fɔlo di awtrich, ɛn ron di disaypulship paplayn.
