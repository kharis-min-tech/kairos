# Mɛmba dɛn

<SectionClip slug="platform/members" />

Di Mɛmba pej na di chɔch in dairɛktri. I de sho di pipul dɛn insay yu branch (ɔ akrɔs ɔl di branch dɛn if yu na Admin) wit dɛn stetɔs, kɔntakt infɔmeshɔn, ɛn wetin fɛloship dɛn de.

::: tip Udat de insay di dairɛktri
Di dairɛktri kɔva **kɔnfam Mɛmba dɛn** (pipul we dɔn finish di [mɛmbaship klas](/kri/platform/membership)) ɛn **Ritɔna dɛn** (pipul we de kam bɔt dɛn nɔ du di klas yet). Vizitɔ ɛn pikin rɛkɔd dɛn, we di Fɔs-Tɛm Vizitɔ fɔm ɔ sefgadin prɔses kin mek, de bihayn dɛn yon ples, so dat di rɔl nɔ ful ɔp wit layn dɛn we nɔ gɛt sayn in. Yuz di filta dɛn fɔ pik wan kayn.
:::

## Di mɛmba dairɛktri

Ɛvri mɛmba kad de sho:
- Nem ɛn di avata inishal dɛn
- Om branch
- Imel ɛn fon
- Aproval stetɔs bajj (Dɛn gri, De wet)
- **Diaktivet** bɔtin, fɔ ɛnibɔdi we gɛt branch grant

## Fɔ luk ɛn filta

Yuz di sach ba fɔ fɛn mɛmba wit nem ɔ imel. Filta wit di drɔpdawn dɛn:

- **Ɔl Fɛloship**: pik mɛmba dɛn fɔ wan spɛshal fɛloship
- **Branch**: filta bay branch (Admin nɔmɔ)
- **Ɔl Stetɔs**: filta bay Dɛn gri, De wet, ɔ Nɔ aktiv

## Fɔ ad wan mɛmba

Klik **+ Ad Mɛmba** fɔ mek wan mɛmba wit yu an. Di fil dɛn we yu fɔ ful:

| Fil | Not dɛn |
|-------|-------|
| Fɔs nem, Las nem | Yu fɔ ful am |
| Imel | I fɔ difrɛn frɔm ɔl ɔda |
| Fon | I gud fɔ put am |
| Om branch | Yu fɔ ful am; i de sɛt dɛn branch |
| Jɛnda | Man ɔ Uman |
| Di de we dɛn bɔn am | Yu nɔ nid fɔ put am |
| Adrɛs | Yu nɔ nid fɔ put am |
| Imajɛnsi kɔntakt | Nem, fon, ɛn aw una tay |

Mɛmba dɛn we pɔsin wit branch grant ad wit an, dɛn aprov dɛn wan tɛm.

## Rejista yusɛf

Mɛmba dɛn kin rejista dɛnsɛf frɔm di sayn in pej. Dɛn de stat wit **De wet** stetɔs ɛn dɛn fɔ aprov dɛn bifo dɛn gɛt ful akses. Mɛmba we de wet kin si in yon prɔfayl nɔmɔ.

## Aproval Lis

Di **Aproval Lis** bɔtin na di Mɛmba pej ed de sho ɔl di rejistreshɔn dɛn we de wet. Klik am fɔ chɛk ɛn aprov ɔ se nɔ to ɛvri wan. Si [Aproval dɛn](/kri/administration/approvals) fɔ mɔ.

## Fɔ impɔt mɛmba wit CSV

Klik **Impɔt CSV** fɔ put plɛnti mɛmba wan tɛm. Di CSV fɔ gɛt kɔlɔm fɔ fɔs nem, las nem, imel, fon, jɛnda, di de we dɛn bɔn am, ɛn om branch. Di sistem de chɛk ɛvri layn bifo i impɔt ɛn i de sho wan privyu wit ɛni ɛra we i mak.

## Fɔ ɛkspɔt mɛmba

Klik **Ɛkspɔt CSV** fɔ dawnlod wetin yu de si naw as wan CSV fayl. I gud fɔ ripɔt ɔ fɔ muv data.

## Sefgadin rivyu

Di **Sefgadin rivyu** bɔtin de opin wan filta vyu fɔ rɛkɔd dɛn we gɛt sefgadin not ɔ mak.

Dis sɛpret frɔm di [Wɔri](/kri/platform/concerns) inbɔks. Sefgadin rivyu na bɔt di rɛkɔd dɛn dɛnsɛf, mɔstli vizitɔ ɛn pikin rɛkɔd dɛn we de wet fɔ disizhɔn; di Wɔri inbɔks na bɔt fɔlo ɔp dɛn we sɔmbɔdi mak. Difrɛn pipul de rid dɛn: Wɔri nid sefgadin sayt, we branch grant nɔ de gi.

## Fɔ diaktivet wan mɛmba

Fɔ diaktivet wan mɛmba na sɔf dilit. Dɛn prɔfayl, atɛndans istri, fɔlo ɔp not dɛn, ɛn grup mɛmbaship dɛn, ɔl de de. Dɛn lɔs akses to di sistem bɔt dɛn data de de fɔ ripɔt.

Fɔ diaktivet nid branch grant, ɔ platfɔm admin.
