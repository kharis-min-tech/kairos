## Di sɛkshɔn dɛn

### Pɔsinal Infɔmeshɔn

| Fil | Not dɛn |
|-------|-------|
| Fɔs Nem / Las Nem | Yu ful nem as dɛn rejista am |
| Midul Nem | Yu nɔ nid fɔ put am |
| Imel | Di imel we yu de yuz fɔ sayn in |
| Fon | Kɔntakt nɔmba |
| Jɛnda | Man ɔ Uman |
| Di de we dɛn bɔn yu | Dɛn de yuz am fɔ bɔtde |

### Adrɛs

Strit adrɛs, siti, ɛn postal kod. Dɛn de yuz dis fɔ sefgadin ɛn fɔ kɔntakt mɛmba.

### Usay a bilɔng

I de sho yu **om branch**, di branch usay dɛn rejista yu.

### Mi kɔmyuniti

I de lis di fɛloship dɛn usay yu na mɛmba naw, wit di fɛloship tayp bajj.

### Mi lidaship

I de sho ɛni rol we yu gɛt, fɔ ɛgzampul «Chɔch adminstretɔ», «Fɛloship Lida, Kharis Express London», ɔ «Dipatmɛnt Lida, Kwaya».

### Imajɛnsi Kɔntakt

Nem, aw una tay, ɛn fon nɔmba fɔ yu imajɛnsi kɔntakt. Na yu, yu pasto, ɛn admin dɛn nɔmɔ kin si am.

## Fɔ chenj yu prɔfayl

Klik **Chenj Prɔfayl** na di tɔp rayt kɔna fɔ ɔpdet yu infɔmeshɔn. Mɛmba kin chenj dɛn yon prɔfayl ɛni tɛm. Pasto ɛn Admin kin chenj ɛni mɛmba in prɔfayl frɔm di Mɛmba pej.

## Sɛtin dɛn

Klik **Sɛtin** na di tɔp rayt fɔ go na yu [App Sɛtin dɛn](/kri/administration/settings).

::: tip Wetin mɛmba kin si
Ɛvri yuza kin si in yon ful prɔfayl. Mɛmba nɔ kin si ɔda mɛmba in prɔfayl. Pasto kin si ɔl di prɔfayl dɛn insay in branch. Admin kin si ɔl di prɔfayl dɛn akrɔs ɔl di branch dɛn.
:::
