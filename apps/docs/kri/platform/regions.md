## Wetin rijɔn de du

Rijɔn de mek yu grup branch dɛn bay ples: fɔ ɛgzampul, ɔl di UK branch dɛn na wan rijɔn ɛn ɔl di Gana branch dɛn na wan ɔda. Dis de ɛp fɔ filta we yu gɛt branch na plɛnti kɔntri.

Kairos de yuz tri rijɔn fɔ Kharis Church:
- **Greater Accra**: Gana
- **United Kingdom**: UK
- **Western Area**: Salon

## Fɔ ad wan rijɔn

Klik **+ Nyu Rijɔn** (Admin nɔmɔ) ɛn gi:

| Fil | Yu fɔ ful am |
|-------|----------|
| Rijɔn nem | Yɛs |
| Kɔntri | Yɛs |

Rijɔn nem fɔ difrɛn frɔm ɔl di ɔda dɛn insay di sistem.

## Fɔ chenj wan rijɔn

Klik ɛni rijɔn kad fɔ chenj in nem ɔ in kɔntri.

## Fɔ dilit wan rijɔn

Yu kin dilit wan rijɔn nɔmɔ if i nɔ gɛt branch tay pan am. If branch dɛn de insay dat rijɔn, tɔn dɛn ɔf ɔ muv dɛn fɔs.

## Udat kin ron rijɔn

Na **Admin** dɛn nɔmɔ kin mek, chenj, ɔ dilit rijɔn. Pasto ɛn mɛmba nɔ kin go na di Rijɔn pej.
