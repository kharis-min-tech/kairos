# Atɛndans

<SectionClip slug="platform/attendance" />

Di Atɛndans pej na fɔ sɛvis atɛndans: Sɔnde sɛvis, midwik sɛvis, ɛn spɛshal sɛvis. Fɛloship mitin atɛndans dɛn de manej am sɛpret, frɔm insay ɛvri fɛloship.

## Di sɛvis lis

Ɛvri layn pan di lis de sho:
- Sɛvis tayp bajj (fɔ ɛgzampul **Sɔnde**)
- Di sɛvis nem ɛn tim
- Di de, di branch, ɛn di prich taytul
- **Chɛk In →** link fɔ opin di atɛndans rɛjista fɔ dat sɛvis

## Fɔ filta

Filta di sɛvis lis bay:
- **Tayp**: Ɔl tayp, Sɔnde Sɛvis, Midwik Sɛvis, Spɛshal Sɛvis
- **Frɔm / To**: De rɛnj

## Fɔ rɛkɔd wan sɛvis

Klik **+ Rɛkɔd wan sɛvis** (Pasto ɛn Admin nɔmɔ):

| Fil | Not dɛn |
|-------|-------|
| Branch | Yu fɔ ful am |
| Sɛvis tayp | Sɔnde, Midwik, Spɛshal, Prea Mitin, Ɔda |
| De | Yu fɔ ful am |
| Sɛvis taytul / tim | Yu nɔ nid fɔ put am |
| Pricha | Yu nɔ nid fɔ put am; link am to wan mɛmba |
| Prich tɔpik | Yu nɔ nid fɔ put am |
| Omɔch pipul yu ɛkspɛkt | Yu nɔ nid fɔ put am |

## Fɔ chɛk in mɛmba dɛn

Afta yu mek di sɛvis, opin am wit **Chɛk In →**. Mak ɛvri mɛmba as:
- **De ya**
- **Nɔ de ya**
- **Ɔnlayn**

Yu kin mak mɛmba as **Fɔs-Tɛm Vizita** bak we yu de chɛk in.

Fɔ rɛkɔd atɛndans fɔ wan sɛvis, yu fɔ gɛt branch grant, ɔ yu fɔ na platfɔm admin.

## Ripɔt dɛn

Fɔ di ful ripɔt skrin — di pa-mɛmba it map, di atɛndans frikwɛnsi band dɛn, ɛn di fɔs-tɛm vas ritɔnin split — opin **Ripɔt dɛn** frɔm di Atɛndans pej. Si [Atɛndans Ripɔt dɛn](/kri/analytics/attendance-reports#di-ful-ripɔt-skrin).

## Sɛf chɛk in

Mɛmba dɛn kin chɛk dɛnsɛf in, so di wɛlkɔm dɛsk nɔto di onli rod fɔ go pan di rɛjista. **Chɛk in** de insay ɛvribɔdi saydba.

Tu we de:

- **Tap fɔ chɛk in.** Wan tap de rɛkɔd di mɛmba na wan sɛvis we in chɛk-in windo opin naw.
- **Skan di dɛsk QR kod.** Admin de opin di sɛvis ɛn sho **Sɛf chɛk-in QR** pan skrin na di dɛsk. Di kod de chenj ɛvri 30 sɛkɔn, ɛn di mɛmba de skan am frɔm **Chɛk in → Skan QR na di dɛsk**. Di kod we bin de bifo de wok smɔl tɛm afta di chenj, so dat pɔsin we de skan nɔ go gɛt rifyuz.

Na di kod we de chenj de stɔp mɛmba fɔ chɛk in frɔm os: skrinshɔt we yu tek las Sɔnde nɔ gud igen.

### Branch sɛtin dɛn

Ɛvri branch de kɔntrol in yon sɛf chɛk in frɔm **Mi Branch → Atɛndans sɛtin dɛn**:

| Sɛtin | Wetin i de du |
|---------|--------------|
| Sɛf chɛk in opin | Di mayn swich. If i ɔf, nɔn pan di tu we nɔ de wok fɔ dis branch. |
| Opin bifo di stat | Omɔch minit bifo di sɛvis stat di windo de opin |
| Lɔk afta di stat | Omɔch minit afta di stat di windo de lɔk |
| Let afta | Omɔch minit afta di stat dɛn de rɛkɔd chɛk-in as **Let** nɔto **De ya** |

If yu chɛk in tu tɛm, dat nɔ de mek tu rɛkɔd, ɛn na di sava de put di Let layn, so i sem wan ɛni we yu yuz.

## Mi Atɛndans

### Wetin i de sho

- **Atɛndans Ret**: di pasɛnt fɔ di las 12 wik wit wan wɔd fɔ ɛnkɔrej yu
- **Atɛnd vas Ɔl**: fɔ ɛgzampul "2 we yu atɛnd pan 3 sɛvis"
- **Las tɛm dɛn si yu**: di de ɛn di sɛvis fɔ yu las atɛndans

### Pɔnkchualiti brekdawn

Wan ba chat we de sho aw yu sɛvis dɛn split bitwin De ya, Let, Ɔnlayn, ɛn Mis.

### Strik naw

Omɔch sɛvis bak to bak yu dɔn atɛnd.

### Sɛvis istri

Lis fɔ ɛvri sɛvis insay di 12-wik windo, di oldes wan fɔs, wit yu atɛndans stetɔs fɔ ɛvri wan (Mis, De ya, Ɔnlayn, Let).
