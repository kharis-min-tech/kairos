## Di paplayn wan tɛm

Tri nɔmba de pan tɔp:

| Nɔmba | Wetin i min |
|------|---------|
| Aktiv | Mɛmba dɛn we de insay di paplayn naw |
| Nɔ muv fɔ 7+ de | Ɛnrolmɛnt dɛn we tap; dɛn nid atɛnshɔn |
| Jɔyn Dipatmɛnt | Dɛn dɔn finish di ful paplayn |

## Di Kanban bɔd

Di paplayn de sho as Kanban bɔd wit kɔlɔm fɔ ɛvri stej:

| Stej | Diskripshɔn |
|-------|-------------|
| Sɛshɔn 1 | Fawndeshɔn fɔ Fet |
| Sɛshɔn 2 | Udat na Kristian |
| Sɛshɔn 3 | Fɔ Wok Awt Yu Salveshɔn |
| Sɛshɔn 4 | Wetin Mek Fɛloship Impɔtant |
| Dɔn | Ɔl di sɛshɔn dɛn dɔn |
| Jɔyn Dipatmɛnt | I dɔn ɛnta chɔch layf fayn fayn |

Ɛvri kad de sho:
- Di mɛmba nem ɛn di de we i ɛnrol
- Di ticha we dɛn gi am (wit in inishal)
- Di mɛnto we dɛn gi am (wit in inishal)

## Fɔ ɛnrol nyu biliva

Klik **+ Ɛnrol Mɛmba** fɔ stat pɔsin pan di paplayn. We dɛn mak sol as kɔnvat insay di [Sol Paplayn](/kri/platform/souls-pipeline), ɛnrolmɛnt rɛkɔd de mek insɛf.

## Fɔ sɔt ɛn sach

- Sach bay mɛmba ɔ ticha nem wit di sach ba
- Sɔt bay **De we dɛn Ad am**, **Nem**, ɔ **Las Aktiviti**
- Filta bay stej wit di tab dɛn

## Sɛshɔn dɛn

Klik di **Sɛshɔn** bɔtin fɔ manej di klas sɛshɔn dɛn we dɛn plan: de, tɔpik, ples, ɛn us ticha de lid ɛvri sɛshɔn.

## Udat kin go pan Nyu Biliva

| Ɛnrol mɛmba | Nɔ | Yɛs (Nyu Biliva dipatmɛnt lida) | Yɛs | Yɛs |
