# Fɔlo ɔp dɛn

<SectionClip slug="platform/follow-ups" />

Fɔlo ɔp na di rɛkɔd fɔ pastɔral kɔntakt: udat rich awt, aw, wetin apin, ɛn wetin fɔ apin nɛks. Di **Fɔlo ɔp** pej insay di saydba na di branch-lɛvul wok lis fɔ pipul we nɔbɔdi nɔ rich yet.

## Usay fɔlo ɔp de liv

Ɛvri fɔlo ɔp tay to pɔsin, ɛn to wan **kɔntɛks** bak: di ples we di kɔntakt apin. Tri de:

| Kɔntɛks | We dɛn de yuz am |
|---------|-----------|
| Fɛloship | Fɛloship lida de fɔlo ɔp wan pan in fɛloship mɛmba dɛn. Dɛn de rɛkɔd am frɔm di fɛloship. |
| Dipatmɛnt | Dipatmɛnt lida ɔ dɛpyuti de fɔlo ɔp wan pan in dipatmɛnt mɛmba dɛn. Dɛn de rɛkɔd am frɔm di dipatmɛnt. |
| Branch | Branch-lɛvul pastɔral kɔntakt, nɔ grup nɔ de insay. Dɛn de rɛkɔd am frɔm di Fɔlo ɔp pej ɔ di mɛmba in prɔfayl. |

Di branch kɔntɛks na di wan we chenj naw-naw, ɛn i chenj sɔntin big: **pɔsin we nɔ de na fɛloship ɔ dipatmɛnt kin gɛt fɔlo ɔp naw.** Bifo, fɔlo ɔp bin fɔ tay to grup, so di pipul dɛn we nid kɔntakt pas ɔl — di wan dɛn we nɔbɔdi nɔ bin pul kam yet — na dɛn di sistem nɔ bin ebul fɔ rɛkɔd kɔntakt fɔ.

Na di pɔsin we de rɛkɔd di fɔlo ɔp de tɔk di kɔntɛks. Kairos nɔ de ges am frɔm di pɔsin in grup dɛn, bikɔs di ples fɔ wan tɔk na fakt bɔt di tɔk, nɔto bɔt di pɔsin.

## Di tri kyu dɛn

Opin **Fɔlo ɔp** ɛn yu go gɛt tri tab. Na sɛpret kyu, nɔto wan lis, bikɔs na difrɛn kayn wok pan difrɛn klɔk.

### Fɔs-tɛm pipul

Ɛvribɔdi we kam tru **Fɔs-Tɛm Vizita** fɔm insay yu branch insay di **las 60 de** ɛn we **nɔ gɛt no fɔlo ɔp at ɔl**, di nyu wan fɔs.

Dis na wɛlkɔm pan klɔk fɔ awa ɔ de. Di kyu de sho udat invayt dɛn, ɛn usay dɛn kach am, so di pɔsin we de rich awt gɛt ples fɔ stat.

Fɔs-tɛm pɔsin de lɛf dis kyu di tɛm we ɛnibɔdi rɛkɔd ɛni fɔlo ɔp fɔ am, insay ɛni kɔntɛks. Di kyu na "ɛnibɔdi dɔn mek kɔntakt", nɔto "ɛnibɔdi dɔn mek di rayt kayn kɔntakt".

### Nɔ grup

Aktiv pipul insay yu branch we nɔ de na **fɛloship ɛn nɔ de na dipatmɛnt**, di nyu rɛkɔd fɔs.

Dis na slo, stɛdi wok: pɔsin de kam bɔt i nɔ tay to ɛnitin. Pikin dɛn nɔ de insay, bikɔs kɔntakt bɔt pikin de pas tru dɛn gadian.

Difrɛn frɔm di fɔs-tɛm kyu, dis wan nɔ de klia we yu rɛkɔd fɔlo ɔp. Pɔsin de lɛf am we i tru-tru jɔyn grup, we na di tin di kyu de ya fɔ mek apin.

### Du naw

Fɔlo ɔp dɛn we sɔmbɔdi bin put **nɛks fɔlo ɔp de** we dɔn rich ɔ dɔn pas, insay yu branch, fɔ pipul we stil aktiv.

Dis na di kyu we de ol prɔmis. We yu rɛkɔd fɔlo ɔp ɛn yu put nɛks de, na ya i de kam bak to yu.

## Fɔ rɛkɔd fɔlo ɔp

Fɔlo ɔp na **kɔntakt** ɔ **vizit**, ɛn di fɔm de chenj akɔdin.

**Ɔltu de rɛkɔd:**
- Di chanɛl dɛn we yu yuz, te siks, so dat "a kɔl, dɔn a tɛks, dɔn a WhatsApp" na wan rɛkɔd nɔto tri
- Omɔch minit
- Not dɛn
- Nɛks fɔlo ɔp de if yu want, we de put di pɔsin insay di Du naw kyu we di de rich

**Kɔntakt de rɛkɔd bak:**
- If yu tru-tru rich am
- In intrɛs lɛvul: i want, i nɔ want, ɔ i nɔ disayd yet

**Vizit de rɛkɔd bak:**
- Fes to fes ɔ ɔnlayn
- If dɛn bin tɛl am bifo
- Di tɛm yu rich ɛn di tɛm yu lɛf
- Di awtkam: i bin de, i nɔ bin de, ɔ dɛn chenj di de
- Udat go wit yu

## Di tu kɔnsɛn flag dɛn

Ɛni fɔlo ɔp kin gɛt flag, sɛpret-sɛpret, as **wɛlfɛa kɔnsɛn** ɔ **sefgadin kɔnsɛn**. Ɔltu, wan, ɔ nɔn.

Na sɛpret tin dɛn pɔposli, nɔto tu lɛvul fɔ di sem tin:

- **Wɛlfɛa** na pastɔral kia. Pɔsin de sɔfa, pɔsin lɔs sɔmbɔdi, pɔsin sik, pɔsin de na trɔbul.
- **Sefgadin** na prɔtɛkshɔn. Sɔntin de rɛz kɔnsɛn bɔt pikin prɔtɛkshɔn ɔ vɔlnarebul adɔlt.

If yu flag ɛni wan, di fɔlo ɔp de go na di sem tab insay di [Kɔnsɛn](/kri/platform/concerns) inbɔks, we difrɛn pipul de rid. Fɔlo ɔp kin gɛt ɔltu flag ɛn i go sho insay ɔltu inbɔks.

::: warning Flag am, nɔto jɔs fɔ rayt am
Sefgadin mata we yu rayt insay di not nɔmɔ fɔ fɔlo ɔp we nɔ gɛt flag nɔ de rich nɔbɔdi. Na di flag de sɛn am.
:::

## Udat kin wok di kyu dɛn

| Akshɔn | Mɛmba | Lida | Pasto | Admin |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Opin di Fɔlo ɔp pej | Nɔ | Nɔ | Yɛs | Yɛs |
| Fɔs-tɛm, nɔ-grup, ɛn du-naw kyu | Nɔ | Nɔ | In yon branch | Ɛni branch |
| Rɛkɔd branch-kɔntɛks fɔlo ɔp | Nɔ | Nɔ | In yon branch | Yɛs |
| Rɛkɔd fɛloship ɔ dipatmɛnt fɔlo ɔp | Nɔ | In yon grup | Yɛs | Yɛs |
| Rid di wɛlfɛa inbɔks | Nɔ | Nɔ | Branch Sistem Admin nɔmɔ | Yɛs |
| Rid di sefgadin inbɔks | Nɔ | Nɔ | Mayn Pasto ɔ Sefgadin Lida nɔmɔ | Yɛs |

Di tri kyu na branch-lɛvul wok, so dɛn nid branch grant. Platfɔm admin kin luk ɛni branch in kyu; ɔlman ɛls de si in yon branch nɔmɔ.
