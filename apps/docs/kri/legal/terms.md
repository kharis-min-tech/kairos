# Tɛms ɛn Kɔndishɔn

**I stat pan 23 Julay 2026 · Vɔshɔn 2026-07-v1**

::: tip Transleshɔn fɔ ɛp yu
<!-- i18n-allow-en -->
Dɛn mek dis transleshɔn fɔ ɛp yu nɔmɔ. If ɛnitin difrɛn, na di [Inglish vɔshɔn](/legal/terms) de ron.
:::

Dɛn tɛms ya de ron aw yu de yuz Kairos, di insay chɔch administreshɔn platfɔm fɔ Kharis Church ("wi", "di chɔch"). We yu mek akawnt ɔ yu yuz Kairos, yu gri fɔ fɔlo dɛn. If yu nɔ gri, nɔ yuz Kairos.

Na di lɔ fɔ England ɛn Wales de ron dɛn tɛms ya. Ɛni kes we kɔmɔt na dɛn go go to di kot dɛn na England ɛn Wales nɔmɔ.

## 1. Yu akawnt

Kairos akawnt na fɔ Kharis Church mɛmba wan bay wan. Yu fɔ gi tru info we yu sayn ɔp ɛn kip am ɔp to det pan yu prɔfayl.

Na yu gɛt di wok fɔ kip yu paswɔd sikrit. Nɔ shɛr yu akawnt wit ɛnibɔdi. If yu tink se sɔmbɔdi ɛls dɔn go pan yu akawnt, tɛl wi wan tɛm.

Yu fɔ gɛt 16 ia ɔ mɔ fɔ mek yu yon Kairos akawnt. If yu smɔl pas dat, parɛnt ɔ gadian fɔ ful di Kairos fɔm fɔ yu.

## 2. Aksɛptabul yuz

Yu gri fɔ yuz Kairos fɔ tru chɔch biznɛs nɔmɔ. Spɛshali, yu nɔ fɔ:

- Yuz Kairos fɔ sɛn spam, fɔ wɔri ɛni mɛmba, ɔ fɔ shɛr tin we nɔto lɔ, we de abyuz, we de ɛt, ɔ we de diskriminet
- Tray fɔ go pan akawnt, rɛkɔd, ɔ pat pan Kairos we dɛn nɔ gi yu akses to
- Pwɛl aw Kairos de wok, tray fɔ pas in sikyɔriti, ɔ yuz ɔtomatik skrapin tul igens am
- Kɔpi, sɛn araynd, ɔ mek bisnɛs wit info we yu kin si nɔmɔ bikɔs yu na Kharis Church mɛmba (fɔ ɛgzampul, ɔda mɛmba in kɔntakt ditel)
- Pritɛn se yu na sɔmbɔdi ɛls ɔ sɛn info bɔt sɔmbɔdi ɛls witawt dɛn pamishɔn

## 3. Lida ɛn admin dɛn

Fɛloship lida, dipatmɛnt lida, ɛn branch admin gɛt ɛkstra risponsibiliti insay Kairos. If yu ol wan pan dɛn rol, yu gri fɔ:

- Yuz mɛmba data fɔ di pastɔral, sefgadin, ɛn admin wok fɔ di chɔch nɔmɔ
- Fɔlo Kharis Church in sefgadin pɔlisi we yu de rɛkɔd, sɛn ɔp, ɔ shɛr sefgadin info
- Du ɛni trenin we di chɔch aks rol-olda fɔ du bifo yu yuz big pamishɔn

Di branch admin ɔ Kharis Church lidaship kin pul lidaship akses ɛni tɛm.

## 4. Yu data ɛn prayvesi

Wi [Prayvesi Notis](/kri/legal/privacy) de ɛksplen us pɔsnal data wi de tek, wetin mek, udat wi de shɛr am wit, omɔch tɛm wi de kip am, ɛn aw yu kin yuz yu rayt pan am. We yu yuz Kairos, yu de kɔnfam se yu dɔn rid di Prayvesi Notis.

## 5. Kɔntɛnt we yu sɛn

Kairos de lɛ yu sɛn info bɔt yusɛf ɛn, sɔm tɛm, fɔ famili mɛmba ɔ fɔs-tɛm vizita (wit dɛn pamishɔn). Yu kɔnfam se ɛni info we yu sɛn tru as fa as yu no, ɛn se yu gɛt ɛni kɔnsɛnt we yu nid frɔm ɔda pipul dɛn we dɛn info de insay.

Kharis Church kin yuz di info we yu sɛn fɔ di pastɔral, sefgadin, ɛn admin wok we de insay di Prayvesi Notis. Di kɔntɛnt we yu sɛn de stil na yu yon; yu de gi di chɔch laysɛns fɔ yuz am fɔ dɛn tin ya.

## 6. Aw i de de

Wi de wok fɔ mek Kairos de ɛn fɔ mek i strɔng, bɔt wi nɔ de prɔmis se i nɔ go stɔp ɔ se i nɔ go gɛt mistek. Sɔm tɛm wi fɔ pul Kairos ɔflayn fɔ mentenans, ɛn if ɔda pipul in sistem ɔf (lɛk wi ostin ɔ imel provayda), dat kin afɛkt am. Wi nɔ gɛt blem fɔ lɔs we kɔmɔt frɔm shɔt tɛm we i nɔ de.

## 7. Sɔspɛnshɔn ɛn klozin

Yu kin klos yu akawnt ɛni tɛm if yu kɔntakt lida ɔ yu imel [privacy@kharis.org](mailto:privacy@kharis.org).

Wi kin sɔspɛnd ɔ klos yu akawnt if yu brok dɛn tɛms bad bad, if yu lɛf di chɔch, ɔ if fɔ lɛ yu de de go mek sefgadin risk. If wi kin, wi go ɛksplen di rizin.

We yu akawnt klos, wi de kip ɛn dilit data lɛk aw di Prayvesi Notis se.

## 8. Intɛlɛkchual prɔpati

Di Kairos platfɔm, in dizayn, ɛn in sos kod na Kharis Church ɔ dɛn laysɛnsa in prɔpati. Yu akawnt de gi yu pɔsnal rayt we yu nɔ kin gi ɔda pɔsin fɔ yuz Kairos wayl yu na mɛmba; i nɔ de gi yu ownaship.

## 9. Warɛnti ɛn layabiliti

Dɛn de gi Kairos "as i de" ɛn "as i de avelebul". As fa as di lɔ alaw, Kharis Church de pul ɔl implayd warɛnti. Nɔtin insay dɛn tɛms ya nɔ de limit wi layabiliti fɔ dɛt ɔ bɔdi ɛt we nɛgligɛns kɔz, fɔ frɔd, ɔ fɔ ɛni layabiliti we Inglish lɔ nɔ alaw yu fɔ pul.

Ɔnda dat, wi ful layabiliti we kɔmɔt frɔm aw yu yuz Kairos tay to ɛni mɔni we yu dɔn pe wi bɔt Kairos insay di twɛlv mont bifo di klem (we fɔ plɛnti mɛmba go bi ziro).

## 10. Chenj pan dɛn tɛms ya

We wi mek big chenj pan dɛn tɛms ya, wi go put nyu vɔshɔn ɛn aks yu fɔ rid ɛn aksɛpt dɛn di nɛks tɛm yu sayn in. Smɔl tayp mistek wi kin fiks witawt aks yu. If yu nɔ aksɛpt big chenj, yu kin klos yu akawnt.

## 11. Kɔntakt

Kharis Church: [privacy@kharis.org](mailto:privacy@kharis.org)
