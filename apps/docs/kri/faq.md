# Kwɛshɔn dɛn we Pipul de Aks

## Fɔ stat

**Udat kin mek akawnt?**

Ɛnibɔdi kin rɛjista frɔm di login pej. Afta i rɛjista, di akawnt de stat as **Pɛndin** ɛn Pasto ɔ Admin fɔ aprov am bifo i gɛt ful akses.

**A rɛjista bɔt a nɔ de si nɔtin. Wetin rɔng?**

Prɔbabli yu akawnt de wet fɔ aproval. Kɔntakt yu branch pasto ɔ di chɔch admin ɛn aks dɛn fɔ aprov yu rɛjistreshɔn. Te dat tɛm, yu kin si yu yon prɔfayl nɔmɔ.

**A kin chenj mi om branch?**

Nɔto stret. Kɔntakt yu chɔch admin. Na Admin ɛn Pasto de manej branch asaynmɛnt, bikɔs dat de disayd us mɛmba, dipatmɛnt, ɛn fɛloship yu kin si.

---

## Mɛmba dɛn

**Mɛmba kin si ɔda mɛmba in prɔfayl?**

Nɔ. Mɛmba we nɔ gɛt grant de si in yon prɔfayl nɔmɔ, ɛn di Mɛmba dayrɛktri nɔ de insay in saydba. Grup lida de si di pipul insay di fɛloship ɔ dipatmɛnt we i de lid, wit dɛn kɔntakt ditel. Di ful branch dayrɛktri nid branch grant.

**Udat de sho insay di Mɛmba dayrɛktri?**

Kɔnfam Mɛmba ɛn Ritɔna: di pipul we dɔn sayn in ɛn we de na di branch. Vizita ɛn pikin rɛkɔd, we plɛnti tɛm fɔm ɔ sefgadin prɔses de mek insɛf, de bihɛn dɛn yon skrin so dat dɛn nɔ ful di dayrɛktri. Yu kin yuz di filta fɔ si wan kayn nɔmɔ.

**Aw a go jɔyn fɛloship?**

Go na **Fɛloship dɛn**, luk di grup dɛn we de, ɛn aks fɔ jɔyn. Di fɛloship lida go aprov ɔ rifyuz. Yu kin de na wan fɛloship nɔmɔ at a tɛm.

**Aw a go jɔyn dipatmɛnt?**

Aks fɔ jɔyn frɔm di Dipatmɛnt pej. Di dipatmɛnt lida de aprov. Yu kin de na tu dipatmɛnt nɔmɔ at a tɛm.

**A kin si mi atɛndans istri?**

Yɛs. Go na **Mi Atɛndans** insay di saydba. I de sho yu las 12 wik fɔ sɛvis atɛndans, yu pɔnkchualiti brekdawn, ɛn yu strik naw.

---

## Mɛmbaship klas

**A na "Mɛmba"?**

Nɔmɔ if yu dɔn finish di fɔ-wik mɛmbaship klas. Na spɛshal tin wit spɛshal satifiket, ɛn nɔtin ɛls nɔ de gi am: nɔto plɛnti ia fɔ kam, nɔto fɔ sav insay dipatmɛnt, nɔto fɔ lid fɛloship, nɔto fɔ finish di Nyu Biliva program. Si [Mɛmbaship Klas](/kri/platform/membership).

**Aw a go jɔyn mɛmbaship klas?**

Yu nɔ kin ɛnrol yusɛf. Go na **Mɛmbaship**, luk di kɔhɔt dɛn, ɛn sho se yu want. Dat de put yu pan di wetlis. Dɔn mɛmbaship admin de admit pipul frɔm di wetlis insay wan spɛshal kɔhɔt.

**Omɔch tɛm mi ples pan di wetlis de las?**

180 de. If i laps, yu kin sho se yu want bak, ɛn di klɔk de stat egen.

**Wetin a fɔ du fɔ gradyuet?**

Siks tin: atɛnd ɔl di fɔ sɛshɔn, pas di omwok fɔ ɛvri wan, pas di kwiz fɔ ɛvri wan, pas di las tɛst, tek dat tɛst bifo di dɛdlayn if di kɔhɔt gɛt wan, ɛn atɛnd di indɔkshɔn. Yu yon Mɛmbaship pej de sho us pan di siks nɔ de yet.

---

## Lida dɛn

**A na dipatmɛnt lida. Wetin mek a nɔ kin si mɛmba awtsay mi dipatmɛnt?**

Lida akses tay tayt to di grup dɛn we yu de lid. Yu kin si ɛn manej mɛmba dɛn we de insay yu dipatmɛnt ɔ fɛloship nɔmɔ. Na so dɛn mek am: i de protɛkt mɛmba prayvesi.

**A kin si mi yon grup in Nyu Biliva ɛn mɛmbaship klas prɔgrɛs?**

Yɛs. Fɛloship ɔ dipatmɛnt lida de si dat prɔgrɛs fɔ in yon pipul, nɛro bay udat de insay in grup nɔto bay branch.

**A kin aprov mɛmba fɔ ɛnta di chɔch?**

Nɔ. Na Pasto ɛn Admin nɔmɔ kin aprov nyu mɛmba rɛjistreshɔn. As lida, yu kin aprov jɔyn rikwɛst fɔ yu yon dipatmɛnt ɔ fɛloship, bɔt nɔto sistem-lɛvul aproval.

**A na lida insay di Admin dipatmɛnt. A de si di fɔm dɛn we dɛn sɛn?**

Yɛs. Admin dipatmɛnt lida kin rivyu di frɔnt dɛsk fɔm dɛn, wit Fɔs-Tɛm Vizita, Alta Kɔl, ɛn Baptizm fɔm.

---

## Pasto dɛn

**A kin si data frɔm ɔda branch?**

Nɔ. Branch grant tay tayt to dat branch. Krɔs-branch say na fɔ platfɔm admin, ɛn fɔ di tu chɔch-wayd grant: Mɛmbaship Admin ɛn Sefgadin Ɛd.

**A de administa mi branch. Wetin mek a nɔ kin si di sefgadin inbɔks?**

Bikɔs sefgadin say nɔ de kam frɔm branch grant. Di branch in **Mayn Pasto** we de na di sit de gɛt am insɛf bikɔs i ol dat ɔfis, ɛn **Sefgadin Lida** de gɛt am bay grant, pɔposli as pɔsin we nɔ tay to di branch lidaship. Minista we ol di sem branch grant lɛk di Mayn Pasto nɔ de si am. Di wɛlfɛa inbɔks na ɔda tin igen, ɛn na di Branch Sistem Admin in yon.

**A kin mek nyu branch?**

Nɔ. Fɔ mek ɛn manej branch na Admin wok. Yu kin manej ɔltin insay yu branch, bɔt nɔto di branch dɛn dɛnsɛf.

**A kin gi mɛmba Admin-lɛvul rol?**

Nɔ. Fɔ gi sistem-lɛvul admin akses na Admin wok nɔmɔ.

---

## Admin dɛn

**Aw a go gi pɔsin fɛloship lida akses?**

Nɔto frɔm in prɔfayl. Na di grup de gi fɛloship ɛn dipatmɛnt grant, so go na **Fɛloship dɛn**, opin di fɛloship, ɛn nem am as in lida ɔ ko-lida. Di grant de rayt insɛf, ɛn if yu pul am awt pan di grup, dat de pul am. Di sem tin fɔ Dipatmɛnt Lida ɛn Dɛpyuti, frɔm **Dipatmɛnt dɛn**. If yu tray fɔ ad dɛn grant frɔm mɛmba prɔfayl, dɛn go rifyuz ɛn pɔynt yu to di grup.

Branch-skop ɛn chɔch-skop grant, lɛk Branch Sistem Admin ɔ Mɛmbaship Admin, *dɛn de gi dɛn* frɔm di mɛmba in rol sɛkshɔn.

**A ad grant bɔt di pɔsin se nɔtin nɔ chenj.**

Di sava ɔnɔ am wan tɛm; in skrin nɔ ɔnɔ am. Mɛnyu ɛn bɔtin de kam frɔm in sayn-in tokin, we de rinyu bakgrawnd we i de yuz di ap. If yu aks am fɔ sayn awt ɛn sayn in bak, i go sho wan tɛm. Fɔ pul grant na di ɔda we: di sava stɔp fɔ ɔnɔ am wan tɛm, ivin if wan bɔtin we nɔ wok igen de stil pan in skrin smɔl tɛm.

**If a gi pɔsin di "Pasto" onorifik, dat de gi am pamishɔn?**

Nɔ. Di onorifik na displey nɔmɔ. Fɔ mɛmba gɛt pasto-lɛvul akses pan branch, i nid `BranchAdmin` grant we Admin gi am.

**Kairos de ol givin ɔ doneshɔn?**

Nɔ. Faynanshal rɛkɔd nɔ de insay di skop pɔposli, ɛn no doneshɔn data nɔ de insay di sistem.

---

## Data ɛn Prayvesi

**Dɛn ɛva dilit mi data?**

Kairos de yuz sɔft dilit, so if dɛn diaktivet mɛmba ɔ grup, ɔl di istri rɛkɔd de stil de. Yu kin dawnlod kɔpi fɔ yu yon data, ɛn aks fɔ dilit yu akawnt, frɔm **Sɛtin → Prayvesi ɛn Data**.

**Udat kin si mi atɛndans?**

Yu atɛndans de sho to yu, yu pasto, ɛn admin dɛn. Lida dɛn kin si atɛndans data fɔ mɛmba dɛn insay dɛn yon grup nɔmɔ.

**Udat kin si mi ɛmajɛnsi kɔntakt?**

Yu ɛmajɛnsi kɔntakt info de sho to yu, yu pasto, ɛn admin dɛn. Rɛgyula mɛmba ɛn lida nɔ kin si am.
