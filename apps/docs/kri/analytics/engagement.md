# Ɛngejmɛnt

<SectionClip slug="analytics/engagement" />

Ɛngejmɛnt de aks difrɛn kwɛshɔn frɔm atɛndans. Atɛndans de aks if pɔsin kam. Ɛngejmɛnt de aks if i tru-tru de insay di chɔch layf, ɛn na dat kin disayd if i go stil de ya nɛks ia.

::: warning Dɛn dɔn pul di old ɛngejmɛnt kad
Old vɔshɔn fɔ di dashbɔd bin gɛt wan **Mɛmba Ɛngejmɛnt** kad we bin de sho wan skɔ: Ay / Midul / Lo. I dɔn gɔn, wit di rɛst fɔ di Mishɔn Kɔntrol strip. Di mɛzhɔ dɛn we de dɔng ya na wetin tek in ples, ɛn na plɛnti nɔmba pɔposli, nɔto wan.
:::

## Di onɛs dinomineta

Ɔltin pan dis pej de stan pan wan ayidia: **ɛngejd mɛmba** na pɔsin we kam at lis wan tɛm insay wan windo we yu pik, we yu kin sɛt to 3, 6, ɔ 12 mont frɔm di ɛda fɔ [Atɛndans Ripɔt dɛn](/kri/analytics/attendance-reports#di-ɛngejd-mɛmba-dinomineta).

Dat windo na di kɔntrol we gɛt di bigis ifɛkt insay di ful ripɔt skrin. Sɛt am nɛro ɛn yu de aks aw yu de du wit di pipul dɛn we de araynd naw. Sɛt am wayd ɛn yu de aks omɔch pan ɔl di pipul dɛn we yu ɛva gɛt de kam stil. Ɔltu na tru kwɛshɔn, ɛn dɛn de gi tu difrɛn pasɛnt fɔ di sem chɔch.

## Omɔch ɔfin ɛngejd mɛmba dɛn tru-tru de kam

Di atɛndans frikwɛnsi brekdawn de split di ɛngejd rol insay fayv band ovas di windo we yu pik:

| Band | Wetin i min |
|------|---------|
| Ɛvri wik | I atɛnd at lis 75% pan di sɛvis dɛn |
| Ɛvri tu wik | 40% te 74% |
| Ɛvri mont | 15% te 39% |
| Nɔ ɔfin | Ɔnda 15%, bɔt at lis wan tɛm |
| Dɔman | I de pan di aktiv rol, bɔt no atɛndans at ɔl insay di windo |

Luk di shep, nɔto wan ba. Chɔch we in Ɛvri wik band de smɔl dɔng wayl Ɛvri mont de gro, i de lɔs strɛnt bifo i lɔs pipul, ɛn na dat tɛm fɔ akt.

## Fɔ de insay, nɔto jɔs fɔ de ya

Sɛvis atɛndans bay insɛf na wik signal. Pɔsin we de kam ɛvri wik bɔt i nɔ de na fɛloship, nɔ de na dipatmɛnt, ɛn nɔbɔdi nɔ kɔntakt am, i tay to di bildin, nɔto di chɔch. Kairos de sho ɛvri wan pan dɛn sɛpret:

| Signal | Usay fɔ si am |
|--------|----------------|
| I de kam na sɛvis | Di pa-mɛmba it map insay [Atɛndans Ripɔt dɛn](/kri/analytics/attendance-reports#atɛndans-it-map) |
| I de na fɛloship | Di nɔ-grup kyu de lis ɛnibɔdi we nɔ de |
| I de na dipatmɛnt | Di sem kyu; i want di tu fɔ ful |
| Dɛn dɔn kɔntakt am | Fɔlo ɔp istri pan di mɛmba in prɔfayl |
| I de muv go Mɛmbaship | [Mɛmbaship Klas](/kri/platform/membership) |
| I de muv tru disaypulship | [Nyu Biliva dɛn](/kri/platform/new-believers) |

## Fɔ fɔlo wan pɔsin

- **Di it map** de sho wan layn fɔ ɛvri mɛmba akrɔs di las 13 wik, wit di misin-strik kawnt, so yu kin sɔt bay udat gɔn lɔngɛs
- **Mi Atɛndans** de gi di mɛmba in yon ret, pɔnkchualiti split, ɛn strik
- **Fɔlo ɔp istri** pan mɛmba in prɔfayl de sho ɛvri kɔntakt we dɛn rɛkɔd bɔt am, insay ɛvri kɔntɛks, wit di wɛlfɛa ɛn sefgadin flag dɛn we yu kin si
- **Dipatmɛnt ɛn fɛloship mɛmba lis** de sho if i aktiv ɔ if i kwayɛt insay di grup

## Fɔ mek ɛngejmɛnt bɛta

Di kyu dɛn de so dat dis go bi wok, nɔto ges:

- Wok di **fɔs-tɛm** kyu insay de, nɔto wik. I gɛt pipul we nɔbɔdi nɔ kɔntakt at ɔl nɔmɔ.
- Wok di **nɔ-grup** kyu stɛdi. I de klia we pɔsin tru-tru jɔyn sɔntin, ɛn na dat na di pɔynt.
- Wok di **du naw** kyu fɔ ol di prɔmis we yu yon fɔlo ɔp mek.
- Ron afta di **Dɔman** band bifo dɛn nem tɔn Nɔ ɔfin insay wan waydɛr windo.
- Ron di mɛmbaship klas ɔfin inɔf so dat di gap bitwin Ɔl Kɔngrigeshɔn ɛn Kɔnfam Mɛmba stɔp fɔ wayd.
- Rɛkɔd fɛloship atɛndans, so dat di pashipeshɔn kin sho at ɔl. Mitin we dɛn nɔ rɛkɔd tan jɔs lɛk mitin we nɔbɔdi nɔ kam.
