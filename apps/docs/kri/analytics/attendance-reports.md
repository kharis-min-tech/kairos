# Atɛndans Ripɔt dɛn

<SectionClip slug="analytics/attendance-reports" />

Atɛndans ripɔt de gi yu klia vyu fɔ di sɛvis ɛn fɛloship pashipeshɔn trɛnd ovas tɛm, we de ɛp yu fɔ si drɔp kwik ɛn fɔ ansa bifo i tɔn patan.

## Tu ples fɔ luk

| Usay | Wetin i de du |
|-------|----------------|
| **Ripɔt → Atɛndans** | Di big trɛnd: atɛndans de go ɔp ɔ dawn |
| **Atɛndans → Ripɔt** | Di ful ripɔt skrin: udat de kam, omɔch ɔfin, ɛn udat dɔn stɔp |

## Di ful ripɔt skrin

Frɔm **Atɛndans**, opin **Ripɔt**. Ɔltin pan di pej de shɛr wan kɔntrol, ɛn na dat yu fɔ ɔndastand pas ɔl.

### Di ɛngejd-mɛmba dinomineta

Atɛndans *ret* gud nɔmɔ lɛk wetin yu de divayd bay. If yu divayd bay di ful rol, ɛvri branch go luk bad, bikɔs di rol gɛt pipul we muv go ɔda ples tri ia bifo.

So di dinomineta ya na **ɛngejd mɛmba**: pipul we kam at lis wan tɛm insay wan windo we yu pik. Sɛt am to **3, 6, ɔ 12 mont** frɔm di kɔntrol pan di pej ɛda, ɛn ɛvri ret, bɔkɛt, ɛn it map layn pan di pej go kalkulet bak akɔdin to dat pik.

Nɛro windo de aks "aw wi de du wit di pipul dɛn we de araynd naw". Wayd wan de aks "omɔch pan di pipul dɛn we wi ɛva gɛt de kam stil". Ɔltu na tru kwɛshɔn; dɛn de gi difrɛn nɔmba, ɛn di windo de tɛl yu us wan yu aks.

### Atɛndans it map

Wan layn fɔ ɛvri ɛngejd mɛmba, wan kɔlɔm fɔ ɛvri sɛvis, akrɔs di las 13 wik, di oldes wan pan di lɛft. Ɛvri sɛl gɛt kɔlɔ: de ya, ɔnlayn, let, ɔ mis.

Dis na di vyu we de fɛn pɔsin, nɔto trɛnd. Ɔrizɔntal band fɔ kɔlɔ we stɔp wan tɛm na pɔsin we drɔp ɔf, ɛn di misin-strik kɔlɔm de kawnt in absɛns dɛn bak to bak, so dat yu kin sɔt di lis bay udat gɔn lɔngɛs. Ɔ sɔt bay atɛndans pasɛnt ɔ bay nem if yu want.

Di it map na fɔ ɛvri branch: pik wan branch ɛn i de sho.

### Atɛndans frikwɛnsi

Aw di ɛngejd rol de split bay omɔch ɔfin pipul tru-tru de kam, ovas di windo we yu pik:

| Band | Wetin i min |
|------|---------|
| Ɛvri wik | I atɛnd at lis 75% pan di sɛvis dɛn |
| Ɛvri tu wik | 40% te 74% |
| Ɛvri mont | 15% te 39% |
| Nɔ ɔfin | Ɔnda 15%, bɔt at lis wan tɛm |
| Dɔman | I de pan di aktiv rol, bɔt no atɛndans at ɔl insay di windo |

Dɔman na di band fɔ wach. Dɛn na pipul we di chɔch de stil kawnt bɔt nɔbɔdi nɔ si dɛn insay di ful windo.

### Fɔs-tɛm vas ritɔnin

Wiki kawnt fɔ fɔs-tɛm pipul igens pipul we de kam bak, ovas di las 12 wik. If yu rid di tu layn dɛn togeda, dɛn de tɛl yu us wan pan tu difrɛn prɔblɛm yu gɛt: if di fɔs-tɛm layn flat, nɔ nyu pɔsin nɔ de kam; if di fɔs-tɛm layn ɛlti bɔt di ritɔnin layn flat, pipul de kam wan tɛm ɛn dɛn nɔ de kam bak.

### Filta dɛn

Nɛro ɛnitin we de ɔp de bay branch, bay dipatmɛnt, ɔ bay fɛloship, so dat dipatmɛnt lida kin si in yon tim in patan, nɔto di branch in yon.

## Fɔ go pan di big trɛnd

Go na **Ripɔt** ɛn di pej de opin pan di **Atɛndans** tab bay difɔlt.

## Sɛvis atɛndans

### Wiki Atɛndans Ret chat

Layn chat we de sho di avrej atɛndans ret (di pasɛnt fɔ aktiv mɛmba dɛn we de ya) ovas di las 8 wik. Ɛvri pɔynt na wan wik.

- Flat layn araynd 80%+ ɛlti
- Layn we de go dawn ɔltɛm nid atɛnshɔn
- Sharp drɔp pan wan spɛshal de kin min spɛshal ivɛnt ɔ sɛvis we dɛn kansel

### Trɛnd sayn

Di chat ɛda de sho wan trɛnd bajj:
- **Trɛndin ɔp**: atɛndans de bɛta
- **Trɛndin dawn**: atɛndans de drɔp (dɛn de sho am insay rɛd as wɔnin)

### Mont Aylayt

Sɔmari insay plen wɔd pan di rayt say fɔ di chat. Fɔ ɛgzampul:

> "Atɛndans nid atɛnshɔn. Di avrej ret na 80% fɔ di las 8 wik"

Klik **Si di brekdawn** fɔ go insay di ditel.

## Fɛloship atɛndans

### Tɔp Fɛloship dɛn we Pipul Atɛnd

Ba chat we de rank di fɛloship dɛn bay ɔl di mitin atɛndans. Di ba lɛnt na omɔch mɛmba atɛnd mitin insay di tɛm. Klik ɛni ba fɔ si dat fɛloship in ful mitin istri.

Dis de ɛp yu fɔ si us fɛloship de du fayn ɛn us wan nid pastɔral sapɔt ɔ nyu plan.

## Mi Atɛndans (wan pɔsin in vyu)

Ɛvri mɛmba gɛt in yon atɛndans ripɔt pan in **Mi Atɛndans** pej:

- 12-wik atɛndans ret
- Pɔnkchualiti brekdawn (De ya, Let, Ɔnlayn, Mis) as ba chat
- Strik naw (sɛvis bak to bak we yu atɛnd)
- Di ful sɛvis istri fɔ di windo, di oldes fɔs

Dis na prayvet: na di mɛmba, in pasto, ɛn admin dɛn nɔmɔ kin si am.

## Sɛvis tayp insay ripɔt

Di atɛndans data gɛt ɔl di sɛvis tayp:
- Sɔnde Sɛvis
- Midwik Sɛvis
- Spɛshal Sɛvis
- Prea Mitin

Filta bay sɛvis tayp we yu want si Sɔnde atɛndans sɛpret frɔm midwik.

## Fɔ ɛkspɔt atɛndans data

Yuz **Ɛkspɔt CSV** pan di Atɛndans pej fɔ dawnlod di ful atɛndans data fɔ di filta we de naw. Di de rɛnj ɛn branch filta de wok pan di ɛkspɔt.

## Skop bay rol

| Udat | Wetin i de si |
|-----|--------------|
| Admin | Ɔl branch, ɔl sɛvis |
| Branch grant | In yon branch nɔmɔ |
| Grup lida | In yon fɛloship ɔ dipatmɛnt nɔmɔ |
| No grant | In yon atɛndans nɔmɔ (Mi Atɛndans pej) |
