# Groth Ripɔt dɛn

<SectionClip slug="analytics/growth-reports" />

Groth ripɔt de fɔlo aw di chɔch de gro: nyu mɛmba dɛn we de jɔyn, mɛmba dɛn we kam bak afta dɛn bin kwayɛt, ɛn aw di ful kɔngrigeshɔn de go ovas tɛm.

## Fɔ go pan di groth data

Go na **Ripɔt** ɛn klik di **Gro** tab pan di tɔp rayt.

## Di big nɔmba dɛn

| Nɔmba | Diskripshɔn |
|------|-------------|
| Nyu Mɛmba | Mɛmba dɛn we dɛn ad insay di tɛm yu pik |
| Mont-pan-mont chenj | Lɛk "+24 las mont"; aw dis tɛm tan wit di wan bifo |
| Gro Mont dɛn | Mont dɛn bak to bak we di mɛmba nɔmba gro |

## Mɛmbaship groth chat

Di Dashbɔd in Mishɔn Kɔntrol panɛl gɛt wan **Mɛmbaship Groth** chat we de sho di las 6 mont fɔ di mɛmba kawnt. Ɛvri pɔynt na di ɔl aktiv mɛmba kawnt pan di ɛn fɔ dat mont.

Yuz di Ripɔt pej in Gro tab fɔ wan ful vyu wit de rɛnj kɔntrol.

## Wetin de kawnt as gro

Dɛn de kawnt mɛmba as nyu we dɛn **aprov** in akawnt, nɔto we i rɛjista. Dis de stɔp pɛndin akawnt dɛn fɔ pump di groth nɔmba.

## Kɔngrigeshɔn brekdawn

Di Dashbɔd de sho di ful kɔngrigeshɔn split insay fɔ katigɔri, ɛn di fɔ ɔltɛm de mek di ful rol.

| Katigɔri | Wetin i de kawnt tru-tru |
|----------|------------------------|
| Mɛmba | Pipul we dɔn **finish di fɔ-wik mɛmbaship klas**. Dis na di fɔmal mɛmbaship nɔmba, ɛn nɔtin ɛls nɔ de mek am. |
| Ritɔna | Pipul we de kam ɔltɛm bɔt dɛn nɔ du di klas yet. Fɔ plɛnti branch, dis na di bigis pan di fɔ. |
| Vizita | Pipul we de kam sɔm tɛm, plɛnti tɛm Fɔs-Tɛm Vizita fɔm de mek dɛn |
| Pikin dɛn | Dɛn de trak dɛn sɛpret, wit dɛn yon prɔtɛkshɔn |

::: warning Mɛmba nɔto "ɛvribɔdi we gɛt akawnt"
Dis na di nɔmba we pipul de misrid pas ɔl insay Kairos. Fetful pɔsin we dɔn sav insay dipatmɛnt fɔ fayv ia na **Ritɔna**, nɔto Mɛmba, te i finish di klas. If di Ritɔna kawnt de go ɔp bɔt di Mɛmba kawnt flat, dat min se pipul de kam ɛn de tap bɔt di klas nɔ de ron ɔfin inɔf. Si [Mɛmbaship Klas](/kri/platform/membership).
:::

## Branch-lɛvul vas ɔl-di-chɔch

- **Admin** de si groth akrɔs ɔl branch, wit wan branch kɔmperizn chat
- **Pasto** (ɛnibɔdi we gɛt branch grant) de si groth fɔ in branch nɔmɔ
- **Lida** de si groth fɔ in dipatmɛnt ɔ fɛloship nɔmɔ
- **Mɛmba** nɔ kin go pan Ripɔt

## Fɔ yuz groth ripɔt fayn

- If di mɛmba kawnt flat ɔ de drɔp bɔt atɛndans de ay, dat min se vizita nɔ de kɔnvat, so chɛk di [Sol Paplayn](/kri/platform/souls-pipeline) ɛn di [Nyu Biliva](/kri/platform/new-believers) paplayn
- If di mɛmba kawnt de gro bɔt atɛndans de drɔp, dat min se nyu mɛmba nɔ de tap, so chɛk fɛloship mɛmbaship ɛn fɔlo ɔp ret
- Put di groth tugeda wit di [Ɛngejmɛnt](/kri/analytics/engagement) nɔmba dɛn fɔ gɛt di ful pikchɔ
