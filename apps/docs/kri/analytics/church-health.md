# Chɔch Ɛlt

<SectionClip slug="analytics/church-health" />

"Chɔch ɛlt" nɔto wan nɔmba insay Kairos. Na sɔm nɔmba insay sɔm ples, ɛn dis pej de tɛl yu us nɔmba de ansa us kwɛshɔn.

::: warning If yu de luk fɔ di old ɛngejmɛnt skɔ
Old vɔshɔn fɔ di dashbɔd bin gɛt wan "Mishɔn Sɔmari" band wit wan ɛngejmɛnt skɔ: Ay / Midul / Lo, ɛn wan "Mishɔn Kɔntrol Ripɔt" strip wit siks smɔl chat. Dɛn dɔn pul ɔltu. Wan kɔmbayn skɔ bin de ayd mɔ pas wetin i bin de sho: tu branch kin gɛt di sem skɔ fɔ tu difrɛn rizin, ɛn yu nɔ bin kin si nɔn pan di tu rizin. Wetin tek in ples na wan smɔl sɛt nɔmba we yu kin tru-tru akt pan, ɛvri wan na di ples usay yu go akt.
:::

## Di nɔmba dɛn we yu fɔ wach

### Kɔnfam mɛmba igens ɔl di kɔngrigeshɔn

Ɔltu de sho insay Pɔls. Di gap bitwin dɛn na omɔch disaypulship wok yu lɛf bak: pipul we de pat pan di chɔch bɔt dɛn nɔ du di [mɛmbaship klas](/kri/platform/membership) yet. Gap we jɔs de gro min se di klas nɔ de ron ɔfin inɔf.

### Atɛndans ret igens ɛngejd mɛmba

Atɛndans ret min sɔntin nɔmɔ we yu no wetin i de divayd bay. [Atɛndans Ripɔt dɛn](/kri/analytics/attendance-reports#di-ɛngejd-mɛmba-dinomineta) de divayd bay **ɛngejd mɛmba**, we min pipul we kam at lis wan tɛm insay wan windo we yu pik (3, 6, ɔ 12 mont), nɔto bay di ful rol. If yu divayd bay di rol, ɛvri branch go luk bad, bikɔs di rol gɛt pipul we muv go ɔda ples sɔm ia bifo.

### Di Dɔman band

Insay di atɛndans frikwɛnsi brekdawn, Dɔman de kawnt pipul pan di aktiv rol we gɛt **no** atɛndans insay di ful windo. Dis na di ɛlt nɔmba we yu kin akt pan pas ɔl insay di sistem: na lis fɔ nem, ɛn na di lis we di [nɔ-grup ɛn du-naw fɔlo ɔp kyu](/kri/platform/follow-ups) de ya fɔ wok.

### Pɔls wɔnin dɛn

Pɔls de sho dɛn ya insɛf, as link nɔto as nɔmba we yu fɔ ɔndastand:

- Sɛvis rɛjista we nɔbɔdi nɔ tek
- Mɛmba dɛn we de drɔp ɔf pan atɛndans
- Branch dɛn we de fɔdɔm bak
- Branch we nɔ gɛt Mayn Pasto

### Fɔlo ɔp kɔvrej

Sol dɛn we nɔ gɛt kɔntakt fɔ sɛvin de ɔ mɔ, dɛn de kawnt dɛn as **Kritikal** pan di [Sol Dashbɔd](/kri/platform/souls-dashboard). Nyu Biliva ɛnrolmɛnt we tap, we min no prɔgrɛs fɔ sɛvin de ɔ mɔ, dɛn de sho as wɔnin kawnt pan di [Nyu Biliva](/kri/platform/new-believers) pej.
