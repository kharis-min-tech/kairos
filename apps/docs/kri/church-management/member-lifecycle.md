# Mɛmba Layf Sakul

<SectionClip slug="church-management/member-lifecycle" />

Dis gayd de kɔva di ful jɔni fɔ mɛmba insay Kairos, frɔm di fɔs rɛjistreshɔn, tru aproval, aktiv mɛmbaship, ɛn afta dat diaktiveshɔn.

## 1. Rɛjistreshɔn

Nyu mɛmba kin rɛjista insɛf frɔm di login skrin bay fɔ klik **Mek Akawnt**. I de ful:

- Fɔs ɛn las nem
- Imel adrɛs
- Paswɔd
- Fon nɔmba
- De we i bɔn ɛn jɛnda
- Us branch na in yon
- Adrɛs (i nɔ nid fɔ put am at di rɛjistreshɔn)

Afta i sɛn am, dɛn de mek in akawnt wit **Pɛndin** stetɔs. I de gɛt kɔnfɔmeshɔn ɛn i kin login, bɔt i kin si in yon prɔfayl nɔmɔ te dɛn aprov am.

Ɔ, Pasto ɔ Admin kin ad mɛmba stret frɔm **Mɛmba dɛn → + Ad Mɛmba**. Mɛmba dɛn we dɛn ad dis we, dɛn aprov dɛn insɛf.

## 2. Aproval

Rɛjistreshɔn dɛn we de wet de sho insay di **Aproval Kyu** we yu kin fɛn pan di Mɛmba pej ɛda. Fɔ aprov, yu nid branch grant, ɔ yu fɔ na platfɔm admin.

Fɔ aprov mɛmba:
1. Go na **Mɛmba dɛn → Aproval Kyu**
2. Rivyu di ditel dɛn we di mɛmba sɛn
3. Klik **Aprov**. Wan tɛm i de gɛt ful mɛmba akses
4. Ɔ klik **Rifyuz**. Dɛn de pul in akawnt

We dɛn dɔn aprov am, di mɛmba de si di ful navigeshɔn fɔ in rol ɛn i kin:
- Si ɛn ɛdit in prɔfayl
- Si in fɛloship ɛn dipatmɛnt dɛn
- Sɛn fɔm
- Si in atɛndans istri

## 3. Aktiv mɛmbaship

Aktiv mɛmba we dɛn dɔn aprov kin:
- Kip in prɔfayl ɔp to det (kɔntakt ditel, pikchɔ, ɛmajɛnsi info)
- Aks fɔ jɔyn fɛloship
- Aks fɔ jɔyn te tu dipatmɛnt
- Sho se i want fɔ du di mɛmbaship klas
- Rɛjista fɔ awtrich program insay in branch
- Sɛn fɔm (Fɔs-Tɛm Vizita, Baptizm, Tɛstimoni, ɛn so on)
- Si in atɛndans istri pan Mi Atɛndans

## 4. Fɔ gi rol

Usay dɛn de gi grant de pan wetin di grant tay to.

- **Branch ɛn chɔch-wayd grant** dɛn de gi am frɔm di mɛmba in rol sɛkshɔn. Platfɔm admin kin du am ɛniwe; Branch Sistem Admin kin gi Branch Sistem Admin insay in yon branch.
- **Fɛloship ɛn dipatmɛnt grant, na di grup de gi am.** Nem pɔsin as fɛloship lida ɔ ko-lida, ɔ dipatmɛnt lida ɔ dɛpyuti, ɛn di grant de rayt insɛf. If yu pul am awt pan di grup, dat de pul di grant. Yu nɔ kin ad dɛn frɔm mɛmba prɔfayl.

Ɛgzampul:
- Dɛn nem am lida fɔ fɛloship → i de opin dat fɛloship in mɛmba lis, mitin, ɛn fɔlo ɔp
- Dɛn nem am lida fɔ dipatmɛnt → i de opin dat dipatmɛnt in mɛmba lis, rota, ɛn fɔlo ɔp
- `BranchAdmin` tay to wan branch → ɔl di branch wok, plɔs i kin gi am to ɔda pipul

**We di chenj de wok:** di sava de rid di grant dɛn frɔm di databes ɛvri rikwɛst, so nyu ɔ pul-bak grant de wok wan tɛm. Di pɔsin in yon mɛnyu ɛn bɔtin dɛn de kam frɔm in sayn-in tokin, so dɛn de chenj we di tokin rinyu, ɔ wan tɛm if i sayn awt ɛn sayn in bak.

## 4b. Fɔ bi Mɛmba

Fɔ de pan di rol nɔto di sem tin lɛk fɔ bi Mɛmba. Di fɔmal stɛp na di fɔ-wik mɛmbaship klas: sho se yu want, mek mɛmbaship admin admit yu insay kɔhɔt, atɛnd ɛn pas di fɔ sɛshɔn, pas di las tɛst, atɛnd di indɔkshɔn, ɛn gradyuet.

Te dat tɛm, dɛn de kawnt di pɔsin as **Ritɔna** insay di dashbɔd ɛn ripɔt, nɔto as Mɛmba. Si [Mɛmbaship Klas](/kri/platform/membership).

## 5. Diaktiveshɔn

Fɔ diaktivet mɛmba na sɔft dilit. Ɔl in data (atɛndans rɛkɔd, fɔlo ɔp not, dipatmɛnt istri, klas rɛkɔd) de stil de. I nɔ kin login igen bɔt nɔtin nɔ kɔmɔt pan di ripɔt ɔ di istri rɛkɔd dɛn.

Fɔ diaktivet, yu nid branch grant, ɔ yu fɔ na platfɔm admin.

Mɛmba kin dilit in yon akawnt bak frɔm **Sɛtin → Prayvesi ɛn Data**, ɛn i kin dawnlod kɔpi fɔ in data bifo i du am.

Fɔ diaktivet: opin di mɛmba kad pan di Mɛmba pej ɛn klik **Diaktivet**. Kɔnfam di akshɔn.

Fɔ riaktivet: fɛn di mɛmba wit di **Inaktiv** stetɔs filta ɛn klik **Riaktivet**.

## 6. CSV impɔt ɛn ɛkspɔt

Fɔ big wok:

- **Impɔt CSV**: uplod wan sprɛdshit fɔ mɛmba dɛn fɔ mek plɛnti akawnt wan tɛm. Di sistem de chɛk ɛvri layn ɛn sho yu privyu bifo di impɔt.
- **Ɛkspɔt CSV**: dawnlod di mɛmba lis we yu filta. I gud fɔ ripɔt, awtsay mel, ɔ fɔ muv data.

Ɔltu de pan di Mɛmba pej ɛda fɔ ɛnibɔdi we gɛt branch grant.
