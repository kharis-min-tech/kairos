# Atɛndans Trakin

<SectionClip slug="church-management/attendance-tracking" />

Kairos de trak tu kayn atɛndans: **sɛvis atɛndans** fɔ Sɔnde, midwik, ɛn spɛshal sɛvis, ɛn **fɛloship mitin atɛndans** fɔ smɔl grup mitin.

## Sɛvis atɛndans

Dɛn de manej sɛvis atɛndans frɔm di **Atɛndans** pej insay di saydba.

### Udat de rɛkɔd am

Ɛnibɔdi we gɛt branch grant, ɛn platfɔm admin dɛn.

Mɛmba dɛn kin rɛkɔd dɛnsɛf bak, if dɛn branch dɔn ɔn sɛf chɛk in: wan tap, ɔ bay fɔ skan di QR kod we de chenj pan di wɛlkɔm dɛsk skrin. Si [Atɛndans](/kri/platform/attendance#sɛf-chɛk-in).

### Fɔ mek sɛvis rɛkɔd

1. Go na **Atɛndans**
2. Klik **+ Rɛkɔd wan sɛvis**
3. Ful di branch, sɛvis tayp, de, ɛn di ɔda ditel dɛn if yu want (tim, pricha, tɔpik)
4. Sev am. Di sɛvis de sho pan di lis

### Fɔ chɛk in mɛmba dɛn

1. Fɛn di sɛvis pan di lis
2. Klik **Chɛk In →**
3. Mak ɛvri mɛmba as **De ya**, **Nɔ de ya**, ɔ **Ɔnlayn**
4. If yu want, mak mɛmba as **Fɔs-Tɛm Vizita**
5. Sev di rɛjista

### Sɛvis tayp dɛn

| Tayp | We fɔ yuz am |
|------|----------|
| Sɔnde Sɛvis | Di wiki sɛvis |
| Midwik Sɛvis | Wɛnsde ɔ ɔda midwik de |
| Spɛshal Sɛvis | Sizin ɔ wan-tɛm ivɛnt |
| Prea Mitin | Mitin we na prea nɔmɔ |
| Ɔda | Ɛni ɔda kayn |

---

## Fɛloship mitin atɛndans

Dɛn de manej fɛloship atɛndans frɔm insay di fɛloship in pej, nɔto frɔm di mayn Atɛndans pej.

### Udat de rɛkɔd am

Fɛloship lida ɛn ko-lida de rɛkɔd atɛndans fɔ dɛn yon fɛloship. Pasto ɛn Admin kin rɛkɔd fɔ ɛni fɛloship insay dɛn branch.

### Fɔ mek mitin rɛkɔd

1. Go na **Fɛloship dɛn** ɛn opin wan fɛloship
2. Go na di mitin tab
3. Klik **+ Nyu Mitin**
4. Put di de, tɔpik, ples, ɛn not if yu want
5. Sev am

### Fɔ tek atɛndans

1. Opin wan mitin we dɔn de
2. Mak ɛvri mɛmba as **De ya**, **Nɔ de ya**, **Ɛkskyuz**, ɔ **Let**
3. Ad not fɔ ɛvri mɛmba if yu nid
4. Sev am

---

## Mi Atɛndans (pɔsnal vyu)

Ɛvri mɛmba gɛt in yon prayvet **Mi Atɛndans** pej insay di saydba. I de sho:

- Di 12-wik atɛndans ret wit wan wɔd bɔt di trɛnd
- Omɔch sɛvis yu atɛnd vas ɔl di sɛvis insay di windo
- Di de ɛn sɛvis fɔ yu las atɛndans
- Pɔnkchualiti brekdawn (De ya, Let, Ɔnlayn, Mis)
- Yu strik naw
- Di ful sɛvis istri fɔ di 12-wik windo

Na di mɛmba, in pasto, ɛn admin dɛn nɔmɔ kin si dis pej.

---

## Atɛndans insay ripɔt

Sɛvis atɛndans trɛnd de sho insay **Ripɔt → Atɛndans tab**. Di chat de sho di avrej atɛndans ret fɔ di las 8 wik fɔ ɛvri branch, wit di trɛnd sayn dɛn.

Fɔ di ful vyu, wit di pa-mɛmba it map, di atɛndans frikwɛnsi band dɛn, ɛn di fɔs-tɛm vas ritɔnin split, opin **Atɛndans → Ripɔt**. Si [Atɛndans Ripɔt dɛn](/kri/analytics/attendance-reports).

Lida dɛn de si di atɛndans data fɔ dɛn yon fɛloship ɔ dipatmɛnt mɛmba nɔmɔ.
