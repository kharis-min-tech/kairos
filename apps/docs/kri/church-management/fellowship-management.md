# Fɛloship Manejmɛnt

<SectionClip slug="church-management/fellowship-management" />

Dis gayd de sho aw fɛloship lida ɛn admin dɛn de mek, ron, ɛn kip smɔl grup dɛn insay Kairos.

## Fɔ mek fɛloship

Pasto ɛn Admin dɛn de mek fɛloship frɔm **Fɛloship dɛn → + Nyu Fɛloship**.

Fil dɛn we yu fɔ ful:
- Nem (i fɔ difrɛn insay di branch)
- Branch
- Fɛloship tayp (K-Groups, Kharis Express, New Breeds, KOC, KOC Colleges)
- Lida (i fɔ na mɛmba we de na di branch)
- Mitin plan (lɛk "Ɛvri Frayde, 6:30 PM")

If yu want:
- Ko-lida
- Diskripshɔn

## Fɔ kip di mɛmba lis

We di fɛloship dɔn de, mɛmba dɛn kin aks fɔ jɔyn am. Ɛvri mɛmba kin de na **wan fɛloship** nɔmɔ at a tɛm.

Fɛloship lida dɛn kin:
- Si di ful mɛmba lis
- Aprov ɔ rifyuz jɔyn rikwɛst
- Pul mɛmba awt pan di fɛloship
- Si di mɛmba kɔntakt ditel

## Fɔ rɛkɔd fɔlo ɔp

Fɔlo ɔp not pan fɛloship mɛmba de ɛp lida dɛn fɔ si di pastɔral kia. Frɔm di fɛloship lis, opin wan mɛmba ɛn ad fɔlo ɔp.

Fɔlo ɔp na **kɔntakt** ɔ **vizit**, ɛn di fɔm de chenj:

- Ɔltu de rɛkɔd di chanɛl dɛn we yu yuz (te siks, so dat wan tray pan plɛnti chanɛl na wan rɛkɔd), omɔch tɛm, not, ɛn nɛks fɔlo ɔp de if yu want
- Kɔntakt de rɛkɔd bak if yu rich am, ɛn in intrɛs lɛvul
- Vizit de rɛkɔd bak fes-to-fes ɔ ɔnlayn, if dɛn bin tɛl am bifo, di tɛm yu rich ɛn lɛf, di awtkam, ɛn udat go wit yu

Ɛnitin we yu rɛkɔd ya de go ɔnda di **fɛloship** as in kɔntɛks, so dat i de stil tay to di grup usay i apin.

Ɛni fɔlo ɔp kin gɛt flag as **wɛlfɛa kɔnsɛn** ɔ **sefgadin kɔnsɛn**, sɛpret-sɛpret, we de sɛn am to di sem tab insay di [Kɔnsɛn](/kri/platform/concerns) inbɔks fɔ udat de rid am. Yuz di flag: sefgadin mata we yu rayt insay di not nɔmɔ fɔ fɔlo ɔp we nɔ gɛt flag nɔ de rich nɔbɔdi.

Di ko-lida de si di fɔlo ɔp not dɛn bak, so dɛn tu kin kɔva di pastɔral wok. Lida dɛn de gɛt alat we mɛmba nɔ gɛt fɔlo ɔp insay di tɛm we dɛn sɛt (difɔlt: 7 de).

Si [Fɔlo ɔp dɛn](/kri/platform/follow-ups) fɔ di ful pikchɔ, wit di branch-lɛvul kyu dɛn.

## Fɔ rɛkɔd mitin atɛndans

Frɔm di fɛloship pej:

1. Mek wan mitin rɛkɔd (de, tɔpik, ples)
2. Opin di mitin ɛn mak ɛvri mɛmba: **De ya**, **Nɔ de ya**, **Ɛkskyuz**, ɔ **Let**
3. Ad mitin not ɔ di tɔpik dɛn we una tɔk

## Fɔ diaktivet fɛloship

Pasto ɛn Admin dɛn kin diaktivet fɛloship wit di **Diaktivet** bɔtin pan di fɛloship kad. Ɔl di mitin rɛkɔd, atɛndans istri, ɛn fɔlo ɔp not de stil de.

## Map vyu

Ɔl di fɛloship de pan di map. Mɛmba dɛn kin sach bay postkod ɔ kɔntri fɔ fɛn di fɛloship we nia dɛn, wit di tɛm fɔ waka/bɔs/kar/trenj. Si [Fɛloship dɛn](/kri/platform/fellowships) fɔ di ditel.
