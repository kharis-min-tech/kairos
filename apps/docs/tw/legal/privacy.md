# Kokoamsɛm Nkra

**Ɛfiri aseɛ 23 Kutawonsa 2026 · Nsakraeɛ 2026-07-v1**

::: tip Nkyerɛaseɛ a ɛboa wo
<!-- i18n-allow-en -->
Yɛyɛɛ nkyerɛaseɛ yi sɛdeɛ ɛbɛboa wo. Sɛ nsonsonoeɛ ba a, [Borɔfo nkyerɛwee](/legal/privacy) no na ɛdi tumi.
:::

Nkra yi kyerɛ sɛdeɛ Kharis Church ("yɛn", "asɔre no") gye na ɔde w'ankasa nsɛm di dwuma bere a wode Kairos, yɛn asɔre nhyehyɛeɛ no, di dwuma. Yɛtwerɛɛ no sɛdeɛ ɛbɛdi UK Nsɛm Banbɔ Mmara (UK GDPR) ne Data Protection Act 2018 akyi.

Kharis Church na ɔhwɛ nsɛm a Kairos di ho dwuma so. Sɛ wowɔ nsɛmmisa biara a ɛfa nkra yi ho, anaa wopɛ sɛ wodi hokwan biara a ɛwɔ aseɛ ho dwuma a, frɛ yɛn wɔ [privacy@kharis.org](mailto:privacy@kharis.org).

## 1. Wɔn a nkra yi fa wɔn ho

Nkra yi fa Kharis Church mufoɔ a wɔbue akawnt wɔ Kairos mu ho, nnipa a yɛn akannifoɔ de wɔn nsɛm hyɛ Kairos mu (sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ahɔhoɔ foforɔ a wɔgyee wɔn wɔ ahɔhoɔgyeeɛ krataa so, anaa mmɔfra a wɔn awofoɔ de wɔn nsɛm ba) ho, ne obiara a ɔde nsɛm ba denam Kairos krataa a obiara tumi hyɛ so.

## 2. W'ankasa nsɛm a yɛgye

### 2.1 Nsɛm a wode ma yɛn bere a worekyerɛw wo din

- Din nyinaa (deɛ ɛdi kan, mfimfini, abusua din) ne dibea din biara a wopɛ
- Imel akyerɛwdeɛ ne telefon nɔma
- Ɛda a wɔwoo wo ne sɛ woyɛ ɔbaa anaa ɔbarima
- Efie akyerɛwdeɛ ne (sɛ wopɛ a) baabi foforɔ
- Kharis Church asɔre baa a wokɔ
- Ntɛmpɛ nkitahodie din, ne abusuabɔ, ne telefon nɔma
- Nsɛmfua a wode bue (yɛkora so sɛ ɔkwan baako kriptografik nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ nko ara)

### 2.2 Nsɛm a wobɛtumi de aka ho akyire

- Nkrataa mfonini
- Awadeɛ tebea, adwuma, asɔre a na wokɔ kane
- Nkra deɛ wopɛ

### 2.3 Nsɛm a w'asɔre mu adwuma yɛ

- Ɔsom ne fekuo mmaeɛ nkrataa
- Fekuo mu tenaeɛ ne akannidie dibea biara a wokura
- Adwumakuo mu tenaeɛ, nnidisoɔ adwuma, ne ntadeɛ kɛseɛ
- Gyidini foforɔ asuafoyɛ ɔkwan mu beaeɛ, sɛ ɛfa wo ho a, ne nhyiamu nsɛm a w'ɔkwankyerɛfoɔ twerɛeɛ
- Nkrataa a wode ba (sɛ ɛbɛyɛ: asubɔ abisadeɛ, adansedie, afɔrebukyia frɛ mmuaeɛ, abɔfra kyerɛ, nkyerɛkyerɛmu nkrataa)
- Mpaeɛbɔ abisadeɛ, nkɔdi akyi nsɛm, anaa ahobammɔ nsɛm a kannifoɔ kyerɛwee wɔ ɔhwɛ ho
- Nkrataa a ɛkyerɛ ɔpene a woama anaa woagye, ne nkra yi ne yɛn Nhyehyɛeɛ ne Mmara nsakraeɛ a woapene so

### 2.4 Mfidie ho nsɛm

- Bere a wobueeɛ, bere nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ, ne browser a wode diiɛ
- IP akyerɛwdeɛ ne mfidie ho nsɛm kakra, a yɛde bɔ w'akawnt ho ban firi wɔn a wɔnni ho kwan ho nko ara
- Mfomsoɔ nkaeɛbɔ a ɛyɛ ne ho ara sɛ Kairos fa bi yɛ basaa bere a wode redi dwuma

### 2.5 Nsɛm soronko ne ahobammɔ nsɛm

Bere a krataa bisa wo w'apɔwmuden, dɛm, abɔfra a wohwɛ ne so, anaa ahobammɔ asɛm ho nsɛm no, yɛde ɔhwɛ soronko di saa nsɛm no ho dwuma. Yɛgye bere a ɛho hia ma asɔre no ɔhwɛ ne ahobammɔ asɛyɛdeɛ nko ara, na akannifoɔ a wɔwɔ ntease pa a enti wɔbɛhwɛ nko ara na wɔtumi.

## 3. Deɛ enti a yɛde wo nsɛm di dwuma, ne yɛn mmara nnyinasoɔ

Wɔ UK GDPR ase no, yɛntumi nni wo nsɛm ho dwuma gye sɛ yɛwɔ mmara nnyinasoɔ. Yɛgyina yeinom so:

| Botaeɛ | Mmara nnyinasoɔ |
|---------|-------------|
| Asɔremufoɔ nhwɛsoɔ, sɛdeɛ wobɛtumi abue, ne wo ne yɛn ntam nkitahodie a ɛfa ɔsom ne fekuo a wowɔ mu ho | Asɔre no anigyeɛ pa sɛ ɔhwɛ ne mufoɔ |
| Mmaeɛ nkyerɛwee, asuafoyɛ nkɔsoɔ, ne nnidisoɔ nhyehyɛeɛ | Asɔre no anigyeɛ pa sɛ ɔhwɛ ɔsom ne asomdwuma so |
| Ɔhwɛ, mpaeɛbɔ nkɔdi akyi, ne wɔn a wɔyɛ mmerɛ (mmɔfra a wɔnnii mfeɛ 16 ka ho) ahobammɔ | Anigyeɛ pa, ne yɛn mmara ase asɛyɛdeɛ wɔ ahobammɔ mmara mu |
| Nkrataa, dwumadie nsa a yɛfrɛ wo, anaa asɛmpatrɛw nkra a ɛho nhia | W'ɔpene pefee |
| Kairos ho banbɔ firi asisie ne mmara buo ho, ne ɔhwɛ nkrataa a yɛkora | Anigyeɛ pa sɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no bɛyɛ dwoodwoo |

Yɛmfa wo nsɛm nyɛ gyinaesie a mfidie yɛ a ɛka wo kɛseɛ, na yɛntɔn wo nsɛm mma obiara.

## 4. Wɔn a yɛkyɛ wo nsɛm ma

Wo nsɛm da adi wɔ Kharis Church mu ma akannifoɔ a wɔn dibea hwehwɛ saa. Akwannya no bata hɔ sɛdeɛ kannifoɔ hunu nnipa ne mmeaeɛ a ɛda ne so no nsɛm nko ara.

Yɛkyɛ nsɛm ma afoforɔ yi a wɔboa yɛn ma yɛhwɛ Kairos so:

- **Cloudflare, Inc.**: ɔhwɛ Kairos wɛbsaet, app sɛɛva, nkrataakoraeɛ ɔpon, ne mfonini sieeɛ so
- **PlanetScale, Inc.**: ɔhwɛ Postgres nkrataakoraeɛ a ɛkora wo nkrataa so. Wɔkora nsɛm no wɔ UK / EEA
- **Amazon Web Services, Inc.**: ɔsoma imel. Beaeɛ: EU (London / Frankfurt)
- **Sentry, Inc.**: ɔnya mfomsoɔ nkaeɛbɔ bere a Kairos yɛ basaa, sɛdeɛ yɛbɛhunu na yɛasiesie

Saa aboafoɔ yi di nsɛm no ho dwuma sɛ adwumayɛfoɔ: yɛn akwankyerɛ ase nko ara na wɔdi wo nsɛm ho dwuma, na apam kyekyere wɔn sɛ wɔmmɔ ho ban.

Yɛbɛtumi nso ada nsɛm adi baabi a mmara hwehwɛ saa, sɛ ɛbɛyɛ wɔ ahobammɔ nhwehwɛmu, asɛnniiɛ ahyɛdeɛ, anaa UK ɔhwɛfoɔ abisadeɛ ho.

## 5. Bere a yɛkora wo nsɛm so

| Nsɛm su | Bere a yɛkora so |
|-----------|-------------|
| Asɔremufoɔ nkrataa a ɛrekɔ so | Mmere dodoɔ a wobɛyɛ muni |
| Asɔremufoɔ nkrataa a yɛasie | Kɔsi mfeɛ 6 w'akyire |
| Mmɔfra a wɔnnii mfeɛ 16 nkrataa | Sɛdeɛ yɛn ahobammɔ nhyehyɛeɛ kyerɛ |
| Mmaeɛ nkrataa | Mfeɛ 3, a ɛkɔ so |
| Ɔpene nkrataa | Akawnt no nkwa ne mfeɛ 6 |
| Bue nkrataa ne mfomsoɔ nkaeɛbɔ | Kɔsi nna 90, afei ɛyi ne ho ara |

## 6. Wo hokwan

Wɔ UK GDPR ase no, wowɔ hokwan sɛ:

- **Wohunu** w'ankasa nsɛm ("nsɛm hwɛ abisadeɛ")
- **Wosiesie** nsɛm a ɛnteɛ anaa ɛnni mu
- **Woyi** wo nsɛm ("hokwan sɛ wɔn werɛ mfiri wo"), gye baabi a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛkora so ɛsiane ahobammɔ anaa mmara nti
- **Woto** sɛdeɛ yɛdi wo nsɛm ho dwuma ano bere a yɛresiesie asɛm bi
- **Nsɛm a wode kɔ**: wonya nsɛm a wode maa yɛn no wɔ ɔkwan a mfidie tumi kenkan so
- **Wotia** adwuma a ɛgyina yɛn anigyeɛ pa so
- **Woyi w'ɔpene** bere biara, baabi a yɛgyinaa ɔpene so

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wodi hokwan yi mu biara ho dwuma a, twerɛ yɛn wɔ [privacy@kharis.org](mailto:privacy@kharis.org). Yɛbɛbua wɔ bosome baako mu.

Sɛ wogye di sɛ yɛanni wo nsɛm ho dwuma sɛdeɛ mmara kyerɛ a, wotumi de nkurobɔ kɔ UK Information Commissioner's Office hɔ wɔ [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk). Yɛbɛsrɛ wo sɛ ka kyerɛ yɛn kane sɛdeɛ yɛbɛtumi asiesie.

## 7. Mmɔfra ne mmabunu

Kharis Church bu mmɔfra ahobammɔ sɛ adeɛ a ɛho hia. Sɛ wonnii mfeɛ 16 a, srɛ w'awofoɔ anaa wo hwɛfoɔ sɛ wɔnhyɛ Kairos nkrataa mma wo. Awofoɔ anaa ahobammɔ kannifoɔ a wɔapaw no na ɔyɛ na ɔhwɛ mmɔfra nkrataa so, na akannifoɔ a wɔn dibea hwehwɛ saa nko ara na wɔhunu wɔ Kairos mu.

## 8. Kuki ne akyiridie

Kairos de kuki baako pɛ a ɛyɛ yɛn ankasa dea di dwuma sɛdeɛ wobɛtena mu bere a wode redi dwuma. Yɛmfa dawubɔ kuki nni dwuma, yɛnnyɛ afoforɔ nhwɛmu a ɛdi w'akyi wɔ intanɛt so, na yɛnkyɛ sɛdeɛ wode di dwuma mma dawubɔ mmeaeɛ.

## 9. Banbɔ

Yɛkora nsɛmfua sɛ ɔkwan baako kriptografik nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ na obiara ntumi nkenkan, yɛn mpo. TLS bɔ nsɛm a ɛretu kwan ho ban. Dibea so akwannya na ɛbɔ Kairos nhwɛsoɔ ho ban, na yɛkora nkrataa wɔ so.

## 10. Nkra yi mu nsakraeɛ

Sɛ yɛsesa biribi kɛseɛ wɔ nkra yi mu a, yɛbɛma ne nsakraeɛ dodoɔ akɔ soro na yɛabisa wo sɛ kenkan na pene so bere a wobɛbue bio. Nkyerɛwee mu mfomsoɔ nketewa deɛ, yɛbɛtumi asiesie a yɛmmisa.
