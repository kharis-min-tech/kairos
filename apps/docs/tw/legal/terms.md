# Nhyehyɛeɛ ne Mmara

**Ɛfiri aseɛ 23 Kutawonsa 2026 · Nsakraeɛ 2026-07-v1**

::: tip Nkyerɛaseɛ a ɛboa wo
<!-- i18n-allow-en -->
Yɛyɛɛ nkyerɛaseɛ yi sɛdeɛ ɛbɛboa wo. Sɛ nsonsonoeɛ ba a, [Borɔfo nkyerɛwee](/legal/terms) no na ɛdi tumi.
:::

Mmara yi hwɛ sɛdeɛ wode Kairos, Kharis Church ("yɛn", "asɔre no") asɔre nhyehyɛeɛ no, di dwuma so. Sɛ wobue akawnt anaa wode Kairos di dwuma a, wopene so sɛ wobɛdi so. Sɛ wompene so a, mfa Kairos nni dwuma.

England ne Wales mmara na ɛhwɛ mmara yi so. Asɛm biara a ɛfiri mu bɛba no, England ne Wales asɛnniiɛ nko ara na wɔbɛdi.

## 1. W'akawnt

Kairos akawnt wɔ hɔ ma Kharis Church mufoɔ ankorankoro. Ɛsɛ sɛ woma nokwarɛ nsɛm bere a worekyerɛw wo din, na woma ɛyɛ foforɔ wɔ wo nkrataa so.

Wo na ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ wo nsɛmfua so sɛ ɛnyɛ kokoamsɛm. Mfa w'akawnt mma obi foforɔ. Sɛ wosusu sɛ obi foforɔ akɔ w'akawnt mu a, ka kyerɛ yɛn ntɛm ara.

Ɛsɛ sɛ wodi mfeɛ 16 anaa deɛ ɛboro saa ansa na woabue w'ankasa Kairos akawnt. Sɛ wosua kyɛn saa a, ɛsɛ sɛ w'awofoɔ anaa wo hwɛfoɔ hyɛ Kairos nkrataa ma wo.

## 2. Dwumadie a ɛfata

Wopene so sɛ wode Kairos bɛdi dwuma wɔ asɔre ho nneɛma pa nko ara ho. Titiriw, ɛnsɛ sɛ:

- Wode Kairos soma nkrasɛm a wɔmpɛ, haw ɔmanfoɔ biara, anaa woma nsɛm a ɛtia mmara, ɛyɛ atirimuɔden, ɛpira, anaa ɛyɛ nyiyimu da adi
- Wobɔ mmɔden sɛ wobɛkɔ akawnt, nkrataa, anaa Kairos afa a wɔmmaa wo kwan so
- Wosɛe sɛdeɛ Kairos yɛ adwuma, wobɔ mmɔden sɛ wobɛtwa ne banbɔ ho, anaa wode mfidie a ɛtwe nsɛm di dwuma tia no
- Wokopi, wokyɛ, anaa wode nsɛm a wohunu ɛfiri sɛ woyɛ Kharis Church muni nko ara yɛ dwa (sɛ ɛbɛyɛ, asɔremufoɔ afoforɔ nkitahodie nsɛm)
- Wokyerɛ sɛ woyɛ obi foforɔ anaa wode obi foforɔ ho nsɛm ba a wammma wo kwan

## 3. Akannifoɔ ne sohwɛfoɔ

Fekuo akannifoɔ, adwumakuo akannifoɔ, ne asɔre baa sohwɛfoɔ wɔ asɛyɛdeɛ foforɔ wɔ Kairos mu. Sɛ wokura dibea yi mu baako a, wopene so sɛ:

- Wode asɔremufoɔ nsɛm bɛdi dwuma wɔ asɔre no ɔhwɛ, ahobammɔ, ne nhyehyɛeɛ nko ara ho
- Wobɛdi Kharis Church ahobammɔ nhyehyɛeɛ akyi bere a worekyerɛw, woresoma, anaa wokyɛ ahobammɔ nsɛm
- Wobɛkɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ biara a asɔre no hwehwɛ firi wɔn a wɔkura dibea hɔ ansa na woadi tumi kɛseɛ ho dwuma

Asɔre baa sohwɛfoɔ anaa Kharis Church akannifoɔ tumi gye akannidie akwannya bere biara.

## 4. Wo nsɛm ne wo kokoamsɛm

Yɛn [Kokoamsɛm Nkra](/tw/legal/privacy) kyerɛ w'ankasa nsɛm a yɛgye, deɛ enti a yɛgye, wɔn a yɛkyɛ ma, bere a yɛkora so, ne sɛdeɛ wobɛtumi adi wo hokwan ho dwuma. Sɛ wode Kairos di dwuma a, wokyerɛ sɛ woakenkan Kokoamsɛm Nkra no.

## 5. Nsɛm a wode ba

Kairos ma wode wo ho nsɛm ba, na ɛtɔ da bi, w'abusuani anaa ɔhɔhoɔ foforɔ bi deɛ (sɛ wɔama wo kwan a). Wokyerɛ sɛ nsɛm biara a wode ba no yɛ nokorɛ sɛdeɛ wonim, na sɛ nnipa foforɔ nsɛm ka ho a, woanya wɔn ɔpene.

Kharis Church tumi de nsɛm a wode ba di dwuma wɔ ɔhwɛ, ahobammɔ, ne nhyehyɛeɛ adwuma a ɛwɔ Kokoamsɛm Nkra no mu ho. Nsɛm a wode ba no da so yɛ wo dea; woma asɔre no kwan sɛ ɔmfa nni dwuma wɔ saa nneɛma no ho.

## 6. Sɛ ɛwɔ hɔ

Yɛyɛ adwuma sɛ Kairos bɛwɔ hɔ na ayɛ nokwafoɔ, nanso yɛnhyɛ bɔ sɛ ɛrentwa mu da anaa mfomsoɔ biara renni mu. Ɛtɔ da bi a, ɛsɛ sɛ yɛyi Kairos firi intanɛt so ma nsiesieeɛ, na sɛ afoforɔ nhyehyɛeɛ (sɛ ɛbɛyɛ yɛn hɔho anaa imel ɔboafoɔ) yɛ basaa a, ɛbɛtumi aka no. Yɛnnyɛ asodie mma nneɛma a ɛyera ɛsiane bere tiawa a ɛnni hɔ nti.

## 7. Ɔsi ne awieeɛ

Wotumi to w'akawnt mu bere biara sɛ wofrɛ kannifoɔ anaa wotwerɛ [privacy@kharis.org](mailto:privacy@kharis.org).

Yɛbɛtumi asi w'akawnt kwan anaa yɛato mu sɛ wobu mmara yi so kɛseɛ, sɛ wofiri asɔre no mu, anaa sɛ sɛ wokɔ so a, ɛbɛyɛ ahobammɔ asiane. Sɛ yɛbɛtumi a, yɛbɛkyerɛkyerɛ ntease no mu.

Sɛ w'akawnt to mu a, yɛkora nsɛm so na yɛyi firi hɔ sɛdeɛ Kokoamsɛm Nkra no kyerɛ.

## 8. Adwene mu agyapadeɛ

Kairos nhyehyɛeɛ no, ne nsiesieeɛ, ne ne koodu yɛ Kharis Church anaa wɔn a wɔmaa no kwan no dea. W'akawnt ma wo hokwan a ɛyɛ wo nko dea a wontumi mfa mma obi, sɛ wode Kairos di dwuma bere a woyɛ muni; ɛmfa agyapadeɛ biara mma wo.

## 9. Bɔhyɛ ne asodie

Wɔde Kairos ma "sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ" ne "sɛdeɛ ɛwɔ hɔ". Sɛdeɛ mmara ma kwan kɔsi deɛ ɛwieeɛ no, Kharis Church yi bɔhyɛ a wɔnkaa no pefee nyinaa firi mu. Biribiara a ɛwɔ mmara yi mu nto yɛn asodie ano wɔ owuo anaa onipadua pira a adwenemherɛ de baeɛ ho, asisie ho, anaa asodie biara a Borɔfo mmara mma kwan sɛ wɔyi firi mu.

Sɛ ɛno akyi a, yɛn asodie nyinaa a ɛfiri Kairos a wode di dwuma mu ba no to so wɔ sika biara a woatua yɛn wɔ Kairos ho wɔ abosome dumienu a ɛdi asɛm no anim (a ma asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ no ara ɛbɛyɛ hwee).

## 10. Mmara yi mu nsakraeɛ

Sɛ yɛsesa biribi kɛseɛ wɔ mmara yi mu a, yɛbɛma ne nsakraeɛ dodoɔ akɔ soro na yɛabisa wo sɛ kenkan na pene so bere a wobɛbue bio. Nkyerɛwee mu mfomsoɔ nketewa deɛ, yɛbɛtumi asiesie a yɛmmisa. Sɛ wompene nsakraeɛ kɛseɛ so a, wotumi to w'akawnt mu.
