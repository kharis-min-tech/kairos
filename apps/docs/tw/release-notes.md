# Nsakraeɛ Nkrataa

## Ɔbɛnem 2026

### Akwannya

- **Wɔtetee mmoa firii ahobammɔ ho.** Mmoa nsɛm a wokenkan no yɛ n'ankasa akwannya seesei. Kane no na ɛgyina asɔre baa akwannya a ɛda hɔ so, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ Asɔre Baa Nkrataa Sohwɛfoɔ — nkrataa dibea a ɔhwɛ ho tumi biara nni mu — tumi kenkan mmoa ne ahobammɔ nkrataa beaeɛ nyinaa. Hwɛ [Tumi Nhwɛmu](/tw/administration/capabilities#deɛ-enti-a-mmoa-ne-ahobammɔ-da-nsow).
- **Ahobammɔ Panin**, asɔre no nyinaa tumi a ɛkenkan asɔre baa biara ahobammɔ nhwɛmu. Ɔkwan a wɔfa so kɔ soro sene asɔre baa Ahobammɔ Kannifoɔ, bere a ɛsɛ sɛ asɛm bi firi asɔre baa a ɛsɔree wɔ mu no mu.
- **Ahobammɔ ani di Sɔfopanin dibea akyi seesei.** Obiara a ɔwɔ Sɔfopanin dibea mu wɔ asɔre baa bi mu no hunu saa asɔre baa no ahobammɔ nhwɛmu ne ho ara. Ɔsɔfoɔ a ɔkura asɔre baa so tumi korɔ no ara nhunu. Saa tumi yi firi akannifoɔ nkrataa mu, na wontumi mfa mma.
- **"Ɔpanin" adane "Ɔsɔfoɔ"** wɔ asɔre baa akannidie mu.
- Yɛasiesie: akawnt bue nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ foforɔ na ɛreba a akwannya nhwɛmu nni mu, a ɛyii bɔtin ne nkratafa a akwannya bɔ ho ban nyinaa komm kɔsi sɛ onipa no bɛfiri mu asan abue.

### Nkɔdi akyi ne nsɛm a ɛhaw

- **Nkɔdi akyi ka ɔno ankasa tebea seesei**: fekuo, adwumakuo, anaa asɔre baa no ara. Asɔre baa no kyerɛ sɛ **obi a ɔnni kuo biara mu tumi nya nkɔdi akyi seesei**, a na ɛntumi nyɛ yie kane: na ɛsɛ sɛ nkɔdi akyi bata kuo bi ho, enti nnipa a na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔn nsa ka nkitahodie paa no, wɔn na nhyehyɛeɛ no ntumi nkyerɛw wɔn ho hwee.
- **Nkɔdi Akyi kratafa foforɔ** a nhwɛmu mmiɛnsa wɔ mu: **Ahɔhoɔ foforɔ** (wɔfaa ahɔhoɔgyeeɛ krataa so wɔ nna 60 a atwam mu, obiara ne wɔn nkasaeɛ), **Kuo biara nni hɔ** (wɔnni fekuo anaa adwumakuo mu), ne **Ɛduru so** (nkitahodie ɛda a ɛdi so a aduru).
- **Nsɛm a Ɛhaw nkrataa beaeɛ foforɔ** a mmoa ne ahobammɔ tab soronko wɔ mu, na emu biara da adi ma obi a ɔtumi kenkan nko ara.
- **Nkɔdi Akyi Kuo** a wɔde kaa adwumakuo su ho, a ɛda nsow firi Ahɔhoɔ Gyee Kuo a wɔgye nnipa ɛda no ara ho.

### Dashbɔd

- **Wɔsan sii nhwɛsoɔ fa no.** Nnipa su nson a na ɛhyɛ URL baako ase no bɛyɛɛ blɔk fa baako, a tumi a wowɔ na ɛkyerɛ deɛ wohunu. Deɛ ɛsiesieeɛ bi ne sɛ, na fekuo kannifoɔ hunu asɔre baa no nyinaa dodoɔ, ɛfiri sɛ tete nhyehyɛeɛ no de no tɔɔ asɔre baa su no mu.
- **Nkwa kɔkɔbɔ**: nkrataa a obiara annye, asɔremufoɔ a wɔrefiri mu, asɔre mmaa a wɔreka akyi, ne asɔre mmaa a Sɔfopanin nni mu, emu biara yɛ nkitahodie a ɛde wo kɔ baabi a wobɛsiesie.
- **Ɛhia wo** boaboa deɛ ɛretwɛn wo ankasa ano. Kane no na ɔpene yɛ dodoɔ a ɛwɔ kaad so a ɛsɛ sɛ wohunu.
- **Dwumadie a ɛtwa toɔ** yɛ nsɛm a ɛfiri mmeaeɛ enum ankasa seesei: dinkyerɛw, nkrataa, asɔremufoɔ awieeɛ, fekuo nhyiamu, ne akra a wɔnyaa wɔn.
- Nkyekyɛmu afa tumi bobɔ, na deɛ wo kratafa wɔ mu no bue ne ho ara.

### Kasa ahodoɔ

- **Kairos ka Borɔfo, Frɛnkye, Krio ne Twi.** Wɛb app, mobile app, ne nwoma yi nyinaa di deɛ woyi no akyi, enti sɛ wobue mmoa akwankyerɛ bere a Kairos wɔ Twi mu a, nwoma no bue wɔ Twi mu.
- **Nwoma no kratafa biara wɔ kasa nnan no nyinaa mu**, a ɛwɔ n'ankasa mfonini ne n'ankasa video tiawa.
- **Video tiawa wɔ afa biara so**, a ɛnnuru simma aduasa, ne akwantuo kɛseɛ ne akwantuo baako ma dibea biara. Krio ne Twi video wɔ nsɛm a wɔatwerɛ na ɛnyɛ nne, ɛfiri sɛ kasa mmienu no mu biara nni nne a wɔyɛ no nnɛ.
- Wɔdane Atwerɛsɛm nsɛm no nwoma din nko ara. Wɔmfa mfidie ntwerɛ nkyekyɛmu no mu nsɛm da.

### Nkaeɛ

- Kuo akannifoɔ hunu wɔn ankasa nkurɔfoɔ Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ ne asɔremufoɔ adesua nkɔsoɔ seesei, a ɛgyina wɔn a wɔwɔ wɔn kuo mu so na ɛnnyina asɔre baa so.
- Yi-anaa-twerɛ ɔmanfoɔ nkyerɛ wɔ ahɔhoɔ foforɔ hwɛfoɔ beaeɛ ne abɔfra nkrataa awofoɔ beaeɛ mmienu nyinaa so.
- Wɔsan yɛɛ akawnt bue kratafa no foforɔ.

## Ɛbɔ 2026

### Asɔremufoɔ adesua

Afa no nyinaa yɛ foforɔ. Hwɛ [Asɔremufoɔ Adesua](/tw/platform/membership).

- Nnawɔtwe nnan adesua no, ne akuo, nhyiamu, nhyiamu biara nkrataa, efie adwuma ne nsɔhwɛ nsɛmmu, nsɔhwɛ a ɛtwa toɔ, ne ahyɛdeɛ.
- **Wontumi mfa wo ho nhyɛ mu.** Kyerɛ sɛ w'ani gye ho, ka nhwɛmu no ho, na asɔremufoɔ sohwɛfoɔ de wo bɛhyɛ kuo bi mu. Nhwɛmu mu nkrataa di nna 180.
- **Asɔremufoɔ Sohwɛfoɔ**, asɔre no nyinaa tumi, ɛfiri sɛ akuo no gye nnipa firi asɔre mmaa nyinaa so na asɔre baa so tumi biara ntumi nkyerɛ tumi wɔ bi so.
- **Asɔremufoɔ Ɔboafoɔ**, asɔre baa so kenkan-nko tumi: ɔhunu baabi hɔ twɛn nhwɛmu ne wɔn a wɔagye wɔn, ne saa asɔre baa no asɔremufoɔ nkra.
- **Awieeɛ ɔpon** a ahwehwɛdeɛ nsia wɔ mu, sohwɛfoɔ twa-mu-kwan a ɛhwehwɛ ntease a wɔatwerɛ, ne kuo biara nkonimdie dodoɔ sɛdeɛ mprɛ bi bɛtumi asi ne deɛ.
- Awieeɛ kyerɛw ɔmanfoɔ no nkrataa so, na ɛno na ɛma **Asɔremufoɔ ankasa** dodoɔ a ɛwɔ nkaeɛbɔ mu no kyerɛ biribi pɔtee.
- Nkra nnan a ɛkɔ asɔre baa no Sɔfopanin ne Asɔremufoɔ Aboafoɔ nkyɛn: ɔkaa twɛn nhwɛmu ho, wɔgyee no, ɔwieeɛ, ɔyii ne ho firii mu anaa n'anigyeɛ twam.

### Nkaeɛ

- Akawnt bue bere a ɛware, sɛdeɛ nipadua nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ a wode bue wɔ mobile so bɛtena hɔ wɔ nsɛmfua bue ntam.
- Akyerɛwdeɛ nsusuiɛ nhwɛmu baako a wɛb ne mobile nyinaa fa di dwuma.

## Ɔsannaa 2026

### Akawnt bue

- **Fa Google, Microsoft, anaa Apple bue**, a imel ne nsɛmfua ka ho. Akwan no nyinaa ba akawnt korɔ no ara so, enti obi a ɔde nsɛmfua kyerɛwee ne din tumi de Google bue akyire na ɔda so yɛ onipa korɔ no ara a n'abakɔsɛm nsesaeɛ.
- Mfitiaseɛ ɔpon ma akawnt foforɔ a wɔde ɔkwan baako bue, ne banbɔ imel bere biara a wɔde beaeɛ bi bata akawnt a ɛwɔ hɔ ho.

### Mmaeɛ

- **Kyerɛw wo din ankasa.** Asɔremufoɔ tumi kyerɛw wɔn ho wɔ ɔsom ase: mia baako, anaa kenkan QR kod a ɛwɔ ahɔhoɔgyeeɛ pon skrin so a ɛsesa simma 30 biara, sɛdeɛ Kwasida a ɛtwaam mfonini renyɛ adwuma.
- Asɔre baa biara hwɛ ne kyerɛw-wo-din bere ne akyire bere so.
- **Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ**, nhwɛmu foforɔ a emu da hɔ: ɔmanfoɔ biara mfonini wɔ nnawɔtwe 13 mu ne ɔyeraeɛ nnidisoɔ dodoɔ, mmaeɛ mpɛn akuo firi Dapɛn biara kɔsi Ɔdaeɛ, ahɔhoɔ foforɔ ne wɔn a wɔsan ba nkyekyɛmu, ne **ahokekafoɔ dodoɔ a wɔde kyekyɛ** a wotumi yi abosome 3, 6, anaa 12. Hwɛ [Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ](/tw/analytics/attendance-reports).

### Nkɔdi akyi

- **Yɛsan yɛɛ nkɔdi akyi sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ ankasa**: Nkitahodie anaa Nsrahwɛ, a akwan pii nkitahodie ka ho (mmɔden baako a ɛfa frɛ, nkrasɛm, ne WhatsApp so yɛ nkrataa baako na ɛnyɛ mmiɛnsa), nsrahwɛ su, sɛ wɔbɔɔ ho amaneɛ anaa, bere a wobaeɛ ne bere a wofiriiɛ, deɛ ɛsiiɛ, ne wɔn a wɔka wo ho kɔeɛ.
- **Mmoa** ne **ahobammɔ** agyiraeɛ a emu biara ne ne ho, wɔ nkɔdi akyi biara so.
- Nkɔdi akyi abakɔsɛm wɔ ɔmanfoɔ nkrataa so, sɛ bere nnidisoɔ baako a ɛfa tebea biara so.

### Asase mfonini ne akyerɛwdeɛ

- **Mapbox** bɛyɛɛ asase mfonini ne akyerɛwdeɛ ɔboafoɔ baako pɛ wɔ wɛb ne mobile nyinaa so.
- Akyerɛwdeɛ ɔboafoɔ a wɔde bataa dinkyerɛw, asɔremufoɔ, asɔre mmaa, fekuo, ne nkrataa ho.
- Fekuo ne asɔre mmaa kora wɔn ankasa akyerɛwdeɛ ne wɔn beaeɛ seesei.

### Nkrataa

- Nkrataa adwuma yi baa intanɛt so wɔ **docs.kairos.kharis.org**, ne **Mmoa ne Akwankyerɛ** mmeaeɛ wɔ app nkyekyɛmu mu, kratafa a ɛdi kan so, ne ahɔhoɔgyeeɛ skrin so.

## Kutawonsa 2026

### Deɛ ɛdi kan

- **Akawnt bue**: dinkyerɛw, bue, nsɛmfua foforɔ, ɔpene nhyehyɛeɛ, nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ a ɛkura tumi
- **Asɔre Mmaa ne Amantam**: nhwɛsoɔ nyinaa, yidie brɛoo, ne akannidie abakɔsɛm
- **Asɔremufoɔ**: nhwɛmu, nkrataa, CSV a wɔde ba ne deɛ wɔyi, ɔpene nhwɛmu, ahobammɔ nhwɛ
- **Fekuo**: fekuo su nyinaa, asase mfonini a postkod ne ɔman ɔhwehwɛ ne ɔkwantuo bere wɔ mu, nhyiamu mmaeɛ, nkɔdi akyi
- **Adwumakuo**: asomdwuma su nyinaa, mufoɔ nhwɛsoɔ, abisadeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ, nkɔdi akyi, adwumayɛfoɔ gyeɛ ɔkwan (Abisadeɛ → Nsɛmmisa → Gyinabea Mma → Nsɔhwɛ bere → Muni), nnidisoɔ nhwɛsoɔ, ntadeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ
- **Mmaeɛ**: ɔsom mmaeɛ nkyerɛwee ne dinkyerɛw, Me Mmaeɛ a nnawɔtwe 12 abakɔsɛm ne nnidisoɔ wɔ mu
- **Asɛmpatrɛw Nhyehyɛeɛ**: nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔyɛ, adwumayɛfoɔ dinkyerɛw, akra gyeɛ
- **Akra Ɔkwan**: Kanban bɔd (Foforɔ → Yɛredi Akyi → N'ani Gye Ho → Wasakra), nkɔdi akyi nkyerɛwee, ɛmu-yɛ-den/hwɛ-so/ɛrekɔ-yie nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ, CSV yidie
- **Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ**: nhyiamu nnan asuafoyɛ ɔkwan, nhyiamu nhyehyɛeɛ, mmaeɛ nkyerɛwee, agyinaeɛ kɔkɔbɔ
- **Nkrataa ne Nsɛm Gyeɛ**: Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ, Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ Adesua, Asubɔ, Adansedie, Abɔfra Din Tuo, Abɔfra Kyerɛ; nkrataa a wɔde baeɛ nhwɛmu a nyiyimu ne CSV yidie wɔ mu; ahɔhoɔ a wɔada adwinnadeɛ
- **Akra Dashbɔd**: ɔkwan tebea mfonini, akra ne wɔn tebea, nkɔdi akyi apɔwmuden, nsakraeɛ kwan, afa mu nkɔsoɔ dodoɔ, bere ne nhyehyɛeɛ nyiyimu
- **Nkaeɛbɔ ne Nhwɛmu**: asɔremufoɔ nkɔsoɔ, dapɛn mmaeɛ dodoɔ, asɛmpatrɛw dodoɔ, fekuo a nnipa kɔ mu pii
- **Nkrataa**: onipa ankasa nsɛm, kuo mu tenaeɛ, akannidie, ntɛmpɛ nkitahodie
- **Nhyehyɛeɛ**: nsɛmfua nsakraeɛ, imel nsakraeɛ, bue a ɛtwa toɔ, tebea, kasa, imel deɛ wopɛ, mmara ne ɔpene

## Deɛ aba firi saa bere no, a ɛsɛ sɛ wohunu

- **Yi me nsɛm** ne **Yi m'akawnt** reyɛ adwuma wɔ **Nhyehyɛeɛ → Kokoamsɛm ne Nsɛm** mu. Na wɔaka wɔ deɛ ɛdi kan no mu sɛ ɛreba.
- **Nkrataa mfonini a wode kɔ** yɛ adwuma firi ahyɛaseɛ kɔsi awieeɛ.
- **Imel a ɛkɔ ankasa** kɔ ankasa, denam Amazon SES so. Deɛ ɛdi kan no kaa sɛ nsɛmfua foforɔ ne ɔpene imel pue wɔ API mmuaeɛ mu nko ara wɔ nsiesieeɛ tebea mu.
- **Nkra deɛ wopɛ** wɔ kuo biara mu, wɔ **Nhyehyɛeɛ → Nkra** mu.
- **Mmara a Ɛfa Nhyehyɛeɛ no Dibea Ho** ne **Kokoamsɛm Bɔhyɛ** kaa Nhyehyɛeɛ ne Mmara ne Kokoamsɛm Nkra ho, ne nsakraeɛ a wɔdi akyi ne ɔpene foforɔ bere a wɔsan de mmara foforɔ ba.

## Deɛ wɔresɔ hwɛ

- **Mobile app** ma Android ne iOS, a ɛfa deɛ wɛb app yɛ no dodoɔ no ara ho, ne nipadua bue ne nkrataa gyeɛ a intanɛt nhia. Wɔsii no firii API korɔ no ara so. Wɔmfaa no nkɔɔ app sotɔɔ so nnɛ.
