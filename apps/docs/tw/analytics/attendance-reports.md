# Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ

<SectionClip slug="analytics/attendance-reports" />

Mmaeɛ nkaeɛbɔ ma wohunu ɔsom ne fekuo mu mmaeɛ nkɔsoɔ wɔ bere mu pefee, na ɛboa wo ma wohunu nsiane ntɛm na woayɛ ho adeɛ ansa na ayɛ su.

## Mmeaeɛ mmienu a wohwɛ

| Baabi | Deɛ ɛyɛ |
|-------|----------------|
| **Nkaeɛbɔ → Mmaeɛ** | Nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ no: mmaeɛ rekɔ soro anaa ɛresiane |
| **Mmaeɛ → Nkaeɛbɔ** | Nkaeɛbɔ a emu da hɔ: hwan na ɔba, mpɛn ahe, ne hwan na wagyae |

## Nkaeɛbɔ a emu da hɔ

Firi **Mmaeɛ** so, bue **Nkaeɛbɔ**. Biribiara a ɛwɔ kratafa no so fa ɔkwan baako so, na ɛno ne deɛ ɛho hia sene biara sɛ wote aseɛ.

### Ahokekafoɔ dodoɔ a wɔde kyekyɛ

Mmaeɛ *dodoɔ* ho mfasoɔ gyina deɛ wode kyekyɛ so. Sɛ wode nkrataa no nyinaa kyekyɛ a, asɔre baa biara bɛyɛ sɛ ɛnyɛ yie, ɛfiri sɛ nkrataa no mu wɔ nnipa a wɔtu kɔɔ baabi mfeɛ mmiɛnsa ni.

Enti deɛ wɔde kyekyɛ wɔ ha ne **ahokekafoɔ**: nnipa a wɔabɛyɛ pɛnkorɔ wɔ bere a woyi mu. Yi **abosome 3, 6, anaa 12** firi kratafa no atifi, na dodoɔ, akuo, ne mfonini nsɛntitire biara a ɛwɔ kratafa no so bɛsan abu ɛno so.

Bere tiawa bisa sɛ "ɛrekɔ so dɛn ma yɛn ne nnipa a wɔwɔ hɔ seesei". Bere tenten bisa sɛ "wɔn a yɛanya wɔn pɛn nyinaa mu, dodoɔ sɛn na wɔda so ba". Mmienu nyinaa yɛ nsɛmmisa ankasa; wɔma dodoɔ soronko, na bere a woyiiɛ no kyerɛ deɛ wobisaeɛ.

### Mmaeɛ mfonini

Nsɛntitire baako ma ahokekani biara, fa baako ma ɔsom biara, wɔ nnawɔtwe 13 a atwam mu, deɛ ɛdi kan wɔ benkum. Beaeɛ biara wɔ kɔla: ɔwɔ hɔ, intanɛt so, akyire, anaa ɔyeraeɛ.

Yei ne nhwɛmu a ɛhwehwɛ nnipa ankorankoro, na ɛnyɛ nkɔsoɔ. Kɔla a ɛda hɔ a ɛgyae mpofirim no yɛ obi a wagyae ba, na ɔyeraeɛ nnidisoɔ fa no kan ne mmaeɛ a wanyɛ bi nnidisoɔ so, sɛdeɛ wobɛtumi ahyehyɛ nhwɛmu no so afa obi a wakyɛ paa so. Anaa hyehyɛ fa mmaeɛ ɔha nkyekyɛmu anaa din so sɛ ɛho hia a.

Mfonini no yɛ asɔre baa biara dea: yi asɔre baa bi na ɛpue.

### Mmaeɛ mpɛn dodoɔ

Sɛdeɛ ahokekafoɔ nkrataa no kyɛ wɔ mpɛn a nnipa ba ankasa so, wɔ bere a woyiiɛ mu:

| Kuo | Aseɛ |
|------|---------|
| Dapɛn biara | Ɔbaa ɔsom no 75% so anaa deɛ ɛboro saa |
| Nnawɔtwe mmienu biara | 40% kɔsi 74% |
| Bosome biara | 15% kɔsi 39% |
| Ɛtɔ da | Ɛnnuru 15%, nanso ɔbaa pɛnkorɔ |
| Ɔdaeɛ | Ɔwɔ nkrataa a ɛrekɔ so mu, nanso wamma koraa wɔ bere no mu |

Ɔdaeɛ kuo no na ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ. Yeinom yɛ nnipa a asɔre no da so kan wɔn nanso obiara anhunu wɔn wɔ bere no nyinaa mu.

### Ahɔhoɔ foforɔ ne wɔn a wɔsan ba

Dapɛn biara ahɔhoɔ foforɔ dodoɔ ne wɔn a wɔsan ba dodoɔ, wɔ nnawɔtwe 12 a atwam mu. Sɛ wokenkan nsensaneeɛ mmienu no bom a, ɛkyerɛ wo ɔhaw mmienu a ɛsono emu biara no mu deɛ wowɔ: sɛ ahɔhoɔ foforɔ nsensaneeɛ no yɛ tamaa a, obi foforɔ mmɛba; sɛ ahɔhoɔ foforɔ nsensaneeɛ no yɛ fɛ na wɔn a wɔsan ba deɛ ɛyɛ tamaa a, nnipa ba pɛnkorɔ na wɔnnsan mma bio.

### Nyiyimu

Te deɛ ɛwɔ soro hɔ nyinaa so fa asɔre baa, adwumakuo, anaa fekuo so, sɛdeɛ adwumakuo kannifoɔ bɛtumi ahunu ɔno ara ne kuo su na ɛnyɛ asɔre baa no deɛ.

## Kɔ nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ no so

Kɔ **Nkaeɛbɔ** na kratafa no bue wɔ **Mmaeɛ** tab so.

## Ɔsom mmaeɛ

### Dapɛn Mmaeɛ Dodoɔ mfonini

Nsensaneeɛ mfonini a ɛkyerɛ mmaeɛ dodoɔ mfimfini (asɔremufoɔ a wɔwɔ hɔ ɔha nkyekyɛmu) wɔ nnawɔtwe 8 a atwam mu. Beaeɛ biara gyina dapɛn baako ananmu.

- Nsensaneeɛ tamaa a ɛbɛn 80%+ no yɛ apɔwmuden
- Sɛ ɛresiane daa a, ɛhia hwɛ
- Sɛ ɛte hwe ɛda bi so a, ebia na dwumadie soronko anaa ɔsom a wɔtwaa mu ho asɛm

### Nkɔsoɔ nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ

Mfonini no atifi kyerɛ nkɔsoɔ agyiraeɛ:
- **Ɛrekɔ soro**: mmaeɛ rebɛyɛ yie
- **Ɛresiane**: mmaeɛ resiane (wɔkyerɛ no kɔkɔɔ mu sɛ kɔkɔbɔ)

### Bosome Nsɛmpɔtee

Nsɛm tiawa a ɛda adi wɔ mfonini no nifa so. Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ:

> "Mmaeɛ hia hwɛ. Dodoɔ mfimfini yɛ 80% wɔ nnawɔtwe 8 a atwam mu"

Mia **Hwɛ emu nkyekyɛmu** na kɔ mu.

## Fekuo mmaeɛ

### Fekuo a Nnipa Kɔ Mu Pii

Mfonini a ɛhyehyɛ fekuo so wɔ nhyiamu mmaeɛ nyinaa so. Mfonini no tenten gyina mufoɔ dodoɔ a wɔkɔɔ nhyiamu wɔ bere no mu ananmu. Mia fa biara so na hwɛ saa fekuo no nhyiamu abakɔsɛm nyinaa.

Yei boa wo ma wohunu fekuo a ɛrenyini ne deɛ ebia ɛhia ɔhwɛ anaa bere nsakraeɛ.

## Me Mmaeɛ (ɔbaakofoɔ nhwɛmu)

Ɔmanfoɔ biara wɔ ɔno ankasa mmaeɛ nkaeɛbɔ wɔ ne **Me Mmaeɛ** kratafa so:

- Nnawɔtwe 12 mmaeɛ dodoɔ
- Bere dodie nkyekyɛmu (Ɔwɔ hɔ, Akyire, Intanɛt so, Ɔyeraeɛ) sɛ mfonini
- Nnidisoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei (ɔsom a woakɔ a anto mu)
- Ɔsom abakɔsɛm nyinaa wɔ bere no mu, deɛ ɛdi kan kane

Ɛyɛ kokoamsɛm: ɔmanfoɔ no, ne sɔfoɔ, ne sohwɛfoɔ nko ara na wɔhunu.

## Ɔsom ahodoɔ wɔ nkaeɛbɔ mu

Mmaeɛ nsɛm no fa ɔsom ahodoɔ nyinaa ho:
- Kwasida Ɔsom
- Nnawɔtwe Mfimfini Ɔsom
- Ɔsom Soronko
- Mpaeɛbɔ

Yiyi ɔsom su mu bere a wopɛ sɛ wohwɛ Kwasida mmaeɛ a ɛnka nnawɔtwe mfimfini ho.

## Yi mmaeɛ nsɛm firi mu

Fa **Yi CSV** a ɛwɔ Mmaeɛ kratafa no so di dwuma na twe mmaeɛ nsɛm nyinaa a ɛfa nyiyimu a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei ho. Nna dodoɔ ne asɔre baa nyiyimu ka deɛ woyi no ho.

## Deɛ obiara hunu

| Hwan | Deɛ ɔhunu |
|-----|--------------|
| Sohwɛfoɔ | Asɔre mmaa nyinaa, ɔsom nyinaa |
| Asɔre baa so tumi | Ɔno ara asɔre baa nko ara |
| Kuo kannifoɔ | Ɔno ara fekuo anaa adwumakuo nko ara |
| Tumi biara nni hɔ | Ɔno ara mmaeɛ nko ara (Me Mmaeɛ kratafa) |
