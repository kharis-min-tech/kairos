# Ahokeka

<SectionClip slug="analytics/engagement" />

Ahokeka bisa asɛm foforɔ sene mmaeɛ. Mmaeɛ bisa sɛ obi baeɛ anaa. Ahokeka bisa sɛ ɔwɔ asɔre asetena mu ankasa anaa, na ɛno na ɛtaa si gyinae sɛ ɔbɛwɔ hɔ afe a ɛreba no anaa.

::: warning Wɔayi ahokeka kaad dada no
Dashbɔd no mu tete no na **Asɔremufoɔ Ahokeka** kaad a ɛkyerɛ gyinabea baako — Ɛkorɔn / Mfimfini / Ɛba fam — wɔ so. Ɛnni hɔ bio, ne Mishɔn Kɔntrol fa no nyinaa. Nsusuiɛ a ɛdidi so yi na ɛsii ananmu, na ɛyɛ dodoɔ pii hyɛ da na ɛnyɛ baako.
:::

## Deɛ wɔde kyekyɛ a ɛyɛ nokorɛ

Biribiara a ɛwɔ kratafa yi so gyina adwene baako so: **ahokekani** yɛ obi a ɔabɛyɛ pɛnkorɔ wɔ bere a woyi mu, a wotumi yi abosome 3, 6, anaa 12 firi [Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ](/tw/analytics/attendance-reports#ahokekafoɔ-dodoɔ-a-wɔde-kyekyɛ) atifi.

Saa bere no ne nhwɛsoɔ a ne nsɛm so sene biara wɔ nkaeɛbɔ no mu. Yi no tiawa a, worebisa sɛdeɛ ɛrekɔ so ma wo ne nnipa a wɔwɔ hɔ seesei. Yi no tenten a, worebisa sɛ wɔn a woanya wɔn pɛn nyinaa mu, dodoɔ sɛn na wɔda so ba. Mmienu nyinaa yɛ nsɛmmisa ankasa, na wɔma ɔha nkyekyɛmu a ɛsono emu biara ma asɔre korɔ no ara.

## Mpɛn a ahokekafoɔ ba ankasa

Mmaeɛ mpɛn nkyekyɛmu no kyekyɛ ahokekafoɔ nkrataa no mu akuo enum wɔ bere a woyiiɛ mu:

| Kuo | Aseɛ |
|------|---------|
| Dapɛn biara | Ɔbaa ɔsom no 75% so anaa deɛ ɛboro saa |
| Nnawɔtwe mmienu biara | 40% kɔsi 74% |
| Bosome biara | 15% kɔsi 39% |
| Ɛtɔ da | Ɛnnuru 15%, nanso ɔbaa pɛnkorɔ |
| Ɔdaeɛ | Ɔwɔ nkrataa a ɛrekɔ so mu, nanso wamma koraa wɔ bere no mu |

Hwɛ ne tebea nyinaa, na ɛnyɛ mfonini baako. Asɔre a ne Dapɛn biara kuo resua na Bosome biara kuo rekɔ soro no rehwere ahoɔden ansa na wahwere nnipa, na ɛno ne bere a ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ ho adeɛ.

## Wo ho ka mu, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wowɔ hɔ kɛkɛ

Ɔsom mmaeɛ nko ara yɛ nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ mmerɛ. Obi a ɔba dapɛn biara nanso ɔnni fekuo, ɔnni adwumakuo, na obiara ne no nkasa no, ɔbata ɔdan no ho na ɛnyɛ asɔre no. Kairos kyerɛ emu biara soronko:

| Nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ | Baabi a wohunu |
|--------|----------------|
| Ɔba ɔsom | Ɔmanfoɔ biara mfonini wɔ [Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ](/tw/analytics/attendance-reports#mmaeɛ-mfonini) mu |
| Ɔwɔ fekuo mu | Kuo-biara-nni-hɔ nhwɛmu kyerɛ obiara a ɔnni bi mu |
| Ɔwɔ adwumakuo mu | Nhwɛmu korɔ no ara; ɛhwehwɛ sɛ mmienu nyinaa yɛ hwee |
| Obi ne no akasa | Nkɔdi akyi abakɔsɛm wɔ ɔmanfoɔ no nkrataa so |
| Ɔrekɔ Asɔremufoɔ mu | [Asɔremufoɔ Adesua](/tw/platform/membership) |
| Ɔrekɔ asuafoyɛ mu | [Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ](/tw/platform/new-believers) |

## Ɔbaakofoɔ a wodi n'akyi

- **Mfonini no** kyerɛ nsɛntitire baako ma ɔmanfoɔ biara wɔ nnawɔtwe 13 a atwam mu, ne ɔyeraeɛ nnidisoɔ dodoɔ, sɛdeɛ wobɛtumi ahyehyɛ so afa obi a wakyɛ paa so
- **Me Mmaeɛ** ma ɔmanfoɔ no ɔno ankasa dodoɔ, bere dodie nkyekyɛmu, ne nnidisoɔ
- **Nkɔdi akyi abakɔsɛm** a ɛwɔ ɔmanfoɔ nkrataa so kyerɛ nkitahodie biara a wɔakyerɛw ne ho, wɔ tebea biara mu, a mmoa ne ahobammɔ agyiraeɛ da adi
- **Adwumakuo ne fekuo mufoɔ nhwɛmu** kyerɛ sɛ ɔwɔ kuo no mu ankasa anaa wadane

## Ma ahokeka nkɔ soɔ

Nhwɛmu no wɔ hɔ sɛdeɛ yei bɛyɛ adwuma na ɛnyɛ nsusuiɛ:

- Di **ahɔhoɔ foforɔ** nhwɛmu no ho dwuma wɔ nna kakra mu, na ɛnyɛ nnawɔtwe. Nnipa a obiara ne wɔn nkasaa koraa nko ara na ɛwɔ mu.
- Di **kuo-biara-nni-hɔ** nhwɛmu no ho dwuma daa. Ɛyɛ hwee bere a obi de ne ho ka biribi ho ankasa, na ɛno ne botaeɛ no.
- Di **ɛduru so** nhwɛmu no ho dwuma na woadi bɔhyɛ a w'ankasa nkɔdi akyi hyɛeɛ no so.
- Di **Ɔdaeɛ** kuo no akyi ansa na saa din no abɛyɛ Ɛtɔ da wɔ bere tenten mu.
- Ma asɔremufoɔ adesua no nkɔ so mpɛn pii sɛdeɛ ntam a ɛda Asafo nyinaa ne Asɔremufoɔ ankasa ntam no rentrɛ bio.
- Kyerɛw fekuo mmaeɛ, sɛdeɛ nnipa a wɔkɔ bɛda adi koraa. Nhyiamu a wɔankyerɛw no te sɛ nhyiamu a obiara ankɔ.
