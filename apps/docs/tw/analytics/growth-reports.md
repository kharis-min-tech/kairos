# Nkɔsoɔ Amanneɛbɔ

<SectionClip slug="analytics/growth-reports" />

Nkɔsoɔ amanneɛbɔ di sɛdeɛ asɔre no retrɛ so akyi: asɔremufoɔ foforɔ a wɔreba, asɔremufoɔ a wɔresan aba bere a wɔantena hɔ akyi, ne sɛdeɛ asafo no rekɔ wɔ bere mu.

## Kɔ nkɔsoɔ nsɛm so

Kɔ **Nkaeɛbɔ** na mia **Nkɔanim** tab a ɛwɔ atifi nifa.

## Dodoɔ atitire

| Dodoɔ | Nkyerɛkyerɛmu |
|------|-------------|
| Asɔremufoɔ Foforɔ | Asɔremufoɔ a wɔde kaa ho wɔ bere a woyiiɛ mu |
| Bosome ne bosome nsakraeɛ | Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ "+24 bosome a ɛtwaam"; sɛdeɛ saa bere yi ne deɛ ɛdii kan no te |
| Nkɔanim Abosome | Abosome a anto mu a asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ kɔɔ soro |

## Asɔremufoɔ nkɔsoɔ mfonini

Dashbɔd no Mishɔn Kɔntrol fa no wɔ **Asɔremufoɔ Nkɔsoɔ** mfonini a ɛkyerɛ abosome 6 a atwam asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ. Beaeɛ biara gyina asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ a wɔwɔ hɔ wɔ bosome no awieeɛ ananmu.

Fa Nkaeɛbɔ kratafa no Nkɔanim tab di dwuma sɛ wopɛ deɛ emu da hɔ a nna dodoɔ nyiyimu ka ho a.

## Deɛ wɔkan sɛ nkɔsoɔ

Wɔkan ɔmanfoɔ sɛ ɔyɛ foforɔ bere a **wɔpene** n'akawnt so, na ɛnyɛ bere a ɔkyerɛw ne din. Yei si akawnt a ɛretwɛn kwan sɛ ɛbɛma nkɔsoɔ dodoɔ ayɛ kɛseɛ kwa.

## Asafo no nkyekyɛmu

Dashbɔd no kyerɛ asafo no nyinaa a wɔakyekyɛ mu akuo nnan, na ɛnnan no bom yɛ nkrataa no nyinaa bere biara.

| Kuo | Deɛ ɛkan ankasa |
|----------|------------------------|
| Asɔremufoɔ | Nnipa a **wɔawie nnawɔtwe nnan asɔremufoɔ adesua no**. Yei ne asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ ankasa, na biribi foforɔ biara mma. |
| Wɔn a Wɔsan Ba | Nnipa a wɔba daa nanso wɔnyɛɛ adesua no. Wɔ asɔre mmaa dodoɔ no ara mu no, yei ne ɛnnan no mu kɛseɛ. |
| Ahɔhoɔ | Wɔn a wɔba ɛtɔ da, a Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ krataa na ɛtaa yɛ wɔn |
| Mmɔfra | Wɔdi wɔn akyi soronko, na wɔwɔ wɔn ankasa banbɔ |

::: warning Asɔremufoɔ nkyerɛ "obiara a ɔwɔ akawnt"
Yei ne dodoɔ a nnipa taa nte aseɛ sene biara wɔ Kairos mu. Obi a ɔba daa na wasom wɔ adwumakuo mu mfeɛ num no yɛ **Deɛ Ɔsan Ba**, na ɛnyɛ Muni, kɔsi sɛ ɔbɛwie adesua no. Sɛ Wɔn a Wɔsan Ba dodoɔ rekɔ soro na Asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ yɛ tamaa a, ɛkyerɛ sɛ nnipa reba na wɔretena hɔ nanso adesua no nnkɔ so mpɛn pii. Hwɛ [Asɔremufoɔ Adesua](/tw/platform/membership).
:::

## Asɔre baa so ne asɔre no nyinaa so

- **Sohwɛfoɔ** hunu nkɔsoɔ wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa so, ne asɔre mmaa ntotoho mfonini
- **Asɔfoɔ** (obiara a ɔwɔ asɔre baa so tumi) hunu wɔn asɔre baa nkɔsoɔ nko ara
- **Akannifoɔ** hunu wɔn adwumakuo anaa fekuo nkɔsoɔ nko ara
- **Asɔremufoɔ** ntumi nkɔ Nkaeɛbɔ so

## Sɛdeɛ wode nkɔsoɔ amanneɛbɔ di dwuma yie

- Sɛ asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ yɛ tamaa anaa ɛresiane na mmaeɛ korɔn a, ɛkyerɛ sɛ ahɔhoɔ nnane; hwɛ [Akra Ɔkwan](/tw/platform/souls-pipeline) ne [Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ](/tw/platform/new-believers) ɔkwan no
- Sɛ asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ rekɔ soro na mmaeɛ resiane a, ɛkyerɛ sɛ asɔremufoɔ foforɔ no nntena hɔ; hwɛ fekuo mu tenaeɛ ne nkɔdi akyi dodoɔ
- Fa nkɔsoɔ no toto [Ahokeka](/tw/analytics/engagement) dodoɔ ho na woahunu ne nyinaa
