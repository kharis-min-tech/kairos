# Asɔre Apɔwmuden

## Dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ

### Asɔremufoɔ ankasa ne asafo no nyinaa

Mmienu nyinaa wɔ Nkwa mu. Ntam a ɛda wɔn ntam no kyerɛ w'asuafoyɛ adwuma a aka: nnipa a wɔyɛ asɔre no fa bi na wɔnyɛɛ [asɔremufoɔ adesua](/tw/platform/membership) no. Ntam a ɛkɔ so trɛ kɛkɛ no kyerɛ sɛ adesua no nnkɔ so mpɛn pii.

### Mmaeɛ dodoɔ ne ahokekafoɔ

Mmaeɛ dodoɔ ho mfasoɔ nni hɔ kɔsi sɛ wobɛhunu deɛ wɔde kyekyɛ. [Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ](/tw/analytics/attendance-reports#ahokekafoɔ-dodoɔ-a-wɔde-kyekyɛ) de **ahokekafoɔ** kyekyɛ, a ɛkyerɛ nnipa a wɔabɛyɛ pɛnkorɔ wɔ bere a woyi mu (abosome 3, 6, anaa 12), na ɛnyɛ nkrataa no nyinaa. Sɛ wode nkrataa no nyinaa kyekyɛ a, asɔre baa biara bɛyɛ sɛ ɛnyɛ yie, ɛfiri sɛ nkrataa no mu wɔ nnipa a wɔtu kɔɔ baabi mfeɛ bi ni.

### Ɔdaeɛ kuo

Wɔ mmaeɛ mpɛn nkyekyɛmu mu no, Ɔdaeɛ kan nnipa a wɔwɔ nkrataa a ɛrekɔ so mu a **wɔammɛyɛ koraa** wɔ bere no nyinaa mu. Yei ne apɔwmuden dodoɔ a wotumi yɛ ho adeɛ sene biara wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no mu: ɛyɛ din nhwɛmu, na ɛyɛ nhwɛmu a [kuo-biara-nni-hɔ ne ɛduru-so nkɔdi akyi nhwɛmu](/tw/platform/follow-ups) wɔ hɔ sɛ wɔbɛdi ho dwuma.

### Nkwa kɔkɔbɔ

Nkwa kyerɛ yeinom ne ho ara, sɛ nkitahodie na ɛnyɛ dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ wote aseɛ:

- Ɔsom nkrataa a obiara annye
- Asɔremufoɔ a wɔrefiri mmaeɛ mu
- Asɔre mmaa a wɔreka akyi
- Asɔre baa a Sɔfopanin nni mu

### Nkɔdi akyi a ɛduru nnipa so

Akra a obiara ne wɔn nkasaa nnanson anaa deɛ ɛboro saa no, wɔkan wɔn sɛ **Ɛmu yɛ den** wɔ [Akra Dashbɔd](/tw/platform/souls-dashboard) so. Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ nkrataa a agyina, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ nkɔsoɔ biara nni hɔ nnanson anaa deɛ ɛboro saa, wɔkyerɛ no sɛ kɔkɔbɔ dodoɔ wɔ [Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ](/tw/platform/new-believers) kratafa so.
