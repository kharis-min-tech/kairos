# Ɔpene

<SectionClip slug="administration/approvals" />

Ɔpene nhwɛmu no so na obiara a ɔwɔ asɔre baa so tumi hwɛ asɔremufoɔ foforɔ dinkyerɛw ansa na wama wɔn akwannya nyinaa.

## Deɛ enti a ɔpene wɔ hɔ

Sɛ obi kyerɛw ne din ankasa firi akawnt bue kratafa so a, wɔmma no akwannya nyinaa ntɛm ara. N'akawnt firi aseɛ wɔ **Ɛretwɛn** tebea mu. Yei si nnipa a wɔnnim wɔn kwan sɛ wɔbɛkɔ asɔremufoɔ nsɛm so ansa na asɔre no ahwɛ ahunu wɔn.

## Kɔ nhwɛmu no so

Kɔ **Asɔremufoɔ**. **Ɔpene Nhwɛmu** bɔtin pue wɔ kratafa no atifi. Abisadeɛ a ɛretwɛn dodoɔ wɔ bɔtin no so.

Dashbɔd no nso kyerɛ ɔpene a ɛretwɛn wɔ ne dodoɔ atitire mu, ma obiara a ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ho adeɛ. Dodoɔ **0** kyerɛ sɛ biribiara nni hɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ.

## Hwɛ abisadeɛ

Mia **Ɔpene Nhwɛmu** na hwɛ dinkyerɛw a ɛretwɛn nyinaa. Biara kyerɛ:

- Ɔmanfoɔ din ne ne mfonini (sɛ ɔde baeɛ a)
- Imel ne telefon
- Asɔre baa a ɔyiiɛ
- Ɛda a ɔkyerɛwee ne din

Wotumi **Pene So** anaa **Po** abisadeɛ biara ankorankoro.

## Pene ɔmanfoɔ so

Mia **Pene So**. Ɔmanfoɔ no tebea dane **Wɔapene So** ntɛm ara. Afei ɔtumi bue na ɔnya asɔremufoɔ akwannya nyinaa: ne nkyekyɛmu trɛ, ɔhunu ne nkrataa, ne fekuo, na ɔtumi de nkrataa kɔ.

## Po abisadeɛ

Mia **Po**. Wɔyi akawnt no firi hɔ. Imel akyerɛwdeɛ no tumi san di dwuma ma dinkyerɛw foforɔ. Fa yei di dwuma ma dinkyerɛw a ɛyɛ mmienu, akawnt a ɛnyɛ nokorɛ, anaa deɛ wɔyɛɛ no mfomsoɔ so.

## Asɔremufoɔ a adwumayɛfoɔ de kaa ho

Asɔremufoɔ a adwumayɛfoɔ de kaa ho tẽẽ (fa **Asɔremufoɔ → + Fa Ɔmanfoɔ Ka Ho** so) no, wɔpene so wɔn ho ara. Ɔpene nhwɛmu no fa wɔn a wɔkyerɛw wɔn din ankasa no nko ara ho.

## Deɛ ɔmanfoɔ a ɔretwɛn tumi hunu

Kɔsi sɛ wɔbɛpene so no, ɔmanfoɔ a ɔretwɛn tumi hunu ɔno ara ne nkrataa nko ara. Ɔntumi nhunu asɔremufoɔ nhwɛmu, fekuo, adwumakuo, anaa nkaeɛbɔ biara. Wɔhyɛ yei API no so, na ɛnyɛ anim no nko ara.

::: tip Ɔpene nyɛ asɔremufoɔ
Sɛ wopene akawnt so a, ɛma obi tumi bue na ɔka ho. Ɛmma ɔnyɛ Muni ankasa; [asɔremufoɔ adesua](/tw/platform/membership) no nko ara na ɛyɛ saa.
:::
