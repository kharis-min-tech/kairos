# Akwannya

<SectionClip slug="administration/permissions" />

Akwannya wɔ Kairos mu gyina **tumi a wɔde ma adwuma ho** so, na ɛnyɛ dibea din so. Sɛ wote nsonsonoeɛ a ɛda nhyehyɛeɛ dibea, tumi, ne ne ntrɛsoɔ ntam ase a, na wobɛtumi asiesie akwannya no yie.

## Nhyehyɛeɛ dibea mmienu

Ɔmanfoɔ nkrataa biara kura `systemRole`, na ɛwɔ gyinabea mmienu pɛ:

| Dibea | Hwan na ɔwɔ |
|------|-----------|
| `admin` | Nhyehyɛeɛ kuo ketewa no. Ɔfa nhwɛsoɔ biara so wɔ asɔre baa biara mu. Fa no sɛ ntɛmpɛ akwannya, na ɛnyɛ dibea din. |
| `member` | Obiara foforɔ, ɛfiri ɔhɔhoɔ foforɔ kɔsi Sɔfopanin. Wɔn tumi nyinaa firi tumi a wɔde ma mu. |

::: warning Ɔsɔfoɔ anaa Kannifoɔ dibea nni hɔ
Kane no na Kairos wɔ `pastor`, `leader`, ne `elder` sɛ nhyehyɛeɛ dibea. Ɛnni hɔ bio. Sɛ krataa, tebo, anaa mfonini dada bi ka obi "dibea" sɛ Ɔsɔfoɔ anaa Kannifoɔ a, ɛreka **tumi ahodoɔ a ɔwɔ** ho asɛm, na ɛnyɛ beaeɛ a ɛwɔ nkrataakoraeɛ mu.
:::

## Tumi a wɔde ma adwuma ho

Tumi yɛ dibea bagua a wɔato din na ɛbata beaeɛ pɔtee ho. Tumi na ɛma `member` nya akwannya a ɛboro ɔno ankasa nkrataa so. Ɔmanfoɔ tumi kura pii bere korɔ mu na emu biara ne ne ho.

| Tumi | Din a ɛwɔ dibea nhwɛmu mu | Beaeɛ a ɛduru | Deɛ ɛbue |
|-------|----------------------|-------|-----------------|
| `BranchAdmin` | Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ | Asɔre baa | Adwuma nyinaa wɔ saa asɔre baa no mu, ne tumi sɛ ɔde Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ bɛma na wapaw akannifoɔ. Ɔkenkan mmoa nkrataa beaeɛ nso. |
| `BranchDataAdmin` | Asɔre Baa Nkrataa Sohwɛfoɔ | Asɔre baa | Asɔre baa adwuma a akwannya safoa nka ho: ɔmanfoɔ nsesaeɛ, nsɛm a wɔde ba ne deɛ wɔyi, ɔpene. Ɔmma dibea, na **ɔnni** mmoa anaa ahobammɔ ani. |
| `FellowshipLeader` | Fekuo Kannifoɔ | Fekuo | Hwɛ fekuo baako mufoɔ, nhyiamu, mmaeɛ, ne nkɔdi akyi so. |
| `DepartmentLeader` | Adwumakuo Kannifoɔ | Adwumakuo | Hwɛ adwumakuo baako mufoɔ, abisadeɛ, nnidisoɔ, ntadeɛ, ne nkɔdi akyi so. |
| `DepartmentDeputy` | Adwumakuo Ɔboafoɔ | Adwumakuo | Deɛ kannifoɔ no tumi yɛ no ara, ma adwumakuo baako. |
| `SafeguardingLead` | Ahobammɔ Kannifoɔ | Asɔre baa | Asɔre baa no ahobammɔ nkrataa beaeɛ, na ɛno nko ara. Ɛnyɛ nhwɛmu, ɛnyɛ nkaeɛbɔ, ɛnyɛ mmoa. |
| `SafeguardingHead` | Ahobammɔ Panin | Asɔre | Ahobammɔ wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa so. Ɔkwan a wɔfa so kɔ soro sene asɔre baa kannifoɔ. |
| `NewBelieversMentor` | Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ Ɔkwankyerɛfoɔ | Asɔre baa | Ɔkwankyerɛ adwuma wɔ Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ ɔkwan no mu. |
| `NewBelieversTeacher` | Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ Ɔkyerɛkyerɛni | Asɔre baa | Nkyerɛkyerɛ adwuma wɔ Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ ɔkwan no mu. |
| `MembershipAdmin` | Asɔremufoɔ Sohwɛfoɔ | Asɔre | Ɔhwɛ [asɔremufoɔ adesua nhyehyɛeɛ](/tw/platform/membership) no nyinaa so: akuo, nhyiamu, anigyeɛ nhwɛmu, ɔgyeɛ, nkyerɛwee, ne awieeɛ. |
| `MembershipChampion` | Asɔremufoɔ Ɔboafoɔ | Asɔre baa | Ɔkenkan nko: asɔre baa baako twɛn nhwɛmu ne wɔn a wɔagye wɔn, ne saa asɔre baa no asɔremufoɔ nkra. Ɔnsesa hwee da. |

### Tumi a ɛfiri dibea mu: asɔre baa sɔfo

Tumi a ɛtɔ so dubaako bi wɔ hɔ a obiara mmfa mma. Kairos ankasa na ɛhunu.

Obiara a ɔwɔ **Sɔfopanin** nsɛntitire no mu wɔ asɔre baa bi akannifoɔ mu no, wɔde `BranchPastor` tumi ma no ne ho ara, bere biara a ɔbisa biribi. Ɛkura saa asɔre baa no ahobammɔ ani, na ɛno nko ara.

Ɛwɔ hɔ ɛfiri sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ahobammɔ ani di onipa no ankasa akyi na ɛnyɛ tumi akyi. Sɔfopanin ne Ɔsɔfoɔ bi tumi kura Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ tumi korɔ no ara, nanso Sɔfopanin no nko ara na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhunu asɔre baa no ahobammɔ nhwɛmu. Dibea-ne-akwannya nhwɛsoɔ biara ntumi nkyerɛ nnipa baanu no mu nsonsonoeɛ, enti akannifoɔ nkrataa no na ɛkasa.

Deɛ ɛfiri mu ba:

- Wontumi mfa Asɔre Baa Sɔfo nka ho anaa wonyi mfiri dibea nhwɛmu no mu. Ɛmpue hɔ.
- Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wode ahobammɔ ani ma obi a, fa no yɛ asɔre baa no Sɔfopanin, anaa fa Ahobammɔ Kannifoɔ tumi ma no.
- Sɛ woyi obi firi Sɔfopanin dibea mu a, n'ahobammɔ ani nso kɔ saa bere no ara.

## Beaeɛ a tumi duru

Tumi biara bata beaeɛ bi ho, na ahodoɔ nnan wɔ hɔ:

| Su | Deɛ ɛbɔ din | Hwan na ɔde di dwuma |
|------------|-------|---------|
| `branch` | Asɔre baa baako | Tumi dodoɔ no ara |
| `fellowship` | Fekuo baako | Fekuo Kannifoɔ |
| `department` | Adwumakuo baako | Adwumakuo Kannifoɔ, Adwumakuo Ɔboafoɔ |
| `church` | Biribiara; asɔre baako pɛ na ɛwɔ hɔ | Asɔremufoɔ Sohwɛfoɔ, Ahobammɔ Panin |

Asɔre baa so tumi duru fekuo ne adwumakuo a ɛwɔ **saa asɔre baa no mu** nso so. Asɔre no nyinaa so tumi duru biribiara so. Ɛkɔ ɔkwan baako pɛ so: sɛ wokura Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ ma asɔre baa biara a ɛwɔ asɔre no mu mpo a, ɛnsɔ asɔre no nyinaa so nhwɛsoɔ ani, ɛfiri sɛ asɔre no nni asɔre baa bi mu.

Hwɛ [Tumi Nhwɛmu](/tw/administration/capabilities) sɛ wopɛ mmara no pɔtee a.

## Fa tumi ma

Baabi a wode tumi ma gyina deɛ ɛbata ho so.

**Asɔre baa ne asɔre no nyinaa tumi**, wode ma firi ɔmanfoɔ no nkrataa so:

1. Kɔ **Asɔremufoɔ** na bue ɔmanfoɔ no nkrataa
2. Bue ne dibea fa no
3. Yi dibea no ne asɔre baa no
4. Sie

Nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ tumi yɛ yei ma ɔmanfoɔ biara wɔ asɔre baa biara mu. Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ tumi de **Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ** ma na wagye bio wɔ ɔno ara asɔre baa mu, na ɔtumi paw anaa ɔyi akannifoɔ wɔ hɔ, nanso ɔnnuru asɔre mmaa foforɔ so.

::: tip Asɔre no nyinaa tumi da so bisa asɔre baa
Sɛ wode Asɔremufoɔ Sohwɛfoɔ anaa Ahobammɔ Panin ma a, krataa no da so bisa asɔre baa a ɛwɔ hɔ. Saa asɔre baa no yɛ nkrataa sie beaeɛ pɛ, sɛdeɛ tumi no bɛpue baabi a ɛfata. Ɛnto tumi no ano; ɛduru asɔre no nyinaa so ɔkwan biara so.
:::

**Fekuo ne adwumakuo tumi deɛ, ɛnyɛ ha na wode ma.** Sɛ wobɔ mmɔden sɛ wode Fekuo Kannifoɔ bɛka ho afiri ɔmanfoɔ nkrataa so a, wɔpo na wɔka baabi a ɛsɛ sɛ wokɔ kyerɛ wo. Saa tumi no twerɛ ne ho ara bere a wobɔ obi din sɛ kannifoɔ, ɔboafoɔ kannifoɔ, anaa ɔboafoɔ wɔ kuo no ankasa so:

- Fekuo Kannifoɔ ne ɔboafoɔ: wɔ fekuo no so, firi **Fekuo**
- Adwumakuo Kannifoɔ ne Ɔboafoɔ: wɔ adwumakuo no so, firi **Adwumakuo**

Sɛ woyi wɔn firi kuo no mu a, tumi no nso kɔ.

## Gye tumi

Yi tumi no firi ɔmanfoɔ no dibea fa mu, anaa yi no firi kuo a ɛmaa no mu. Wonnyi hwee: wɔhyɛ sɛ ɛnyɛ adwuma bio, sɛdeɛ abakɔsɛm a ɛkyerɛ hwan na ɔwɔ deɛn, ne bere ko, bɛtena hɔ.

Nhyehyɛeɛ no mpene sɛ wɔbɛyi Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ a ɔtwa toɔ afiri asɔre baa bi mu. Ɛsɛ sɛ asɔre baa nya obi bere biara a ɔtumi ma akwannya.

## Bere a nsakraeɛ yɛ adwuma

Nneɛma mmienu kɔ ahoɔhare soronko so, na ɛyɛ sɛ wohunu emu nsonsonoeɛ.

- **API no san kenkan tumi firi nkrataakoraeɛ mu bisadeɛ biara mu.** Bere a wosie no, sɛɛva no gyae tumi a woayi no die na ɛfiri aseɛ di tumi foforɔ no. Bere biara nni hɔ a akwannya dada da so yɛ adwuma.
- **Onipa no ankasa skrin firi n'akawnt bue nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ mu.** Bɔtin, nkyekyɛmu, ne nkratafa pue anaa ɛyera bere a nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ no yɛ foforɔ, a ɛkɔ so wɔ akyire bere a ɔde di dwuma, anaa ntɛm ara sɛ ɔfiri mu na ɔsan bue a.

Enti tumi a woayi no gyae adwuma ntɛm ara, sɛ bɔtin a wɔabra no da so wɔ ne skrin so kakra mpo a; sɛ ɔmia a, ɛnyɛ adwuma. Tumi foforɔ a wode kaa ho no, sɛɛva no di so ntɛm ara, nanso nkyekyɛmu foforɔ no pue bere a nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ no yɛ foforɔ. Sɛ obi ka sɛ "wode akwannya maa me nanso menhunu" a, ka kyerɛ no sɛ ɔmfiri mu na ɔnsan mmue.

## Ɔsɔfoɔ dibea din

`pastor` wɔ hɔ wɔ beaeɛ baako pɛ: **dibea din** a ɛwɔ ɔmanfoɔ nkrataa so, a ɛka "Ɔsɔfopanin", "Ɔpanin", ne biribiara a wopɛ sɛ wotwerɛ ho. Ɛyɛ din a wɔkyerɛ, kɔsi nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ 50, na ɛmma **hwee**.

Ɔmanfoɔ a ɔwɔ dibea din "Ɔsɔfoɔ" na ɔkura Fekuo Kannifoɔ tumi nko ara no hunu deɛ fekuo kannifoɔ hunu pɛpɛɛpɛ. Sɛ ɔpɛ asɔre baa no nyinaa akwannya a, ɛsɛ sɛ ɔnya Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ tumi.

Hunu sɛ dibea din no ne asɔre baa **akannidie** a wɔde ma no nyɛ adeɛ korɔ. Dibea din no ne sɛdeɛ wɔfrɛ obi; akannifoɔ nkrataa no (Sɔfopanin anaa Ɔsɔfoɔ) na ɛkyerɛ hwan na ɔwɔ dibea wɔ asɔre baa mu, na Sɔfopanin nko ara na ɔkura ahobammɔ ani a yɛkaa ho asɛm wɔ soro hɔ no.

## Dibea nsesaeɛ nni hɔ

Dibea nsesaeɛ biara nni app no mu, na "yi wo dibea" afa biara nni hɔ wɔ akawnt bue akyi. Ɔmanfoɔ ntumi nyɛ obi foforɔ. Nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ baako kura ne tumi nyinaa, na biribiara a ɔhunu firi mu ba. Sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ n'akwannya sesa a, sesa ne tumi.
