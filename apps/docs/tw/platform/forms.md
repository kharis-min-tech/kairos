## Nkrataa a ɛwɔ hɔ

| Krataa | Deɛ ɛyɛ |
|------|---------|
| Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ | Gye ɔhɔhoɔ na kyerɛw ne nsɛm ma nkɔdi akyi |
| Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ Adesua | Fa obi a ɔgyee afɔrebukyia frɛ so hyɛ asuafoyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ mu |
| Asubɔ | Gye abisadeɛ sɛ wɔmmɔ obi asu |
| Adansedie | Ka deɛ Onyankopɔn ayɛ |
| Abɔfra Din Tuo | Bisa abɔfra din tuo afahyɛ |
| Abɔfra Kyerɛ | Bisa abɔfra kyerɛ |

Mia **Bue krataa →** wɔ kaad biara so na ɛbue saa krataa no.

::: warning Sohwɛfoɔ ne Asɔfoɔ nko ara
Sohwɛfoɔ ne Asɔfoɔ nko ara na wɔtumi kɔ nkrataa nsia no nyinaa so. Akannifoɔ a wɔwɔ Sohwɛ adwumakuo no mu nso tumi hwɛ anim pon nkrataa a wɔde ba. Asɔremufoɔ a aka no ntumi nkɔ Nkrataa kratafa no so.
:::

### Yiyi nkrataa no mu

| Nyiyimu | Deɛ ɛwɔ hɔ |
|--------|---------|
| Krataa su | Nyinaa, Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ, Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ Adesua, Asubɔ, Adansedie, Abɔfra Din Tuo, Abɔfra Kyerɛ |
| Tebea | Nyinaa, Ɛretwɛn, Wɔahwɛ, Wɔayɛ ho adeɛ |
| Ɛfiri / Kɔsi | Nna dodoɔ |

### Yi nkrataa no firi mu

Mia **Yi CSV** na twe nkrataa a woyiyii mu no nyinaa sɛ tebo. Ɛho wɔ mfasoɔ ma nkɔdi akyi adwuma a ɛkɔ so wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no akyi.

## Ahɔhoɔ a Wɔada

Mia **Ahɔhoɔ a wɔada** na fa nkrataa a nkrataa yɛeɛ na ɛnni mu adeɛ no sie prɛko: nnipa a wɔfaa ɔhɔhoɔ anaa afɔrebukyia frɛ krataa so baeɛ nanso wɔanyɛ biribi foforɔ. Yei ma asɔremufoɔ nhwɛmu no yɛ kronn a wɔnnyi nkrataa biara mfiri hɔ koraa.

## Sɛdeɛ nkrataa no bata afa foforɔ ho

- **Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ Adesua** krataa → ɛyɛ nkrataa wɔ [Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ](/tw/platform/new-believers) ɔkwan no mu ne ho ara
- **Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ** krataa → ɛyɛ nkitahodie nkrataa ma nkɔdi akyi
- **Asubɔ** krataa → ɛyɛ abisadeɛ a ɛpue wɔ sohwɛfoɔ nhwɛmu mu
- **Abɔfra Din Tuo / Kyerɛ** → ɛyɛ abisadeɛ ma sohwɛfoɔ hyehyɛ
