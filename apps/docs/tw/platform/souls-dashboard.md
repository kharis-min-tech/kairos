## Dodoɔ atitire

Dodoɔ nnan wɔ kratafa no atifi:

| Dodoɔ | Aseɛ |
|------|---------|
| Akra Nyinaa | Akra a wɔnyaa wɔn wɔ bere a woyiiɛ no mu |
| Ɛmu yɛ den | Akra a wɔnnii wɔn akyi nna 7+ mu; ɛhia ntɛm hwɛ |
| Hwɛ so | Akra a wɔahyɛ wɔn agyiraeɛ sɛ wɔnhwɛ wɔn daa |
| Ɛrekɔ yie | Akra a wɔadi wɔn akyi nnansa yi |

Mia dodoɔ kaad biara na kɔ saa akra no nhwɛmu a wɔayiyi mu wɔ [Akra Ɔkwan](/tw/platform/souls-pipeline) no so.

## Bere nyiyimu

Fa tab no sesa nsɛm no bere: **Dapɛn**, **Bosome 1**, **Bosome 3**, **Bosome 6**, anaa **Wo Dea**. Yiyi asɛmpatrɛw nhyehyɛeɛ pɔtee mu fa **Nhyehyɛeɛ** nkyekyɛmu a ɛwɔ nifa so.

## Akra Ɔkwan Tebea

Mfonini a ɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ akra no kyɛ nkɔdi akyi apɔwmuden tebea mmiɛnsa so: **Ɛmu yɛ den**, **Hwɛ so**, ne **Ɛrekɔ yie**.

## Akra ne wɔn Tebea

Mfonini a ɛkyerɛ akra dodoɔ a wɔwɔ ɔkwan no afa biara mu: Foforɔ, Yɛredi Akyi, N'ani Gye Ho, Wasakra, N'ani Nnye Ho. Mia fa biara so na hwɛ akra a wɔwɔ saa tebea no mu.

## Nkɔdi Akyi Tebea

Mfonini a ɛkyerɛ nkɔdi akyi adwuma apɔwmuden: akra dodoɔ a wɔne wɔn akasa nnansa yi ne wɔn a bere atwam wɔn so.

## Nsakraeɛ Kwan

Mfonini a ɛda hɔ a ɛkyerɛ ɔkwan no nyinaa firi Foforɔ → Yɛredi Akyi → N'ani Gye Ho → Wasakra. Mia fa biara so na hwɛ akra a wɔwɔ hɔ.

## Afa Mu Nkɔsoɔ Dodoɔ

Ɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ akra no renya nkɔsoɔ wɔ afa biara mu: wɔn a wɔrekɔ w'anim ɔha nkyekyɛmu ne wɔn a wɔagyina hɔ.

## Hwan na ɔtumi hunu Akra Dashbɔd

Asɔfoɔ ne Sohwɛfoɔ. Akannifoɔ hunu akra nsɛm a ɛfa wɔn ankasa adwumakuo nkɔdi akyi adwuma ho nko ara.
