# Asɔre Mmaa

Asɔre Mmaa kratafa kyerɛ asɔre no beaeɛ biara a ɛwɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no mu. Sohwɛfoɔ hunu asɔre mmaa nyinaa wɔ mpɔtam nyinaa mu. Asɔfoɔ ne asɔrefoɔ hunu wɔn ankasa asɔre baa.

## Asɔre baa kaad

Asɔre baa biara pue sɛ kaad a ɛwɔ:
- Asɔre baa din ne ne su agyiraeɛ (**Panin** anaa **Nkekaho**)
- Ɔmantam din
- Kurow, email ne telefon nɔma
- **Yi Firi Adwuma Mu** bɔtin (Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara)

## Yi fa ɔmantam so

Fa **Mpɔtam Nyinaa** a ɛwɔ atifi no yi asɔre mmaa fa ɔmantam so. Ɛho wɔ mfasoɔ bere a wohwɛ asɔre mmaa wɔ aman pii so.

## Fa asɔre baa ka ho

Mia **+ Asɔre Baa Foforɔ** (Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara) na hyɛ mu:

| Beaeɛ | Ɛho hia | Nsɛm |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Asɔre baa din | Aane | Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ soronko wɔ ɔmantam no mu |
| Ɔmantam | Aane | Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛwɔ hɔ; yɛ ɔmantam no kan |
| Asɔre baa su | Aane | Panin, Nkekaho, Kuo, Sukuupɔn anaa Intanɛt so |
| Kurow | Daabi | Ɛpue wɔ kaad no so |
| Adres | Daabi | Abɔnten adres nyinaa |
| Telefon | Daabi | Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ soronko wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa mu |
| Email | Daabi | Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ soronko wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa mu |
| Ɛda a wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ | Daabi | Abakɔsɛm nkrataa |

## Yi asɔre baa firi adwuma mu

Sɛ woyi asɔre baa firi adwuma mu a, ɛyɛ brɛoo yie: asɔrefoɔ nkrataa nyinaa, mmaeɛ nsɛm ne abakɔsɛm nyinaa wɔ hɔ. Asɔre baa no firi nhwɛbea a ɛreyɛ adwuma mu nanso ɛda so wɔ nsɛm korabea no mu.

Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara na wɔtumi yi asɔre baa firi adwuma mu.

## M'asɔre Baa

Asɔrefoɔ ne asɔfoɔ kɔ wɔn ankasa deɛ so tee firi **M'asɔre Baa** link a ɛwɔ nkyɛnmu no so. Saa kratafa yi kyerɛ asɔre baa no nsɛm, akannifoɔ a wɔwɔ hɔ seesei, ne ntɛm nneyɛeɛ link.

### Asɔre Baa Nsɛm

Ɛkyerɛ kurow, adres, telefon, email, asɔrefoɔ dodoɔ nyinaa, ne sɛ ɛreyɛ adwuma anaa.

### Akannifoɔ a Wɔwɔ Hɔ Seesei

Ɛkyerɛ Ɔsɔfoɔ Panin a ɔwɔ hɔ seesei ne Asomfoɔ biara a wɔde wɔn ama asɔre baa no, ne wɔn din ne wɔn dibea. Asɔre baa wɔ Ɔsɔfoɔ Panin baako pɛ berɛ bi mu; Asomfoɔ ka ne ho.

Asɔre baa akannifoɔ yɛ **dibea**, ɛnyɛ kwan a wɔma. Ɛkyerɛw hwan na ɔkura gyinabea bɛn. Adeɛ baako pɛ a ɛde ba ne ahobammɔ kwan: Ɔsɔfoɔ Panin a ɔwɔ hɔ seesei hunu n'asɔre baa [ahobammɔ nkrataa](/tw/platform/concerns) ntɛm ara, na Ɔsomfoɔ deɛ ɔnhunu, sɛ wɔn mmienu kura asɔre baa akyɛdeɛ korɔ no ara mpo a.

### Ntɛm Nneyɛeɛ

Bɔtin a ɛde wo kɔ **Hwɛ Asɔrefoɔ**, **Hwɛ Fekuo** ne **Amanneɛbɔ** ma asɔre baa no.
