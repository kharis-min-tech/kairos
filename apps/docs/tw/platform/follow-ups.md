# Nkɔdi Akyi

<SectionClip slug="platform/follow-ups" />

Nkɔdi akyi yɛ nkrataa a ɛfa ɔhwɛ nkitahodie ho: hwan na ɔfrɛeɛ, ɔkwan bɛn so, deɛ ɛsiiɛ, ne deɛ ɛsɛ sɛ ɛba akyire. **Nkɔdi Akyi** kratafa a ɛwɔ nkyekyɛmu no mu no yɛ asɔre baa no adwuma nhwɛmu a ɛkyerɛ nnipa a obiara nnuruu wɔn nkyɛn ɛ.

## Baabi a nkɔdi akyi te

Nkɔdi akyi biara bata onipa bi ho, ne **tebea** bi nso ho: baabi a nkitahodie no siiɛ. Mmiɛnsa wɔ hɔ:

| Tebea | Bere a wɔde di dwuma |
|---------|-----------|
| Fekuo | Fekuo kannifoɔ a ɔredi ne fekuo muni bi akyi. Wɔkyerɛw firi fekuo no mu. |
| Adwumakuo | Adwumakuo kannifoɔ anaa ɔboafoɔ a ɔredi ne adwumakuo muni bi akyi. Wɔkyerɛw firi adwumakuo no mu. |
| Asɔre baa | Asɔre baa no so ɔhwɛ nkitahodie, a kuo biara nka ho. Wɔkyerɛw firi Nkɔdi Akyi kratafa anaa ɔmanfoɔ no nkrataa so. |

Asɔre baa tebea no na ɛsesaa nnansa yi, na ɛsesaa biribi a ɛho hia: **obi a ɔnni fekuo biara anaa adwumakuo biara mu no, seesei wɔtumi di n'akyi.** Kane no, na ɛsɛ sɛ nkɔdi akyi bata kuo bi ho, enti nnipa a na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔn nsa ka nkitahodie paa — wɔn a na obiara ntwee wɔn mmaeɛ — na nhyehyɛeɛ no ntumi nkyerɛw wɔn ho hwee.

Obi a ɔkyerɛw nkɔdi akyi no na ɔka tebea no. Kairos nsusu no mfiri nnipa no kuo mu tenaeɛ so, ɛfiri sɛ baabi a nkɔmmɔdie bi siiɛ yɛ nkɔmmɔdie no ho asɛm, ɛnyɛ onipa no ho asɛm.

## Nhwɛmu mmiɛnsa

Bue **Nkɔdi Akyi** na wobɛnya tab mmiɛnsa. Ɛyɛ nhwɛmu soronko mmiɛnsa na ɛnyɛ baako, ɛfiri sɛ ɛyɛ adwuma ahodoɔ a wɔn bere nsɛ.

### Ahɔhoɔ foforɔ

Obiara a ɔfaa **Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ** krataa so wɔ w'asɔre baa mu wɔ **nna 60 a atwam** mu na **wɔnkyerɛwee nkɔdi akyi biara mmaa no**, deɛ ɛyɛ foforɔ kane.

Yei yɛ ahɔhoɔgyeeɛ a ne bere yɛ nnɔnhwere anaa nna. Nhwɛmu no kyerɛ hwan na ɔtoo nsa frɛɛ wɔn, ne baabi a wɔkyerɛeɛ, sɛdeɛ onipa a ɔrefrɛ no bɛnya baabi afiri aseɛ.

Ɔhɔhoɔ foforɔ firi nhwɛmu yi mu bere a obiara kyerɛw nkɔdi akyi bi fa ne ho, tebea biara mu. Nhwɛmu no bisa sɛ "obi ne no akasa anaa", na ɛnyɛ "obi ne no akasa wɔ ɔkwan a ɛfata so anaa".

### Kuo biara nni hɔ

Nnipa a wɔwɔ hɔ wɔ w'asɔre baa mu na wɔnni **fekuo biara anaa adwumakuo biara** mu, nkrataa foforɔ kane.

Yei yɛ adwuma a ɛkɔ brɛoo nanso ɛkɔ so: obi a ɔba nanso ɔmmata biribiara ho. Wɔyi mmɔfra firi mu, ɛfiri sɛ abɔfra ho nkitahodie fa ne hwɛfoɔ so.

Sɛ ɛte sɛ ahɔhoɔ foforɔ nhwɛmu no a, yei deɛ nkɔdi akyi a wobɛkyerɛw no nyi obi mfiri mu. Obi firi mu bere a ɔde ne ho ka kuo bi ho ankasa, na ɛno ne deɛ nhwɛmu no wɔ hɔ sɛ ɛbɛyɛ.

### Ɛduru so

Nkɔdi akyi a obi hyɛɛ **nkɔdi akyi a ɛdi so ɛda** a seesei aduru anaa atwam, wɔ w'asɔre baa mu, ma nnipa a wɔda so wɔ hɔ.

Yei ne nhwɛmu a ɛdi bɔhyɛ so. Sɛ wokyerɛw nkɔdi akyi na wohyɛ ɛda a ɛdi so a, ɛha na ɛsan ba wo nkyɛn.

## Kyerɛw nkɔdi akyi

Nkɔdi akyi yɛ **nkitahodie** anaa **nsrahwɛ**, na krataa no sesa ɛno so.

**Mmienu nyinaa kyerɛw:**
- Akwan a wofaa so, kɔsi nsia, sɛdeɛ "mefrɛeɛ, mekyerɛwee, na mede WhatsApp kɔeɛ" bɛyɛ nkrataa baako na ɛnyɛ mmiɛnsa
- Simma dodoɔ
- Nsɛm
- Nkɔdi akyi a ɛdi so ɛda, sɛ wopɛ a, a ɛde onipa no kɔ Ɛduru so nhwɛmu mu sɛ ɛduru a

**Nkitahodie kyerɛw nso:**
- Sɛ wonyaa no ankasa anaa
- N'anigyeɛ gyinabea: n'ani gye ho, n'ani nnye ho, anaa ɔnsii gyinae

**Nsrahwɛ kyerɛw nso:**
- Anim ne anim anaa intanɛt so
- Sɛ wɔbɔɔ ho amaneɛ kane anaa
- Bere a wobaeɛ ne bere a wofiriiɛ
- Deɛ ɛsiiɛ: na ɔwɔ hɔ, na ɔnni hɔ, anaa wɔsesaa ɛda no
- Hwan ne wo kɔeɛ

## Agyiraeɛ mmienu

Wɔtumi hyɛ nkɔdi akyi biara agyiraeɛ sɛ **mmoa asɛm** anaa **ahobammɔ asɛm**, mmienu no mu biara ne ne ho. Mmienu, baako, anaa biara.

Wɔyɛ nneɛma soronko mmienu hyɛ da, ɛnyɛ adeɛ korɔ gyinabea mmienu:

- **Mmoa** yɛ ɔhwɛ. Obi reperepe, obi ayi, obi yare, obi wɔ ɔhaw mu.
- **Ahobammɔ** yɛ banbɔ. Biribi rema abɔfra anaa ɔpanin a ɔyɛ mmerɛ ho asɛm so.

Sɛ wohyɛ emu biara agyiraeɛ a, ɛde nkɔdi akyi no kɔ [Nsɛm a Ɛhaw](/tw/platform/concerns) nkrataa beaeɛ a ɛfata mu, na nnipa soronko na wɔkenkan. Nkɔdi akyi tumi nya agyiraeɛ mmienu nyinaa na ɛbɛpue beaeɛ mmienu no nyinaa mu.

::: warning Hyɛ no agyiraeɛ; mfa nkyerɛkyerɛ mu kɛkɛ
Ahobammɔ asɛm a wotwerɛ wɔ nkɔdi akyi a ɛnni agyiraeɛ nsɛm mu nko ara no nnuru obiara nkyɛn. Agyiraeɛ no na ɛde kɔ.
:::

## Hwan na ɔtumi di nhwɛmu no ho dwuma

| Dwumadie | Ɔmanfoɔ | Kannifoɔ | Ɔsɔfoɔ | Sohwɛfoɔ |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Bue Nkɔdi Akyi kratafa | Daabi | Daabi | Aane | Aane |
| Ahɔhoɔ foforɔ, kuo-nni-hɔ, ne ɛduru-so nhwɛmu | Daabi | Daabi | Ɔno ara asɔre baa | Asɔre baa biara |
| Kyerɛw asɔre baa tebea nkɔdi akyi | Daabi | Daabi | Ɔno ara asɔre baa | Aane |
| Kyerɛw fekuo anaa adwumakuo nkɔdi akyi | Daabi | Ɔno ara kuo | Aane | Aane |
| Kenkan mmoa nkrataa beaeɛ | Daabi | Daabi | Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ nko | Aane |
| Kenkan ahobammɔ nkrataa beaeɛ | Daabi | Daabi | Sɔfopanin anaa Ahobammɔ Kannifoɔ nko | Aane |

Nhwɛmu mmiɛnsa no yɛ asɔre baa so adwuma, enti ɛhia asɔre baa so tumi. Nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ tumi hwɛ asɔre baa biara nhwɛmu; obiara foforɔ hunu ɔno ara deɛ nko ara.
