## Dodoɔ atitire

Dodoɔ nnan pue wɔ atifi, na deɛ wobɛhunu gyina tumi a wowɔ so.

**Sɛ wowɔ asɔre baa so tumi anaa deɛ ɛkorɔn sene saa a:**

| Dodoɔ | Nkyerɛkyerɛmu |
|------|-------------|
| Asɔremufoɔ Foforɔ | Asɔremufoɔ a wɔde kaa ho wɔ bere a woyiiɛ mu, ne bosome ne bosome nsakraeɛ |
| Mmaeɛ Dodoɔ Mfimfini | Ɔsom mmaeɛ dodoɔ mfimfini, ne nkɔsoɔ nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ |
| Akra a Wɔduruu Wɔn Nkyɛn | Akra a wɔnyaa wɔn wɔ asɛmpatrɛw mu nyinaa, ne nsakraeɛ dodoɔ |
| Nkɔanim Abosome | Abosome dodoɔ a asɔre no kɔɔ anim a anto mu |

**Sɛ wonni bi a:**

| Dodoɔ | Nkyerɛkyerɛmu |
|------|-------------|
| Me Fekuo | Dodoɔ a woakɔ mu |
| Me Mmaeɛ | W'ankasa mmaeɛ dodoɔ nnansa yi |
| M'Akra | Akra a w'ankasa wonyaa wɔn |
| Nhyiamu a Mekɔeɛ | Nhyiamu a wokɔeɛ wɔ nna 30 a atwam mu |

## Tab ahodoɔ

Fa tab a ɛwɔ atifi nifa no sesa nkaeɛbɔ su. Tab a wobɛnya gyina wo tumi so: akannifoɔ hunu **Mmaeɛ**, **Nkɔanim**, ne **Asɛmpatrɛw**; obiara foforɔ hunu **Mmaeɛ** ne **Asɛmpatrɛw**.

- **Mmaeɛ**: ɔsom mmaeɛ wɔ bere mu
- **Nkɔanim**: asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ ne wɔn a wɔaba foforɔ
- **Asɛmpatrɛw**: akra a wɔnyaa wɔn, nkɔdi akyi dodoɔ, ne nsakraeɛ

## Dapɛn Mmaeɛ Dodoɔ mfonini

Mfonini kɛseɛ no kyerɛ mmaeɛ dodoɔ mfimfini wɔ nnawɔtwe 8 a atwam mu sɛ nsensaneeɛ. Fa wo nsa si beaeɛ biara so na hwɛ ɛda ne ɔha nkyekyɛmu no pɔtee.

Sɛ ɛresiane a, mfonini no atifi kyerɛ **Ɛresiane** kɔkɔbɔ wɔ kɔkɔɔ mu.

## Bosome Nsɛmpɔtee beaeɛ

Nhwɛmu adaka a ɛwɔ nifa so a ɛkyerɛ deɛ ɛhia hwɛ. Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ: *"Mmaeɛ hia hwɛ. Dodoɔ mfimfini yɛ 80% wɔ nnawɔtwe 8 a atwam mu"* ne **Hwɛ emu nkyekyɛmu** bɔtin.

## Fekuo a Nnipa Kɔ Mu Pii

Mfonini a ɛkyerɛ fekuo a wɔn nhyiamu mmaeɛ korɔn sene biara. Mia fa biara so na kɔ saa fekuo no nhyiamu abakɔsɛm mu.

## Deɛ wohunu

| Hwan | Deɛ ɔhunu |
|-----|--------------|
| Sohwɛfoɔ | Asɔre no nyinaa nsɛm wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa so |
| Asɔre baa so tumi | Ɔno ara asɔre baa nko ara |
| Kuo kannifoɔ | Ɔno ara adwumakuo anaa fekuo nko ara |
| Tumi biara nni hɔ | Kratafa no a ɛfa wo ankasa ho: wo fekuo, wo mmaeɛ, w'akra, ne wo nhyiamu |

::: tip Nkaeɛbɔ nyɛ sohwɛfoɔ nko ara kratafa
Obiara tumi bue **Nkaeɛbɔ**. Deɛ ɛsesa ne deɛ ɛwɔ mu: sɛ tumi nni hɔ a, ɛbua nsɛmmisa a ɛfa wo ho, na ɛnyɛ asɔre no ho. Wo mmaeɛ abakɔsɛm a emu da hɔ — nnawɔtwe 12 dodoɔ, bere dodie, nnidisoɔ, ne ɔsom abakɔsɛm — wɔ **Me Mmaeɛ** so.
:::

## Mmaeɛ nhwɛmu a emu dɔ

Nkaeɛbɔ kratafa no ma nkɔsoɔ kɛseɛ no. Sɛ wopɛ ɔmanfoɔ biara mfonini, mmaeɛ dodoɔ akuo, ahokekafoɔ dodoɔ a wɔde di dwuma, ne ahɔhoɔ foforɔ ne wɔn a wɔsan ba nkyekyɛmu a, bue **Mmaeɛ → Nkaeɛbɔ**. Hwɛ [Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ](/tw/analytics/attendance-reports#nkaeɛbɔ-a-emu-da-hɔ).
