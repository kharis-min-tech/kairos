## Fekuo ahodoɔ

| Su | Nkyerɛkyerɛmu |
|------|-------------|
| K-Groups | Kuo ketewa Bible adesua |
| Kharis Express | Mmabunu adwumayɛfoɔ fekuo |
| New Breeds | Asɔrefoɔ foforɔ nkabom fekuo |
| KOC | Kharis on Campus |
| KOC Colleges | Kharis on Campus Colleges |

Fa su nkyekyɛmu a ɛwɔ kratafa no atifi yi mu fa su so, anaa fa **Asɔre Mmaa Nyinaa** yi fa asɔre baa so.

## Fekuo kaad

Kaad biara kyerɛ:
- Fekuo din ne ne su agyiraeɛ
- Asɔre baa din
- Nkyerɛkyerɛmu
- Nhyiam berɛ (sɛ nhwɛsoɔ "Fiada biara, 6:30 PM")
- **Yi Firi Adwuma Mu** bɔtin (Asɔfoɔ ne Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara)

## Hwehwɛ fekuo wɔ mfonini so

Mia **Hwehwɛ wɔ Mfonini so** na bue asase mfonini no. Wɔde fekuo si wɔn asɔre baa beaeɛ so, a kɔla kyerɛ ne su.

### Mfonini no so nsɛm

Mia agyiraeɛ biara a ɛwɔ mfonini no so na hunu fekuo no din, n'asɔre baa, ne nhyiam a ɛdi hɔ ɛda.

### Fekuo a ɛbɛn wo

Fa posu nɔma anaa ɔman hwehwɛ deɛ ɛbɛn wo. Kairos kyerɛ:

- Ɛkwan tenten wɔ km mu
- Berɛ a wobɛfa wɔ nantew, bɔs, kaa ne keteke mu
- Link a wode nya ɔkwan wɔ Maps anaa Waze so
- Fekuo ahodoɔ a ɛwɔ saa beaeɛ hɔ

## Fekuo akontaa

Fekuo kratafa no atifi nifa fa kyerɛ fekuo dodoɔ nyinaa ne dodoɔ a ɛreyɛ adwuma seesei.

## Hwan na ɔhunu deɛn

| Dwumadie | Ɔmanfoɔ | Kannifoɔ | Ɔsɔfoɔ | Sohwɛfoɔ |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Hwɛ fekuo nhwɛmu | Aane | Aane | Aane | Aane |
| Fa mfonini di dwuma | Aane | Aane | Aane | Aane |
| Yɛ fekuo foforɔ | Daabi | Daabi | Aane | Aane |
| Yi fekuo firi adwuma mu | Daabi | Daabi | Aane | Aane |
| Hwɛ fekuo mufoɔ so | Daabi | Ne dea nko | Aane | Aane |
| Kyerɛw nhyiam mmaeɛ | Daabi | Ne dea nko | Aane | Aane |
