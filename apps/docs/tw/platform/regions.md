## Deɛ mpɔtam yɛ

Mpɔtam ma wotumi kyekyɛ asɔre mmaa mu fa asase so: sɛ nhwɛsoɔ, UK asɔre mmaa nyinaa wɔ ɔmantam baako mu na Ghana deɛ wɔ foforɔ mu. Yei boa nyiyimu bere a wowɔ asɔre mmaa wɔ aman pii so.

Kairos de mpɔtam mmiɛnsa di dwuma ma Kharis Church:
- **Greater Accra**: Ghana
- **United Kingdom**: United Kingdom
- **Western Area**: Sierra Leone

## Fa ɔmantam ka ho

Mia **+ Ɔmantam Foforɔ** (Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara) na fa:

| Beaeɛ | Ɛho hia |
|-------|----------|
| Ɔmantam din | Aane |
| Ɔman | Aane |

Ɛsɛ sɛ ɔmantam din yɛ soronko wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no nyinaa mu.

## Sesa ɔmantam

Mia ɔmantam kaad biara so na sesa ne din anaa ne ɔman.

## Yi ɔmantam firi hɔ

Wobɛtumi ayi ɔmantam bi afiri hɔ bere a asɔre baa biara nni ne ase. Sɛ asɔre mmaa wɔ saa ɔmantam no mu a, yi wɔn firi adwuma mu anaa fa wɔn kɔ baabi foforɔ kan.

## Hwan na ɔtumi hwɛ mpɔtam so

**Sohwɛfoɔ** nko ara na wɔtumi yɛ, sesa anaa yi mpɔtam. Asɔfoɔ ne asɔrefoɔ ntumi nkɔ Mpɔtam kratafa no so.
