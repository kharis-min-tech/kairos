## Asomdwuma ahodoɔ

Wɔyɛ adwumakuo firi asomdwuma ahodoɔ **nhwɛmu** mu na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔtwerɛ din, sɛdeɛ asomdwuma korɔ no ara bɛkyerɛ adeɛ korɔ wɔ asɔre baa biara mu. Nhwɛmu no yɛ deɛ wɔhwɛ so; nkyekyɛmu a ɛwɔ krataa no so na ɛwɔ tumi wɔ w'asɔre mu bere biara.

Yeinom ne ahodoɔ a Kairos ba a ɛka ho:

| Asomdwuma | Nkyerɛkyerɛmu |
|----------|-------------|
| Sohwɛ | Ɔsom ɛda nkrataa ne ahɔhoɔ foforɔ nkyerɛwee |
| Nnwomtofoɔ | Nnwom asomdwuma ne ɔsom |
| Nsiesiei | Mfonini ne adwinneɛ |
| Agorɔ | Agorɔ ne asɛnnie |
| Nkɔdi Akyi Kuo | Wɔfrɛ ahɔhoɔ foforɔ akyire, ne asɔrefoɔ a wɔnni fekuo anaa adwumakuo mu |
| Ahɔhoyɛ | Aduane, anonneɛ ne ahɔhoɔ hwɛ |
| Ahɔhoɔ Gyee Kuo | Wɔgye ahɔhoɔ foforɔ ɛda no ara |
| Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ | Wɔn a wɔasakra foforɔ hwɛ ne asuafoyɛ |
| Nsiesieeɛ | Asɛnnie nsiesieeɛ ne kanea |
| Asɔredan Hwɛfoɔ | Beaeɛ no hohoro ne nsiesieeɛ |
| Soshal Midia | Intanɛt so adwuma ne nsɛm |
| Nnyegyeeɛ | Nnyegyeeɛ ho adwuma |
| Akwankyerɛfoɔ | Apono, atenaeɛ ne nhyehyɛeɛ |
| Mmoa | Ɔhwɛ ne mmoa |

::: tip Ahɔhoɔ Gyee Kuo ne Nkɔdi Akyi Kuo nyɛ adwuma korɔ
Ahɔhoɔ Gyee Kuo gye obi bere a ɔwɔ ɔdan no mu. Nkɔdi Akyi Kuo frɛ no akyire, na wɔsan di asɔrefoɔ a wɔnka kuo biara ho no nso akyi. Wɔyɛ adwumakuo soronko mmienu hyɛ da, ɛfiri sɛ ɛyɛ adwuma soronko mmienu a ɛhia nimdeɛ soronko. Hwɛ [Nkɔdi Akyi](/tw/platform/follow-ups).
:::

Fa **Asɔre Mmaa Nyinaa** nkyekyɛmu no yi mu bere a wohwɛ mmeaeɛ pii so.

## Adwumakuo kaad

Kaad biara kyerɛ:
- Adwumakuo din ne asɔre baa
- Kannifoɔ din
- Mufoɔ a wɔreyɛ adwuma dodoɔ
- Nkyerɛkyerɛmu
- **Yi Firi Adwuma Mu** bɔtin (Asɔfoɔ ne Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara)
- **Ɛretwɛn** agyiraeɛ sɛ abisadeɛ bi retwɛn ɔpene a

## Ka adwumakuo ho

Ɔmanfoɔ tumi bisa sɛ ɔbɛka adwumakuo bi ho firi ne nsɛm anaa adwumakuo kratafa no so. Abisadeɛ no kɔ adwumakuo kannifoɔ no nkyɛn ma ɔpene so. Ɔmanfoɔ tumi ka **adwumakuo mmienu** pɛ ho berɛ korɔ mu. Nhyehyɛeɛ no bɔ kannifoɔ kɔkɔ sɛ ɔmanfoɔ no wɔ mmienu dada, nanso sohwɛfoɔ tumi twa mu.

## Abisadeɛ a ɛretwɛn

Bere a adwumakuo kaad kyerɛ **ɛretwɛn** agyiraeɛ a, kannifoɔ no wɔ abisadeɛ a ɔnnyee so.

## Adwumakuo mufoɔ nhwɛmu

Akannifoɔ tumi hunu wɔn adwumakuo mufoɔ nyinaa, wɔn nkitahodie nsɛm, ɛda a wɔbɛkaa ho ne nkɔdi akyi nsɛm.

## Hwan na ɔtumi hwɛ adwumakuo so

| Dwumadie | Ɔmanfoɔ | Kannifoɔ | Ɔsɔfoɔ | Sohwɛfoɔ |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Hwɛ adwumakuo nhwɛmu | Aane | Aane | Aane | Aane |
| Yɛ adwumakuo foforɔ | Daabi | Daabi | Aane | Aane |
| Yi adwumakuo firi adwuma mu | Daabi | Daabi | Aane | Aane |
| Pene abisadeɛ so | Daabi | Ne dea nko | Aane | Aane |
| Hwɛ mufoɔ nhwɛmu | Daabi | Ne dea nko | Aane | Aane |
| Kyerɛw nkɔdi akyi nsɛm | Daabi | Ne dea nko | Aane | Aane |
