## Ɔsom nhwɛmu

Nsɛntitire biara kyerɛ:
- Ɔsom su agyiraeɛ (sɛ ɛbɛyɛ **Kwasida**)
- Ɔsom din ne ne nsɛmti
- Ɛda, asɔre baa, ne asɛnka din
- **Kyerɛw Wo Din →** nkitahodie a ɛbue ɔsom no mmaeɛ nkrataa

## Yiyi mu

Yiyi ɔsom nhwɛmu no mu fa:
- **Su**: Nyinaa, Kwasida Ɔsom, Nnawɔtwe Mfimfini Ɔsom, Ɔsom Soronko
- **Ɛfiri / Kɔsi**: Nna dodoɔ

## Kyerɛw ɔsom

Mia **+ Kyerɛw ɔsom** (Asɔfoɔ ne Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara):

| Beaeɛ | Nsɛm |
|-------|-------|
| Asɔre baa | Ɛho hia |
| Ɔsom su | Kwasida, Nnawɔtwe Mfimfini, Soronko, Mpaeɛbɔ, Foforɔ |
| Ɛda | Ɛho hia |
| Ɔsom din / nsɛmti | Ɛho nhia |
| Ɔsɛnkafoɔ | Ɛho nhia; fa bata ɔmanfoɔ bi ho |
| Asɛnka nsɛmti | Ɛho nhia |
| Dodoɔ a wohwɛ kwan | Ɛho nhia |

## Kyerɛw mufoɔ din

Sɛ woyɛ ɔsom no wie a, bue no fa **Kyerɛw Wo Din →** so. Hyɛ ɔmanfoɔ biara sɛ:
- **Ɔwɔ hɔ**
- **Ɔnni hɔ**
- **Intanɛt so**

Wotumi kyerɛ nso sɛ ɔmanfoɔ bi yɛ **Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ** wɔ bere a woreyɛ nkrataa no.

Sɛ wobɛkyerɛw ɔsom mmaeɛ a, ɛsɛ sɛ wowɔ asɔre baa no so tumi, anaa woyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no sohwɛfoɔ.

## Nkaeɛbɔ

Sɛ wopɛ nkaeɛbɔ a emu da hɔ — ɔmanfoɔ biara ne mfonini, mmaeɛ dodoɔ akuo, ne ahɔhoɔ foforɔ ne wɔn a wɔsan ba mu nkyekyɛmu — bue **Nkaeɛbɔ** firi Mmaeɛ kratafa no so. Hwɛ [Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ](/tw/analytics/attendance-reports#nkaeɛbɔ-a-emu-da-hɔ).

## Kyerɛw wo din ankasa

Ɔmanfoɔ tumi kyerɛw wɔn din ankasa, sɛdeɛ ɛnyɛ ahɔhoɔgyeeɛ pon nko ara na ɛkɔ nkrataa no so. **Kyerɛw wo din** wɔ obiara nkyekyɛmu mu.

Akwan mmienu wɔ hɔ:

- **Mia na kyerɛw wo din.** Mia baako kyerɛw ɔmanfoɔ no wɔ ɔsom a ne bere so abue seesei no mu.
- **Kenkan pon no so QR kod.** Sohwɛfoɔ bue ɔsom no na ɔkyerɛ **Kyerɛw wo din QR** wɔ skrin so wɔ pon no ho. Kod no sesa simma 30 biara, na ɔmanfoɔ no kenkan firi **Kyerɛw wo din → Kenkan QR wɔ pon no ho**. Kod a ɛdii kan no da so yɛ adwuma kakra wɔ nsakraeɛ akyi, sɛdeɛ obi a ɔrekenkan no bɛtumi awie.

Kod a ɛsesa no na ɛsi ɔmanfoɔ kwan sɛ ɔbɛkyerɛw ne din afiri efie: mfonini a wotwaa Kwasida a ɛtwaam no nyɛ adwuma bio.

### Asɔre baa nhyehyɛeɛ

Asɔre baa biara hwɛ ne kyerɛw-wo-din-ankasa so firi **Me Asɔre Baa → Mmaeɛ nhyehyɛeɛ**:

| Nhyehyɛeɛ | Deɛ ɛyɛ |
|---------|--------------|
| Kyerɛw wo din ankasa abue | Ɔkwan kɛseɛ no. Sɛ ɛto mu a, akwan mmienu no mu biara nyɛ adwuma wɔ asɔre baa yi mu. |
| Ɛbue ansa na afiri aseɛ | Simma dodoɔ a ɛdi ɔsom no anim na bere no bue |
| Ɛto mu wɔ mfiaseɛ akyi | Simma dodoɔ a ɛdi mfiaseɛ no akyi na bere no to mu |
| Akyire yi yɛ **Akyire** | Simma dodoɔ a ɛdi mfiaseɛ no akyi na wɔkyerɛw sɛ **Akyire** na ɛnyɛ **Ɔwɔ hɔ** |

Sɛ wokyerɛw wo din mprenu a, ɛnyɛ nkrataa mmienu, na sɛɛva no na ɛhyɛ Akyire bere no, enti ɛyɛ pɛ ɔkwan biara a wofaa so.

## Me Mmaeɛ

Ɔmanfoɔ biara wɔ ne **Me Mmaeɛ** kratafa wɔ nkyekyɛmu no mu. Ɛyɛ kokoamsɛm: ɔmanfoɔ no, ne sɔfoɔ, ne sohwɛfoɔ kuo no nko ara na wɔhunu.

### Deɛ ɛkyerɛ

- **Mmaeɛ Dodoɔ**: ɔha nkyekyɛmu wɔ nnawɔtwe 12 a atwam mu, ne nkuranhyɛ asɛm
- **Deɛ wobaeɛ ne ne nyinaa**: sɛ ɛbɛyɛ "wobaa ɔsom 2 wɔ 3 mu"
- **Bere a wohunuu wo**: ɛda ne ɔsom a wobaeɛ akyire pɛɛ

### Bere dodie nkyekyɛmu

Mfonini a ɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ w'asomdwuma nkyekyɛm wɔ Ɔwɔ hɔ, Akyire, Intanɛt so, ne Ɔyeraeɛ ntam.

### Nnidisoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei

Ɔsom dodoɔ a woakɔ a anto mu.

### Ɔsom abakɔsɛm

Ɔsom biara a ɛwɔ nnawɔtwe 12 no mu nhwɛmu, deɛ ɛdi kan kane, ne wo mmaeɛ tebea wɔ biara ho (Ɔyeraeɛ, Ɔwɔ hɔ, Intanɛt so, Akyire).
