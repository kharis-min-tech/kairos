## Ɔkwan no afa

Akra fa afa nnan so:

| Fa | Aseɛ |
|-------|---------|
| Foforɔ | Wɔnyaa no seesei; nkɔdi akyi biara nni hɔ |
| Yɛredi Akyi | Wɔne no akasa |
| N'ani Gye Ho | Ɔrekyerɛ sɛ n'ani gye gyidie no ho ankasa |
| Wasakra | Wasi gyinae; wasiesie ne ho ama Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ nkrataa |

Nsɛm a ɛtɔ so num, **N'ani Nnye Ho**, firi ɔkwan a ɛrekɔ so no mu nanso ɛka nkrataa no mu.

## Akra kaad

Kaad biara a ɛwɔ ɔkwan no mu kyerɛ:
- Ɔkra no din ne ne telefon nɔma
- Adwumayɛfoɔ a wɔde maa no (ne din nnyinasoɔ)
- Ɛda a wɔne no kasaeɛ akyire pɛɛ anaa "Nkɔdi akyi biara nni hɔ"
- Tebea nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ (ne kɔla kyerɛ sɛdeɛ nkɔdi akyi no rekɔ)

## Gye ɔkra foforɔ

Mia **+ Gye Ɔkra** na fa obi ka ɔkwan no ho:

| Beaeɛ | Nsɛm |
|-------|-------|
| Din a ɛdi kan, Abusua din | Ɛho hia |
| Telefon | Ɛyɛ ma nkɔdi akyi |
| Imel | Ɛho nhia |
| Ɔbaa anaa ɔbarima | Ɛho nhia |
| Mfeɛ dodoɔ | Ɛho nhia |
| Asɛmpatrɛw nhyehyɛeɛ | Fa bata nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔhyiaa no wɔ mu ho |
| Akyerɛwdeɛ / Kuropɔn | Ɛho nhia |
| Nsɛm | Mfitiaseɛ nsɛm biara |

Ɔkra no kɔ ɔmanfoɔ a ɔgyee no no nkyɛn ne ho ara. Wotumi de ma adwumayɛfoɔ foforɔ sɛ ɛho hia a.

## Kyerɛw nkɔdi akyi

Bue ɔkra kaad bi na fa nkɔdi akyi ka ho:

- Ɛda ne bere
- Ɔkwan a wofaa so: Telefon Frɛ, Nkrasɛm, Imel, WhatsApp, Nsrahwɛ, Foforɔ
- Deɛ ɛsiiɛ: Ɛyɛɛ yie, Obiara Annye So, Nɔma a Ɛnteɛ, Frɛ Akyire, N'ani Nnye Ho, N'ani Gye Ho
- Simma dodoɔ
- Nsɛm
- Nkɔdi akyi a ɛdi so ɛda

## Yiyi ɔkwan no mu

| Nyiyimu | Deɛ ɛwɔ hɔ |
|--------|---------|
| Asɔre baa | Asɔre Mmaa Nyinaa anaa baako pɔtee (Sohwɛfoɔ nko) |
| Fekuo | Me Fekuo anaa baako pɔtee |
| Adwumakuo | M'adwumakuo anaa baako pɔtee |
| Adwumayɛfoɔ | Obiara anaa ɔmanfoɔ pɔtee |
| Baabi a ɛfiriɛ | Asɛmpatrɛw Nhyehyɛeɛ Nyinaa anaa baako pɔtee |

Hyehyɛ fa **Ɛda a Wɔde Kaa Ho**, **Din**, anaa **Nkitahodie a Ɛtwa Toɔ** so.

## Nkɔdi akyi apɔwmuden nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ

- **Ɛmu yɛ den**: nkitahodie biara nni hɔ nna 7+ mu
- **Hwɛ so**: wɔne no akasa nanso nkɔsoɔ biara nni hɔ
- **Ɛrekɔ yie**: nkɔdi akyi foforɔ a ɛrebɔ aba

## Nkɔ Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ mu

Sɛ wɔkyerɛ ɔkra bi sɛ **Wasakra** a, nkrataa yɛ ne ho ara wɔ [Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ](/tw/platform/new-believers) ɔkwan no mu.
