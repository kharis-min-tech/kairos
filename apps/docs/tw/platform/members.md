# Asɔrefoɔ

<SectionClip slug="platform/members" />

Asɔrefoɔ kratafa no ne asɔre no nhwɛmu. Ɛkyerɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ w'asɔre baa mu (anaa asɔre mmaa nyinaa sɛ woyɛ Sohwɛfoɔ a) ne wɔn gyinabea, nkitahodie nsɛm ne fekuo a wɔwɔ mu.

::: tip Hwan na ɔwɔ nhwɛmu no mu
Nhwɛmu no fa **Asɔrefoɔ a wɔahyɛ mu den** (wɔn a wɔawie [asɔrefoɔ adesua](/tw/platform/membership)) ne **Asanfoɔ** (wɔn a wɔba nanso wɔnyɛɛ adesua no) ho. Ahɔhoɔ ne mmɔfra nkrataa, a Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ krataa anaa ahobammɔ nhyehyɛeɛ na ɛtaa yɛ no, tena wɔn ankasa mmeaeɛ, sɛdeɛ nkrataa no renyɛ ma nsensaneɛ a wɔntumi nhyɛ mu. Fa nyiyimu no so yi baako.
:::

## Asɔrefoɔ nhwɛmu

Kaad biara kyerɛ:
- Edin ne agyiraeɛ nkyerɛwee
- Asɔre baa a ɔwɔ mu
- Email ne telefon
- Ɔpene gyinabea agyiraeɛ (Wɔagye no, Ɔretwɛn)
- **Yi Firi Adwuma Mu** bɔtin, ma obiara a ɔwɔ asɔre baa akyɛdeɛ

## Hwehwɛ ne nyiyimu

Fa hwehwɛbea no so hwehwɛ obi fa din anaa email so. Fa nkyɛmu no yiyi mu:

- **Fekuo Nyinaa**: te so ma ɛyɛ fekuo pɔtee bi mufoɔ
- **Asɔre baa**: yi fa asɔre baa so (Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara)
- **Gyinabea Nyinaa**: yi fa Wɔagye no, Ɔretwɛn anaa Ɔnni adwuma mu so

## Fa ɔmanfoɔ ka ho

Mia **+ Fa Ɔmanfoɔ Ka Ho** na fa obi ka ho w'ankasa. Nsɛm a ɛho hia:

| Beaeɛ | Nsɛm |
|-------|-------|
| Edin a ɛdi kan, Abusua din | Ɛho hia |
| Email | Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ soronko |
| Telefon | Ɛyɛ papa sɛ wode gu mu |
| Asɔre baa | Ɛho hia; ɛsi ne beaeɛ |
| Ɔbarima anaa ɔbaa | Ɔbarima anaa Ɔbaa |
| Awoda | Ɛho nhia |
| Adres | Ɛho nhia |
| Obi a wɔbɛfrɛ no ntɛm | Edin, telefon, ne abusuabɔ |

Asɔrefoɔ a obi a ɔwɔ asɔre baa akyɛdeɛ de wɔn ka ho no, wɔgye wɔn to mu ntɛm ara.

## W'ankasa nkyerɛwee

Asɔrefoɔ tumi kyerɛw wɔn din firi nhyɛmu kratafa no so. Wɔfiri aseɛ wɔ **Ɔretwɛn** gyinabea mu na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔgye wɔn to mu ansa na wɔanya kwan nyinaa. Obi a ɔretwɛn no tumi hunu n'ankasa ne nsɛm nko ara.

## Ɔpene Nhwɛmu

**Ɔpene Nhwɛmu** bɔtin a ɛwɔ Asɔrefoɔ kratafa atifi no kyerɛ nkyerɛwee a ɛretwɛn nyinaa. Mia no na hwɛ na gye anaa pow abisadeɛ biara. Hwɛ [Ɔpene](/tw/administration/approvals) na hunu pii.

## Fa CSV de asɔrefoɔ ba

Mia **Fa CSV Bra** na fa asɔrefoɔ pii bra prɛko. Ɛsɛ sɛ CSV no wɔ edin a ɛdi kan, abusua din, email, telefon, ɔbarima anaa ɔbaa, awoda ne asɔre baa. Nhyehyɛeɛ no hwɛ nsensaneɛ biara ansa na ɛde ba, na ɛkyerɛ wo deɛ ɛbɛyɛ ne mfomsoɔ biara.

## Yi asɔrefoɔ firi mu

Mia **Yi CSV Firi Mu** na fa deɛ wohunu seesei no sie. Ɛho wɔ mfasoɔ ma amanneɛbɔ anaa nsɛm tutuo.

## Ahobammɔ nhwɛ

**Ahobammɔ nhwɛ** bɔtin bue nkrataa a ahobammɔ nsɛm anaa agyiraeɛ wɔ so.

Yei ne [Nsɛm a Ɛha Adwene](/tw/platform/concerns) nkrataa beaeɛ no nsɛ. Ahobammɔ nhwɛ fa nkrataa no ankasa ho, titiriw ahɔhoɔ ne mmɔfra nkrataa a ɛretwɛn gyinaeɛ; nsɛm a ɛha adwene no deɛ, ɛfa nkɔdi akyi a obi ahyɛ no agyiraeɛ ho. Nnipa soronko na wɔkenkan: Nsɛm a Ɛha Adwene hia ahobammɔ kwan, a asɔre baa akyɛdeɛ mma.

## Yi ɔmanfoɔ firi adwuma mu

Sɛ woyi ɔmanfoɔ firi adwuma mu a, ɛyɛ brɛoo yie. Ne nsɛm, ne mmaeɛ abakɔsɛm, nkɔdi akyi nsɛm ne akuo a ɔka ho, ne nyinaa wɔ hɔ. Ɔhwere nhyehyɛeɛ no mu kwan nanso ne nsɛm wɔ hɔ ma amanneɛbɔ.

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ woyi obi firi adwuma mu a, ɛhia asɔre baa akyɛdeɛ, anaa nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ.
