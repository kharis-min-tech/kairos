## Ɔkwan no mu tiawa

Dodoɔ mmiɛnsa wɔ soro hɔ:

| Dodoɔ | Aseɛ |
|------|---------|
| Ɛrekɔ so | Asɔremufoɔ a wɔwɔ ɔkwan no mu seesei |
| Nkɔsoɔ biara nni hɔ nna 7+ | Nkrataa a agyina; ɛhia hwɛ |
| Wɔakɔka Adwumakuo ho | Wɔawie ɔkwan no nyinaa |

## Kanban bɔd

Wɔkyerɛ ɔkwan no sɛ Kanban bɔd a ɛwɔ afa biara ho fa:

| Fa | Nkyerɛkyerɛmu |
|-------|-------------|
| Nhyiamu 1 | Gyidie Fapem |
| Nhyiamu 2 | Hwan ne Kristoni |
| Nhyiamu 3 | W'Agyeɛ Ho Adwumayɛ |
| Nhyiamu 4 | Fekubɔ Ho Hia |
| Awie | Nhyiamu nyinaa awie |
| Wɔakɔka Adwumakuo ho | Wɔakɔ asɔre asetena mu koraa |

Kaad biara kyerɛ:
- Ɔmanfoɔ din ne ɛda a ɔkɔɔ mu
- Ɔkyerɛkyerɛni a wɔde maa no (ne din nnyinasoɔ)
- Ɔkwankyerɛfoɔ a wɔde maa no (ne din nnyinasoɔ)

## Fa gyidini foforɔ hyɛ mu

Mia **+ Fa Ɔmanfoɔ Hyɛ Mu** na fa obi firi ɔkwan no ase. Sɛ wɔkyerɛ ɔkra bi sɛ wasakra wɔ [Akra Ɔkwan](/tw/platform/souls-pipeline) no mu a, nkrataa yɛ ne ho ara.

## Nhyehyɛeɛ ne ɔhwehwɛ

- Hwehwɛ ɔmanfoɔ anaa ɔkyerɛkyerɛni din wɔ ɔhwehwɛ beaeɛ no
- Hyehyɛ fa **Ɛda a Wɔde Kaa Ho**, **Din**, anaa **Dwumadie a Ɛtwa Toɔ** so
- Yiyi mu fa afa so wɔ tab no so

## Nhyiamu

Mia **Nhyiamu** bɔtin no na hwɛ adesua nhyiamu a wɔahyehyɛ so: nna, nsɛmti, mmeaeɛ, ne ɔkyerɛkyerɛni a ɔdi nhyiamu biara anim.
