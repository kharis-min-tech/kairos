# Dibea ne Akwannya

<SectionClip slug="getting-started/roles-permissions" />

Deɛ wotumi hunu na woyɛ wɔ Kairos mu no, **tumi** a ɛwɔ w'akawnt so na ɛsi gyinae, na ɛnyɛ dibea din. Dibea nsesaeɛ biara nni hɔ, na afa biara nni hɔ wɔ akawnt bue akyi a wopaw onipa a wobɛyɛ. Wɔhyɛ w'akwannya bere a wɔsiesie w'akawnt na ɛfiri wo bue a ɛdi so mu kɔ so.

## Adeɛ a ɛdi kan a ɛsɛ sɛ wote aseɛ

Kairos kora nhyehyɛeɛ dibea mmienu pɛ: `admin` ne `member`. Obiara a ɔnni nhyehyɛeɛ kuo ketewa no mu no yɛ `member`, a Sɔfopanin ka ho.

Tumi ankasa firi **tumi a wɔde ma** mu: dibea bagua a wɔato din na ɛbata asɔre baa baako, fekuo baako, adwumakuo baako, anaa asɔre no nyinaa ho. Sɛ wokura Fekuo Kannifoɔ tumi ma Grace K-Group a, wotumi hwɛ Grace K-Group so. Sɛ wonkura hwee a, wotumi hwɛ w'ankasa nkrataa so. Sɛ wokura pii a, wonya ne nyinaa, emu biara wɔ n'ankasa ɛhyeɛ mu.

::: warning "Ɔsɔfoɔ" ne "Kannifoɔ" nyɛ dibea
Ɛyɛ nsɛmfua a mfasoɔ wɔ so a ɛka tumi *ahodoɔ* a obi wɔ ho asɛm, na nkrataa yi de di dwuma saa. Nanso Ɔsɔfoɔ dibea anaa Kannifoɔ dibea nni hɔ a wɔde ma. Ɔmanfoɔ a ne nkrataa kyerɛ dibea din "Ɔsɔfoɔ" no wɔ akwannya a ne tumi de ama no pɛpɛɛpɛ, a ebia ɛnyɛ hwee.
:::

## Akwannya su nnan

Obiara a ɔwɔ asɔre mu no ka su nnan yi mu baako ho. Nkrataa yi fa edin yi di dwuma sɛ tiawa, a "hwan na ɔtumi yɛ deɛn" tebo a ɛwɔ kratafa biara so ka ho.

| Edin | Deɛ ɛkyerɛ ankasa | Baabi a ɛduru |
|-------|-----------------------|-------|
| **Ɔmanfoɔ** | Tumi biara nni hɔ | Ɔno ara nsɛm nko ara |
| **Kannifoɔ** | Fekuo Kannifoɔ, Adwumakuo Kannifoɔ, anaa Adwumakuo Ɔboafoɔ tumi | Kuo a wɔbɔɔ ne din wɔ tumi no so, na ɛnyɛ biribi foforɔ |
| **Ɔsɔfoɔ** | Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ anaa Asɔre Baa Nkrataa Sohwɛfoɔ tumi | Asɔre baa baako |
| **Sohwɛfoɔ** | `systemRole` a ɛyɛ `admin` | Biribiara, wɔ asɔre baa biara mu |

Sɛ wopɛ tumi no nyinaa nhwɛmu, a asɔre no nyinaa deɛ a ɛnka su nnan yi mu biara ho ka ho a, hwɛ [Akwannya](/tw/administration/permissions).

## Ɔmanfoɔ

Obiara firi ha aseɛ. Sɛ ɔkyerɛw ne din na wɔpene so wie a, ɔmanfoɔ tumi:

**Deɛ ɔtumi yɛ:**
- Hwɛ na ɔsesa ɔno ankasa nkrataa (din, nkitahodie, mfonini, ntɛmpɛ nsɛm)
- Hwɛ ɔno ankasa asɔremufoɔ tebea ne n'asɔre baa
- Hwɛ adwumakuo ne fekuo a ɔwɔ mu
- Hwɛ ɔno ankasa mmaeɛ abakɔsɛm wɔ **Me Mmaeɛ** so
- De nkrataa kɔ: Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ, Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ Adesua, Asubɔ, Adansedie, Abɔfra Din Tuo, Abɔfra Kyerɛ
- Hwɛ asɔremufoɔ adesua akuo na ɔkyerɛ sɛ n'ani gye ho
- Bisa sɛ ɔbɛka fekuo, adwumakuo, anaa asɛmpatrɛw nhyehyɛeɛ ho wɔ n'asɔre baa mu
- Kyerɛw ne din sɛ adwumayɛfoɔ wɔ asɛmpatrɛw nhyehyɛeɛ mu
- Yi ɔno ankasa nsɛm firi mu, na ɔbisa sɛ wɔnyi n'akawnt, firi Nhyehyɛeɛ so

**Deɛ ɔntumi nyɛ:**
- Hwɛ asɔremufoɔ afoforɔ nkrataa anaa wɔn nsɛm
- Hwɛ kuo, fekuo, anaa adwumakuo biara so
- Pene abisadeɛ so anaa ɔyɛ sohwɛ adwuma
- Bue Asɔremufoɔ nhwɛmu, nkɔdi akyi nhwɛmu, anaa Nsɛm a Ɛhaw nkrataa beaeɛ

::: tip Asɔremufoɔ a wɔretwɛn
Asɔremufoɔ a wɔretwɛn (wɔakyerɛw wɔn din nanso wɔnnpenee so) tumi hunu wɔn ankasa nkrataa nko ara kɔsi sɛ obi a ɔwɔ ɔpene tumi wɔ wɔn asɔre baa mu bɛpene akawnt no so.
:::

## Kannifoɔ

Kannifoɔ yɛ obi a wɔabɔ ne din sɛ kuo bi kannifoɔ, ɔboafoɔ kannifoɔ, anaa ɔboafoɔ. Din a wɔbɔ wɔ kuo no so na ɛyɛ tumi no; biribiara nni hɔ a wode nsa ma. N'akwannya bata saa kuo no ho pintinn. Kannifoɔ a ɔnni kuo wɔ akwannya korɔ no ara sɛ ɔmanfoɔ.

**Deɛ ɔtumi yɛ:**
- Hwɛ na ɔhwɛ n'adwumakuo anaa ne fekuo mufoɔ nhwɛmu so
- Pene abisadeɛ so anaa ɔpo ma ne kuo
- Kyerɛw nhyiamu mmaeɛ na ɔyɛ nhyiamu
- Kyerɛw nkɔdi akyi a ɛfa ne kuo mufoɔ ho
- Hwɛ ne kuo mufoɔ nkitahodie nsɛm
- Yɛ na ɔsesa asɛmpatrɛw nhyehyɛeɛ a ɔhwɛ so

**Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ ne Asɔremufoɔ ho nhunumu:**
- Fekuo anaa adwumakuo kannifoɔ hunu **ɔno ankasa nkurɔfoɔ** Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ ne asɔremufoɔ adesua nkɔsoɔ, a ɛgyina wɔn a wɔwɔ ne kuo mu so na ɛnnyina asɔre baa so

**Nkaeɛbɔ:**
- Nsɛm a ɛfa ne kuo mufoɔ nko ara ho, na ɛnyɛ asɔre baa no nyinaa

**Deɛ ɔntumi nyɛ:**
- Kɔ nsɛm a ɛwɔ ne kuo akyi so
- Yɛ asɔre mmaa, pene asɔremufoɔ dinkyerɛw so, anaa ɔde tumi ma
- Hwɛ adwumakuo anaa fekuo foforɔ mufoɔ nhwɛmu
- Kenkan mmoa anaa ahobammɔ nkrataa beaeɛ

## Ɔsɔfoɔ

"Ɔsɔfoɔ" wɔ ha kyerɛ obi a ɔkura asɔre baa so tumi. Ahodoɔ mmienu wɔ hɔ na ɛsono emu biara.

**Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ** na ɔkura asɔre baa no akwannya safoa:
- Biribiara a Asɔre Baa Nkrataa Sohwɛfoɔ tumi yɛ, ne
- Ɔde Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ ma na ɔsan gye wɔ ɔno ara asɔre baa mu
- Ɔpaw anaa ɔyi asɔre baa akannifoɔ (Sɔfopanin, Ɔsɔfoɔ)
- Ɔkenkan asɔre baa no mmoa nkrataa beaeɛ

**Asɔre Baa Nkrataa Sohwɛfoɔ** yɛ asɔre baa adwuma a safoa no nka ho:
- Hwɛ na ɔhwɛ asɔremufoɔ nyinaa so wɔ n'asɔre baa mu
- Pene asɔremufoɔ foforɔ dinkyerɛw so
- Yi asɔremufoɔ firi adwuma mu na ɔsan de wɔn ba
- Fa asɔremufoɔ nsɛm ba na ɔyi firi mu fa CSV so
- Yɛ na ɔhwɛ fekuo ne adwumakuo nyinaa a ɛwɔ n'asɔre baa mu so
- Yɛ asɛmpatrɛw nhyehyɛeɛ ma n'asɔre baa
- Kyerɛw ɔsom ne fekuo nhyiamu mmaeɛ
- Di ahɔhoɔ foforɔ, kuo-biara-nni-hɔ, ne ɛduru-so nkɔdi akyi nhwɛmu ho dwuma
- Hwɛ na ɔyi nkrataa a wɔde ba ahodoɔ nyinaa firi mu, na ɔhwɛ ahɔhoɔ a wɔada so
- Kɔ asɔre baa so nkaeɛbɔ so: asɔremufoɔ nkɔsoɔ, mmaeɛ nkɔsoɔ, fekuo adwuma, gyidifoɔ foforɔ ɔkwan

**Emu biara ntumi:**
- Nkɔ asɔre baa foforɔ biara nsɛm so
- Nyɛ, nsesa, anaa nyi asɔre mmaa firi adwuma mu
- Mma obi nnyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ
- Nhwɛ asɔre no nyinaa nhwɛmu wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa so
- Nhwɛ asɔremufoɔ adesua nhyehyɛeɛ so, a ɛyɛ asɔre no nyinaa dea

::: warning Ahobammɔ nka ho
Asɔre baa so tumi mmienu no mu biara nkura ahobammɔ ani. Asɔre baa no **Sɔfopanin** nya ne ho ara ɛfiri sɛ ɔwɔ saa dibea no mu, na **Ahobammɔ Kannifoɔ** nya denam tumi a wɔde ma so. Ɔsɔfoɔ a ɔkura Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ tumi korɔ no ara sɛ Sɔfopanin no nhunu ahobammɔ nhwɛmu. Hwɛ [Akwannya](/tw/administration/permissions#tumi-a-ɛfiri-dibea-mu-asɔre-baa-sɔfo).
:::

## Sohwɛfoɔ

Nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ wɔ akwannya nyinaa a biribiara nsi wɔn kwan. Akwannya nhwɛsoɔ biara ma wɔn kwan, wɔ asɔre baa biara mu. Yei yɛ ntɛmpɛ akwannya ma kuo ketewa a wɔhwɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no so, na ɛnyɛ dibea kɛseɛ ho nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ.

**Deɛ wɔtumi yɛ:**
- Yɛ na wɔhwɛ asɔre mmaa ne amantam so
- De tumi ma na wɔgye firi ɔmanfoɔ biara nsam wɔ asɔre baa biara mu
- Hwɛ asɔremufoɔ so wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa mu
- Hwɛ asɔre no nyinaa nhwɛmu na wɔyɛ nkaeɛbɔ a ɛtra asɔre mmaa so
- Hwɛ asɔremufoɔ adesua nhyehyɛeɛ so
- Hwɛ ɔpene nhwɛmu so wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa mu
- Siesie nhyehyɛeɛ a obiara foforɔ ntumi nnuru so

---

## Asɔre no nyinaa tumi

Tumi mmienu nni obiara asɔre baa mu, ɛfiri sɛ deɛ wɔhwɛ so no yɛ asɔre no nyinaa dea:

| Tumi | Baabi a ɛduru |
|-------|-------|
| **Asɔremufoɔ Sohwɛfoɔ** | Asɔremufoɔ adesua nhyehyɛeɛ no nyinaa: akuo, nhyiamu, anigyeɛ nhwɛmu, ɔgyeɛ, nkyerɛwee, awieeɛ. Akuo no yɛ asɔre no nyinaa dea, enti asɔre baa so tumi biara ntumi nkyerɛ tumi wɔ bi so. |
| **Ahobammɔ Panin** | Ahobammɔ wɔ asɔre mmaa nyinaa so. Ɔkwan a wɔfa so kɔ soro sene asɔre baa no Ahobammɔ Kannifoɔ, ɛfiri sɛ asɛm a ɛsɔre wɔ asɔre baa bi mu ɛtɔ da a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛfiri saa asɔre baa no mu koraa. |

Asɔre no nyinaa tumi duru asɔre baa biara so. Deɛ ɛne no bɔ abira no nyɛ nokorɛ da: sɛ wokura asɔre baa so tumi wɔ asɔre baa biara mu mpo a, ɛnsɔ asɔre no nyinaa nhwɛsoɔ ani.
