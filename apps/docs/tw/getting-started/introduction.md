# Nnianim Asɛm

Akwaaba wɔ Kairos nkyerɛkyerɛmu no mu. Saa akwankyerɛ yi ka deɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wohunu nyinaa wɔ Kairos asɔre sohwɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no ho, sɛ woyɛ asɔre muni, adwumakuo kannifoɔ, ɔsɔfoɔ anaa nhyehyɛeɛ no sohwɛfoɔ.

## Hwan na yei wɔ hɔ ma no?

Deɛ wohunu wɔ Kairos mu firi kwan a wɔama wo wɔ w’akawnt so, ɛnyɛ dibea a wɔfrɛ wo. Sɛdeɛ ɛteɛ no, obiara deɛ kɔ akwan ɛnan yi mu baako so:

- **Asɔrefoɔ**: Hwɛ w’ankasa wo nsɛm, wo mmaeɛ, wo fekuo, w’adesua nkɔsoɔ, na fa nkrataa kɔ.
- **Akannifoɔ**: Hwɛ w’adwumakuo anaa wo fekuo so, kyerɛw nkɔdi akyi, na kyerɛw mmaeɛ.
- **Asɔfoɔ**: Wohunu na wodi biribiara a ɛwɔ asɔre baa a wɔde ahyɛ wo nsa no mu so.
- **Sohwɛfoɔ**: Wonya asɔre mmaa nyinaa, mpɔtam nyinaa ne nhyehyɛeɛ no nhyehyɛeɛ nyinaa mu kwan.

Yei yɛ kasa tiawa a yɛde di dwuma wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛmu yi nyinaa mu. Ɛnyɛ dibea a wɔbɛtumi de ama wo. Kairos kora dibea mmienu pɛ so, `admin` ne `member`, na nkaeɛ nyinaa firi **akyɛdeɛ** mu: dibea a wɔabɔ din a wɔde abata asɔre baa baako, fekuo baako, adwumakuo baako anaa asɔre no nyinaa ho. [Dibea ne Kwan a Wɔma](/tw/getting-started/roles-permissions) kyerɛ sɛdeɛ kasa tiawa yi ne deɛ ɛwɔ hɔ ankasa hyia.

## Sɛdeɛ wode saa nkyerɛkyerɛmu yi di dwuma

- Sɛ woyɛ foforɔ a, fa [Ɛdeɛn ne Kairos?](/tw/getting-started/what-is-kairos) ne [Nsɛm Atitire](/tw/getting-started/core-concepts) firi aseɛ.
- Sɛ woresiesie asɔre bi nnansa a, kɔ [W’asɔre a Ɛdi Kan](/tw/getting-started/your-first-church).
- Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wohunu deɛ obiara tumi yɛ a, hwɛ [Dibea ne Kwan a Wɔma](/tw/getting-started/roles-permissions).
- Sɛ wopɛ adeɛ pɔtee bi a, fa **Nhyehyɛeɛ no** afa a ɛwɔ nkyɛn hɔ no so.

## Deɛ Kairos ka ho

| Ɛfa | Deɛ ɛyɛ |
|--------|-------------|
| Asɔre Mmaa ne Mpɔtam | Hwɛ asɔre no mmeaeɛ ne sɛdeɛ wɔakyekyɛ no mu wɔ asase so |
| Asɔrefoɔ | Asɔrefoɔ nhwɛmu, wɔn nsɛm, ɔpene so, CSV mfa mu ne mfa firi mu |
| Asɔrefoɔ Adesua | Nnawɔtwe nnan adesua no, deɛ wɔpɛ sɛ wɔka ho, akuo, nkyerɛwee ne awieeɛ |
| Fekuo | K-Groups, Kharis Express, New Breeds ne nkaeɛ, a asase mfonini ka ho |
| Adwumakuo | Asomdwuma akuo a wɔn mufoɔ, nnwuma nhyehyɛeɛ ne adwumayɛfoɔ pɛ ka ho |
| Mmaeɛ | Ɔsom nkyerɛwee, w’ankasa nkyerɛwee a wofa QR so, ne fekuo nhyiam nkrataa |
| Nkɔdi Akyi ne Nsɛm a Ɛha Adwene | Ɔhwɛ nkitahodie nkrataa, ahɔhoɔ foforɔ ne wɔn a wɔnni kuo mu nhwɛmu, ne ahobammɔ nkrataa |
| Asɛmpaka | Asɛmpaka nhyehyɛeɛ, akra nkyerɛwee ne nkɔdi akyi |
| Gyidifoɔ Foforɔ | Asuafoyɛ kwan firi afɔrebukyia frɛ kɔsi adwumakuo mu kɔ |
| Nkrataa | Ɔhɔhoɔ foforɔ, asubɔ, adanseɛ, abɔfra dintoɔ ne nkaeɛ |
| Akra Dashboard | Akra kwan ne nsakraeɛ kwan ho nhwɛmu |
| Amanneɛbɔ ne Nsɛm Nhwɛmu | Nkɔsoɔ, mmaeɛ, nkitahodie ne asɛmpaka nsɛm |
| Wo Nsɛm ne Nhyehyɛeɛ | W’ankasa wo ho nsɛm, ahobammɔ, sɛdeɛ wopɛ sɛ ɛyɛ |
