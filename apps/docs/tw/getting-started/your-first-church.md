# W'Asɔre a Ɛdi Kan

<SectionClip slug="getting-started/your-first-church" />

Akwankyerɛ yi di sohwɛfoɔ anim wɔ Kairos nsiesieeɛ mu ma asɔre foforɔ, ɛfiri mfitiaseɛ. Sɛ w'asɔre no asiesie dada a, huri kɔ [Nhyehyɛeɛ](/tw/platform/dashboard) so.

## Ɔkwan 1: Yɛ Ɔmantam

Amantam yɛ mmeaeɛ a wɔde w'asɔre mmaa hyehyɛ mu. Firi ha.

Kɔ **Amantam** wɔ nkyekyɛmu no mu na mia **+ Ɔmantam Foforɔ**. Hyɛ ɔmantam din ne ɔman.

Nhwɛsoɔ a ɛfiri Kharis Church: `Greater Accra (Ghana)`, `United Kingdom`, `Western Area (Sierra Leone)`.

## Ɔkwan 2: Yɛ w'asɔre baa a ɛdi kan

Kɔ **Asɔre Mmaa** na mia **+ Asɔre Baa Foforɔ**. Hyɛ mu:

- Asɔre baa din (sɛ ɛbɛyɛ "Kharis London Central")
- Asɔre baa su (Kɛseɛ, Nkekaho, Sukuupɔn, Fekuo, anaa Intanɛt so)
- Ɔmantam
- Kuropɔn, akyerɛwdeɛ, telefon, ne imel

## Ɔkwan 3: Fa Ɔsɔfoɔ si hɔ

Sɛ w'asɔre baa no wɔ hɔ a, bue no firi **Me Asɔre Baa** so na fa **Akannifoɔ a Wɔwɔ hɔ Seesei** fa no yi Sɔfopanin. Asɔremufoɔ a wɔwɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no mu nko ara na wotumi de si hɔ.

## Ɔkwan 4: Fa Asɔremufoɔ ka ho

Wotumi de asɔremufoɔ ka ho baako baako fa **Asɔremufoɔ → + Fa Ɔmanfoɔ Ka Ho** so, anaa wode wɔn nyinaa ba prɛko fa **Fa CSV Bra** so. Ɛsɛ sɛ wode asɔremufoɔ hyɛ asɔre baa bi mu bere a woreyɛ wɔn.

Sɛ wode asɔremufoɔ ka ho wie a, wɔn a wɔkyerɛwee wɔn din ankasa no bɛpue wɔ **Ɔpene Nhwɛmu** mu wɔ Asɔremufoɔ kratafa atifi. Pene wɔn so na wɔanya akwannya.

## Ɔkwan 5: Siesie Fekuo

Kɔ **Fekuo → + Fekuo Foforɔ** na yɛ wo kuo ketewa a ɛdi kan. Fa kannifoɔ ma, yi fekuo su (K-Groups, Kharis Express, New Breeds, ne nkaeɛ), na fa nhyiamu nhyehyɛeɛ ka ho.

Kɔ **Adwumakuo → + Adwumakuo Foforɔ**. Yi asomdwuma su no (Nnwomtofoɔ, Akwankyerɛfoɔ, Sohwɛ, Ahɔhoyɛ, ne nkaeɛ), fa kannifoɔ ma, na sɛ wopɛ a, fa ɔboafoɔ nso ma.

## Ɔkwan 7: Kyekyɛ akwannya

Obiara nni tumi kɔsi sɛ wode bɛma. Hwɛ hunu obi a ɔhwɛ asɔre baa biara so na fa **Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ** tumi ma no firi ne nkrataa so; saa onipa no na ɔbɛtumi akyekyɛ deɛ aka wɔ ɔno ara asɔre baa mu. Bɔ fekuo biara kannifoɔ din wɔ fekuo no so na adwumakuo biara kannifoɔ din wɔ adwumakuo no so, na ɛyɛ saa tumi no ma wo.

Tumi mmienu yɛ asɔre no nyinaa dea na ɛyɛ sɛ wosi ho gyinae ntɛm: **Asɔremufoɔ Sohwɛfoɔ**, a ɔhwɛ asɔremufoɔ adesua nhyehyɛeɛ so, ne **Ahobammɔ Panin**. Paw asɔre baa biara **Sɔfopanin** nso wɔ **Me Asɔre Baa → Akannifoɔ a Wɔwɔ hɔ Seesei** mu, ɛfiri sɛ saa dibea no na ɛma ɔhunu n'asɔre baa ahobammɔ nhwɛmu.

Hwɛ [Akwannya](/tw/administration/permissions).

## Ɔkwan 8: Hyehyɛ w'asɔremufoɔ adesua a ɛdi kan

Sɛ wo din wɔ nkrataa no mu no nyɛ sɛ woyɛ Muni. Ɛkwan ankasa ne nnawɔtwe nnan adesua no, enti siesie kuo bi ntɛm: kɔ **Asɔremufoɔ**, yɛ kuo, hyehyɛ ne nhyiamu nnan, na ma nnipa nkyerɛ sɛ wɔn ani gye ho. Wobɛgye wɔn afiri twɛn nhwɛmu no mu bere a woasiesie wo ho.

Hwɛ [Asɔremufoɔ Adesua](/tw/platform/membership).

## Ɔkwan 9: Woasiesie wo ho

Sɛ asɔre mmaa, asɔremufoɔ, fekuo, adwumakuo, akwannya, ne adesua kuo a woahyehyɛ wɔ hɔ a, wo kuo no tumi hyɛ aseɛ kyerɛw mmaeɛ, di nkɔdi akyi nhwɛmu no ho dwuma, di asɛmpatrɛw akyi, na wɔhwɛ asuafoyɛ ɔkwan no so.
