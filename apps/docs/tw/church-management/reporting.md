# Nkaeɛbɔ

<SectionClip slug="church-management/reporting" />

Kairos wɔ nkaeɛbɔ a ɛwɔ mu a ɛhwɛ asɔre apɔwmuden so wɔ bere mu. Nkaeɛbɔ no gyina dibea so, enti bere biara wohunu nsɛm a ɛfata w'akwannya gyinabea.

## Kɔ nkaeɛbɔ so

Kɔ **Nkaeɛbɔ** wɔ nkyekyɛmu no mu. Kratafa no bue wɔ **Mmaeɛ** tab so. Akannifoɔ nya **Mmaeɛ**, **Nkɔanim**, ne **Asɛmpatrɛw**; obiara foforɔ nya **Mmaeɛ** ne **Asɛmpatrɛw**, a ɛbua nsɛmmisa a ɛfa wɔn ankasa ho na ɛnyɛ asɔre no ho.

Sɛ wopɛ mmaeɛ nhwɛmu a emu da hɔ, a ɔmanfoɔ biara mfonini ne mmaeɛ dodoɔ akuo wɔ mu a, kɔ **Mmaeɛ → Nkaeɛbɔ** mmom. Hwɛ [Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ](/tw/analytics/attendance-reports).

## Mmaeɛ nkaeɛbɔ

Mmaeɛ nkaeɛbɔ no kyerɛ:

- **Dapɛn Mmaeɛ Dodoɔ**: nsensaneeɛ mfonini wɔ nnawɔtwe 8 a atwam mu a ɛkyerɛ mmaeɛ mfimfini wɔ ɔha nkyekyɛmu mu
- **Nkɔsoɔ nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ**: sɛ mmaeɛ rekɔ soro anaa ɛresiane
- **Bosome Nsɛmpɔtee**: nhwɛmu a ɛfa deɛ ɛhia hwɛ ho
- **Fekuo a Nnipa Kɔ Mu Pii**: mfonini a ɛhyehyɛ fekuo so wɔ nhyiamu mmaeɛ mu

Fa yei hwehwɛ asɔre mmaa anaa mmere a mmaeɛ resiane, ansa na ayɛ ɔhaw kɛseɛ.

## Nkɔanim nkaeɛbɔ

Nkɔanim nkaeɛbɔ no kyerɛ:

- **Asɔremufoɔ Foforɔ**: asɔremufoɔ a wɔde kaa ho wɔ bere no mu, ne bosome ne bosome ntotoho
- **Asɔremufoɔ nkɔsoɔ**: mfonini a ɛkyerɛ asɔremufoɔ dodoɔ wɔ bere mu
- **Wɔn a Wɔsan Ba**: asɔremufoɔ a wɔsan baeɛ bere a wɔantena hɔ kakra akyi

## Asɛmpatrɛw nkaeɛbɔ

Asɛmpatrɛw nkaeɛbɔ no kyerɛ:

- **Akra a Wɔduruu Wɔn Nkyɛn**: deɛ wɔnyaa no nyinaa wɔ bere no mu
- **Nsakraeɛ dodoɔ**: ɔha nkyekyɛmu a ɛkɔɔ Wasakra tebea mu
- **Asɛmpatrɛw wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ biara mu**: nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛmaa akra pii baeɛ

## Akra Dashbɔd

Sɛ wopɛ akra ɔkwan no ho nhwɛmu pɔtee, a nsakraeɛ kwan, nkɔdi akyi apɔwmuden, ne afa mu nkɔsoɔ dodoɔ wɔ mu a, hwɛ **[Akra Dashbɔd](/tw/platform/souls-dashboard)** kratafa no.

## Yi nsɛm firi mu

Nhwɛmu dodoɔ biara wɔ Kairos mu wɔ CSV yidie. Firi Nkaeɛbɔ kratafa so:
- Fa nyiyimu te nsɛm no so
- Mia **Yi CSV** na twe

Asɔremufoɔ ne mmaeɛ nhwɛmu nso wɔ wɔn ankasa yidie bɔtin.

## Deɛ wohunu

| Hwan | Nkaeɛbɔ a ɔhunu |
|-----|-------------|
| Sohwɛfoɔ | Asɔre mmaa nyinaa, nsɛm nyinaa |
| Asɔre baa so tumi | Ɔno ara asɔre baa nko ara |
| Kuo kannifoɔ | Ɔno ara adwumakuo anaa fekuo nko ara |
| Tumi biara nni hɔ | Kratafa no a ɛfa ɔno ankasa adwuma ho |
