# Ɔmanfoɔ Akwantuo

<SectionClip slug="church-management/member-lifecycle" />

Akwankyerɛ yi fa ɔmanfoɔ akwantuo nyinaa wɔ Kairos mu ho, ɛfiri dinkyerɛw a ɛdi kan, kɔsi ɔpene, asɔremufoɔ a ɛrekɔ so, ne akyire yi yidie.

## 1. Dinkyerɛw

Ɔmanfoɔ foforɔ tumi kyerɛw ne din ankasa firi akawnt bue skrin no so sɛ ɔmia **Bue Akawnt**. Ɔhyɛ:

- Din a ɛdi kan ne abusua din
- Imel akyerɛwdeɛ
- Nsɛmfua a wɔde bue
- Telefon nɔma
- Ɛda a wɔwoo no ne sɛ ɔyɛ ɔbaa anaa ɔbarima
- Asɔre baa a ɔbɛkɔ
- Akyerɛwdeɛ (ɛho nhia wɔ dinkyerɛw ase)

Sɛ ɔde kɔ a, wɔyɛ n'akawnt wɔ **Ɛretwɛn** tebea mu. Ɔnya mmuaeɛ na ɔtumi bue, nanso ɔno ankasa nkrataa nko ara na ɔtumi hunu kɔsi sɛ wɔbɛpene so.

Anaa Ɔsɔfoɔ anaa Sohwɛfoɔ tumi de ɔmanfoɔ ka ho tẽẽ firi **Asɔremufoɔ → + Fa Ɔmanfoɔ Ka Ho**. Wɔn a wɔde wɔn ka ho saa no, wɔpene so ne ho ara.

## 2. Ɔpene

Dinkyerɛw a ɛretwɛn pue wɔ **Ɔpene Nhwɛmu** a ɛwɔ Asɔremufoɔ kratafa atifi. Sɛ wobɛpene so a, ɛhia asɔre baa so tumi, anaa nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ.

Sɛ wobɛpene ɔmanfoɔ so a:
1. Kɔ **Asɔremufoɔ → Ɔpene Nhwɛmu**
2. Hwɛ nsɛm a ɔde baeɛ no
3. Mia **Pene So**. Ntɛm ara ɔnya akwannya nyinaa
4. Anaa mia **Po**. Wɔyi n'akawnt firi hɔ

Sɛ wɔpene so wie a, ɔmanfoɔ no hunu ne dibea akwankyerɛ nyinaa na ɔtumi:
- Hwɛ na ɔsesa ne nkrataa
- Hwɛ ne fekuo ne n'adwumakuo
- De nkrataa kɔ
- Hwɛ ne mmaeɛ abakɔsɛm

## 3. Asɔremufoɔ a ɛrekɔ so

Ɔmanfoɔ a wɔapene so na ɔrekɔ so no tumi:
- Ma ne nkrataa yɛ foforɔ (nkitahodie, mfonini, ntɛmpɛ nsɛm)
- Bisa sɛ ɔbɛka fekuo bi ho
- Bisa sɛ ɔbɛka adwumakuo mmienu ho
- Kyerɛ sɛ n'ani gye asɔremufoɔ adesua ho
- Kyerɛw ne din ma asɛmpatrɛw nhyehyɛeɛ wɔ n'asɔre baa mu
- De nkrataa kɔ (Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ, Asubɔ, Adansedie, ne nkaeɛ)
- Hwɛ ne mmaeɛ abakɔsɛm wɔ Me Mmaeɛ so

## 4. Dibea a wɔde ma

Baabi a wɔde tumi ma gyina deɛ ɛbata ho so.

- **Asɔre baa ne asɔre no nyinaa tumi**, wɔde ma firi ɔmanfoɔ no dibea fa mu. Nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ tumi yɛ no baabiara; Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ tumi de Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ ma wɔ ɔno ara asɔre baa mu.
- **Fekuo ne adwumakuo tumi deɛ, kuo no na ɛde ma.** Bɔ obi din sɛ fekuo kannifoɔ anaa ɔboafoɔ, anaa adwumakuo kannifoɔ anaa ɔboafoɔ, na tumi no twerɛ ne ho ara. Sɛ woyi no firi kuo no mu a, ɛsan firi ne nsa. Wontumi mfa yeinom nka ho mfiri ɔmanfoɔ nkrataa so.

Nhwɛsoɔ:
- Wɔbɔ ne din sɛ fekuo bi kannifoɔ → ɛbue saa fekuo no mufoɔ nhwɛmu, nhyiamu, ne nkɔdi akyi
- Wɔbɔ ne din sɛ adwumakuo bi kannifoɔ → ɛbue saa adwumakuo no mufoɔ nhwɛmu, nnidisoɔ, ne nkɔdi akyi
- `BranchAdmin` a ɛbata asɔre baa ho → asɔre baa no adwuma nyinaa, ne tumi sɛ ɔde bɛma afoforɔ

**Bere a nsakraeɛ no yɛ adwuma:** sɛɛva no san kenkan tumi firi nkrataakoraeɛ no mu bisadeɛ biara mu, enti tumi foforɔ anaa deɛ wɔayi no yɛ adwuma ntɛm ara. Onipa no ankasa nkyekyɛmu ne bɔtin deɛ, ɛfiri ne akawnt bue nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ mu, enti ɛno sesa bere a nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ no yɛ foforɔ, anaa ntɛm ara sɛ ɔfiri mu na ɔsan bue a.

## 4b. Sɛdeɛ wobɛyɛ Muni

Sɛ wo din wɔ nhwɛmu no mu no nyɛ sɛ woyɛ Muni. Ɛkwan ankasa ne nnawɔtwe nnan asɔremufoɔ adesua no: kyerɛ sɛ w'ani gye ho, ma asɔremufoɔ sohwɛfoɔ nnye wo nkɔ kuo bi mu, kɔ nhyiamu nnan no na di nkonim, di nkonim wɔ nsɔhwɛ a ɛtwa toɔ mu, kɔ ahyɛdeɛ no ase, na wie.

Kɔsi saa bere no, wɔkan onipa no sɛ **Deɛ Ɔsan Ba** wɔ dashbɔd ne nkaeɛbɔ mu, na ɛnyɛ Muni. Hwɛ [Asɔremufoɔ Adesua](/tw/platform/membership).

## 5. Yidie

Sɛ woyi ɔmanfoɔ firi adwuma mu a, ɛyɛ yidie brɛoo. Ne nsɛm nyinaa (mmaeɛ nkrataa, nkɔdi akyi nsɛm, adwumakuo abakɔsɛm, adesua nkrataa) ka hɔ. Ɔntumi mmue bio nanso biribiara mfiri nkaeɛbɔ anaa abakɔsɛm nkrataa mu.

Sɛ wobɛyi obi firi mu a, ɛhia asɔre baa so tumi, anaa nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ.

Ɔmanfoɔ nso tumi yi n'ankasa akawnt firi **Nhyehyɛeɛ → Kokoamsɛm ne Nsɛm** so, na ɔtumi twe ne nsɛm kopi ansa na wayɛ saa.

Sɛ wobɛyi: bue ɔmanfoɔ kaad wɔ Asɔremufoɔ kratafa so na mia **Yi Firi Adwuma Mu**. Si so dua.

Sɛ wobɛsan de aba: hwehwɛ ɔmanfoɔ no fa **Ɔnni Adwuma Mu** tebea nyiyimu so na mia **Fa San Bra**.

## 6. CSV a wɔde ba ne deɛ wɔyi

Ma adwuma kɛseɛ:

- **Fa CSV Bra**: fa asɔremufoɔ tebo kɔ so na yɛ akawnt pii prɛko. Nhyehyɛeɛ no hwɛ nsɛntitire biara na ɛkyerɛ wo mfonini ansa na ɛde aba.
- **Yi CSV**: twe asɔremufoɔ nhwɛmu a woyiyii mu no. Ɛho wɔ mfasoɔ ma nkaeɛbɔ, nkrataa a wɔde kɔ abɔnten, anaa nsɛm a wode kɔ baabi foforɔ.

Mmienu nyinaa wɔ Asɔremufoɔ kratafa atifi ma obiara a ɔwɔ asɔre baa so tumi.
