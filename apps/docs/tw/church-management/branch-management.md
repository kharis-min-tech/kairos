# Asɔre Mmaa Nhwɛsoɔ

<SectionClip slug="church-management/branch-management" />

Asɔre baa a wɔbɛyɛ foforɔ ne deɛ wɔbɛyi afiri adwuma mu no yɛ Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara adwuma. Asɔre baa sohwɛfoɔ tumi sesa wɔn ankasa asɔre baa adwuma ho nsɛm na wɔpaw n'akannifoɔ, nanso wɔntumi mma asɔre baa mmra baabiara anaa wɔnyi bi mfiri hɔ.

## Yɛ asɔre baa foforɔ

Kɔ **Asɔre Mmaa → + Asɔre Baa Foforɔ** na hyɛ mu:

| Beaeɛ | Ɛho hia | Nsɛm |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Asɔre baa din | Aane | Ɛnsɛ bi wɔ ɔmantam no mu |
| Ɔmantam | Aane | Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛwɔ hɔ; yɛ ɔmantam no kane |
| Asɔre baa su | Aane | Kɛseɛ, Nkekaho, Fekuo, Sukuupɔn, anaa Intanɛt so |
| Kuropɔn | Daabi | |
| Akyerɛwdeɛ | Daabi | |
| Telefon | Daabi | Ɛnsɛ bi wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no nyinaa mu |
| Imel | Daabi | Ɛnsɛ bi wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no nyinaa mu |
| Ɛda a wɔhyɛɛ aseɛ | Daabi | Abakɔsɛm nkrataa nko ara |

## Fa ɔsɔfoɔ si hɔ

Sɛ woyɛ asɔre baa no wie a, fa ne Sɔfopanin si hɔ:

1. Kɔ **Me Asɔre Baa** (anaa bue asɔre baa no firi **Asɔre Mmaa** so)
2. Wɔ **Akannifoɔ a Wɔwɔ hɔ Seesei** fa no mu, fa ɔsɔfoɔ no ka ho
3. Asɔremufoɔ a wɔwɔ nhyehyɛeɛ no mu nko ara na wotumi de si hɔ
4. Asɔre baa biara tumi nya **Sɔfopanin baako** pɛ bere korɔ mu
5. Wotumi de Asɔfoɔ ka Sɔfopanin no ho

Sɛ wobɛpaw anaa woayi obi afiri dibea mu a, ɛhia asɔre baa akwannya safoa, enti nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ anaa asɔre baa no ankasa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ na ɔtumi yɛ. Asɔre Baa Nkrataa Sohwɛfoɔ ntumi.

::: tip Sɔfopanin kura akwannya baako
Asɔre baa akannidie yɛ dibea, na ɛnyɛ tumi a wɔde ma, nanso Sɔfopanin a ɔwɔ hɔ seesei no nya n'asɔre baa [ahobammɔ nkrataa beaeɛ](/tw/platform/concerns) ani ne ho ara. Sɛ wɔyi no firi dibea no mu a, ɛsan firi ne nsa. Hwɛ [Akwannya](/tw/administration/permissions#tumi-a-ɛfiri-dibea-mu-asɔre-baa-sɔfo).
:::

Dibea a wɔde ma no wɔ ɛda a ɛfiri aseɛ ne ɛda a ɛwie (sɛ wopɛ a), na ɛyɛ asɔre baa no akannidie abakɔsɛm nyinaa.

## Sesa asɔre baa ho nsɛm

Mia asɔre baa bi firi Asɔre Mmaa kratafa no so na sesa ne din, ne su, ne nkitahodie nsɛm, anaa n'ɔmantam. Nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ tumi sesa asɔre baa biara; Asɔre Baa Nhyehyɛeɛ Sohwɛfoɔ anaa Asɔre Baa Nkrataa Sohwɛfoɔ tumi sesa ɔno ara dea.

## Yi asɔre baa firi adwuma mu

Fa **Yi Firi Adwuma Mu** bɔtin a ɛwɔ asɔre baa kaad no so di dwuma. Yei yɛ yidie brɛoo: asɔremufoɔ nkrataa, mmaeɛ abakɔsɛm, ne saa asɔre baa no nsɛm nyinaa ka hɔ. Wɔde asɔre baa no sie firi nhwɛmu a ɛrekɔ so mu nanso ɛka nkaeɛbɔ ho.

## Hwɛ asɔre mmaa pii so

**Amantam Nyinaa** nyiyimu a ɛwɔ Asɔre Mmaa kratafa no so no ma wotumi yi asɔre mmaa no mu fa ɔmantam so. Ma asɔre a wɔwɔ aman pii so no, yei ma nhwɛmu no yɛ mmerɛ.

## Asɔre mmaa ntam ɛhyeɛ

Nsɛm no da nsow wɔ asɔre baa biara mu pintinn. Ɔsɔfoɔ a ɔwɔ Kharis London Central ntumi nhunu asɔremufoɔ, adwumakuo, fekuo, anaa nkaeɛbɔ a ɛfiri Kharis Accra. Sohwɛfoɔ nko ara na wɔhunu asɔre mmaa nyinaa.

Wɔhyɛ saa ɛhyeɛ yi API no so, na ɛnyɛ anim nyiyimu kɛkɛ.
