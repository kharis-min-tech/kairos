# Mmaeɛ Nhwɛsoɔ

<SectionClip slug="church-management/attendance-tracking" />

Kairos hwɛ mmaeɛ ahodoɔ mmienu so: **ɔsom mmaeɛ** ma Kwasida, nnawɔtwe mfimfini, ne ɔsom soronko, ne **fekuo nhyiamu mmaeɛ** ma akuo nketewa nhyiamu.

## Ɔsom mmaeɛ

Wɔhwɛ ɔsom mmaeɛ so firi **Mmaeɛ** kratafa a ɛwɔ nkyekyɛmu no mu.

### Hwan na ɔkyerɛw

Obiara a ɔwɔ asɔre baa so tumi, ne nhyehyɛeɛ sohwɛfoɔ.

Asɔremufoɔ nso tumi kyerɛw wɔn din ankasa, sɛ wɔn asɔre baa abue kyerɛw-wo-din-ankasa a: mia baako, anaa kenkan QR kod a ɛsesa a ɛwɔ ahɔhoɔgyeeɛ pon skrin so. Hwɛ [Mmaeɛ](/tw/platform/attendance#kyerɛw-wo-din-ankasa).

### Yɛ ɔsom nkrataa

1. Kɔ **Mmaeɛ**
2. Mia **+ Kyerɛw ɔsom**
3. Hyɛ asɔre baa, ɔsom su, ɛda, ne nsɛm a ɛho nhia (nsɛmti, ɔsɛnkafoɔ, asɛmti)
4. Sie. Ɔsom no pue wɔ nhwɛmu no mu

### Kyerɛw mufoɔ din

1. Hwehwɛ ɔsom no wɔ nhwɛmu no mu
2. Mia **Kyerɛw Wo Din →**
3. Hyɛ ɔmanfoɔ biara sɛ **Ɔwɔ hɔ**, **Ɔnni hɔ**, anaa **Intanɛt so**
4. Sɛ ɛho hia a, kyerɛ obi sɛ **Ɔhɔhoɔ Foforɔ**
5. Sie nkrataa no

### Ɔsom ahodoɔ

| Su | Bere a wode di dwuma |
|------|----------|
| Kwasida Ɔsom | Dapɛn biara ɔsom |
| Nnawɔtwe Mfimfini Ɔsom | Wukuda anaa ɛda foforɔ |
| Ɔsom Soronko | Afahyɛ anaa deɛ ɛba prɛko |
| Mpaeɛbɔ | Nhyiamu a mpaeɛbɔ nko ara wɔ mu |
| Foforɔ | Ɔkwan foforɔ biara |

---

## Fekuo nhyiamu mmaeɛ

Wɔhwɛ fekuo mmaeɛ so firi fekuo no ankasa kratafa so, na ɛnyɛ Mmaeɛ kratafa kɛseɛ no so.

### Hwan na ɔkyerɛw

Fekuo akannifoɔ ne wɔn aboafoɔ na wɔkyerɛw wɔn ankasa fekuo mmaeɛ. Asɔfoɔ ne Sohwɛfoɔ tumi kyerɛw ma fekuo biara a ɛwɔ wɔn asɔre baa mu.

### Yɛ nhyiamu nkrataa

1. Kɔ **Fekuo** na bue fekuo bi
2. Kɔ nhyiamu tab no so
3. Mia **+ Nhyiamu Foforɔ**
4. Hyɛ ɛda, nsɛmti, beaeɛ, ne nsɛm sɛ wopɛ a
5. Sie

### Gye mmaeɛ

1. Bue nhyiamu a ɛwɔ hɔ
2. Hyɛ ɔmanfoɔ biara sɛ **Ɔwɔ hɔ**, **Ɔnni hɔ**, **Wɔagye no afiri mu**, anaa **Akyire**
3. Fa ɔmanfoɔ biara ho nsɛm ka ho sɛ ɛho hia a
4. Sie

---

## Me Mmaeɛ (w'ankasa nhwɛmu)

Ɔmanfoɔ biara wɔ **Me Mmaeɛ** kratafa a ɛyɛ kokoamsɛm wɔ nkyekyɛmu no mu. Ɛkyerɛ:

- Nnawɔtwe 12 mmaeɛ dodoɔ ne nkɔsoɔ ho asɛm
- Ɔsom a wokɔeɛ ne ɔsom nyinaa a ɛwɔ bere no mu
- Ɛda ne ɔsom a wobaeɛ akyire pɛɛ
- Bere dodie nkyekyɛmu (Ɔwɔ hɔ, Akyire, Intanɛt so, Ɔyeraeɛ)
- Nnidisoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei
- Ɔsom abakɔsɛm nyinaa wɔ nnawɔtwe 12 no mu

Ɔmanfoɔ no, ne sɔfoɔ, ne sohwɛfoɔ nko ara na wɔhunu kratafa yi.

---

## Mmaeɛ wɔ nkaeɛbɔ mu

Ɔsom mmaeɛ nkɔsoɔ pue wɔ **Nkaeɛbɔ → Mmaeɛ tab** mu. Mfonini no kyerɛ mmaeɛ dodoɔ mfimfini wɔ nnawɔtwe 8 a atwam mu ma asɔre baa biara, ne nkɔsoɔ nsɛnkyerɛnneɛ.

Sɛ wopɛ deɛ emu da hɔ — ɔmanfoɔ biara mfonini, mmaeɛ dodoɔ akuo, ne ahɔhoɔ foforɔ ne wɔn a wɔsan ba nkyekyɛmu — bue **Mmaeɛ → Nkaeɛbɔ**. Hwɛ [Mmaeɛ Nkaeɛbɔ](/tw/analytics/attendance-reports).

Akannifoɔ hunu mmaeɛ nsɛm a ɛfa wɔn ankasa fekuo anaa adwumakuo mufoɔ ho nko ara.
