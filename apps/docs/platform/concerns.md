# Concerns

The **Concerns** page is the inbox for [follow-ups](/platform/follow-ups) that somebody flagged as needing attention. It has two tabs, holding two different kinds of concern, read by two different sets of people.

## Welfare and safeguarding are not the same thing

They are not two severity levels of one thing. They are separate domains, and Kairos keeps them separate on purpose.

| | Welfare | Safeguarding |
|---|---------|--------------|
| What it is | Pastoral care: somebody is struggling, bereaved, unwell, in financial or practical difficulty | Protection: a child-protection or vulnerable-adult concern |
| Who should act | The branch's pastoral leadership | Somebody independent of branch leadership |
| Who reads the tab | Branch System Admin | The branch's Main Pastor, the branch's Safeguarding Lead, and the church-wide Safeguarding Head |

A single follow-up can be flagged as both and will appear in both tabs.

## Why the reader lists differ

This is the part worth reading carefully, because it is the whole reason the split exists.

**Welfare used to ride on general branch access.** That meant a Branch Data Admin, somebody whose job is records and registers and who has no pastoral standing at all, could read both the welfare and the safeguarding inbox. Giving welfare its own permission fixed that.

**Safeguarding deliberately does not follow branch administration.** A Branch System Admin does not get the safeguarding tab from that grant. Safeguarding sight comes from one of three places:

- **Being the branch's Main Pastor.** Kairos works this out from the branch's leadership record, automatically, every time you make a request. It is not a grant anybody assigns.
- **Holding the Safeguarding Lead grant** for the branch. This is meant to be somebody other than the Main Pastor: the role exists to be independent of branch leadership.
- **Holding the Safeguarding Head grant**, which is church-wide and reads every branch's queue from one grant. This is the escalation route above a branch's lead, for when a concern raised in one branch needs to leave that branch entirely.

The consequence that catches people out: a **Minister** holding exactly the same Branch System Admin grant as their branch's Main Pastor does **not** see the safeguarding tab. Office, not grant, is what opens it. See [User Permissions](/administration/permissions#the-derived-grant-branch-pastor).

## What you see

Tabs appear only if you can read them. Somebody with safeguarding sight and no welfare sight sees one tab, not a welfare tab that refuses to open. If you can read neither, the page is not in your sidebar.

Each tab lists the flagged follow-ups for your branch, with the person, when the contact happened, and the notes. Open a card to go to the member's profile and the full follow-up history.

A Safeguarding Head's safeguarding tab is church-wide rather than branch-scoped, so it lists concerns from every branch.

## Concerns about people in no group

Flagged follow-ups are found by **branch**, not by group. A concern raised about somebody who belongs to no fellowship and no department still reaches the right inbox. This matters because that is often exactly who the concern is about.

## Raising a concern

You do not raise a concern on this page. You raise it by ticking **welfare concern** or **safeguarding concern** when you log a follow-up, from a fellowship, a department, the Follow-ups page, or a member's profile. See [Follow-ups](/platform/follow-ups#the-two-concern-flags).

::: warning Kairos is not the escalation
The inbox makes sure the right person sees it. It does not discharge your church's safeguarding policy, and it is not a substitute for ringing somebody. Follow your written procedure; use the flag so the record exists.
:::
