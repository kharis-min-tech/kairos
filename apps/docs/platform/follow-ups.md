# Follow-ups

Follow-ups are the record of pastoral contact: who reached out, how, what happened, and what should happen next. The **Follow-ups** page in the sidebar is the branch-level working list of people nobody has got to yet.

## Where a follow-up lives

Every follow-up is attached to a person, and also to a **context**: the setting the contact was made in. There are three:

| Context | Used when |
|---------|-----------|
| Fellowship | A fellowship leader following up one of their fellowship members. Logged from the fellowship. |
| Department | A department lead or deputy following up one of their department members. Logged from the department. |
| Branch | Branch-level pastoral contact, with no group involved. Logged from the Follow-ups page or the member's profile. |

The branch context is the one that changed recently, and it changed something important: **a person who belongs to no fellowship and no department can now be followed up.** Before, a follow-up had to hang off a group, so the people most in need of contact, the ones nobody had drawn in yet, were precisely the people the system could not record contact for.

The context is stated by whoever logs the follow-up. Kairos does not guess it from the person's memberships, because the setting of a conversation is a fact about the conversation, not about the person.

## The three queues

Open **Follow-ups** and you get three tabs. They are separate queues rather than one list because they are different kinds of work on different clocks.

### First-timers

Everybody who came through a **First-Time Visitor** form in your branch in the **last 60 days** and has had **no follow-up logged at all**, newest first.

This is a welcome on a clock of hours to days. The queue shows who invited them, where that was captured, so the person reaching out has somewhere to start.

A first-timer leaves this queue as soon as anybody logs any follow-up against them, in any context. The queue is "has anybody made contact", not "has anybody made the right kind of contact".

### No group

Active people in your branch who are in **no fellowship and no department**, newest record first.

This is a slower, steadier piece of work: somebody attending without being connected to anything. Children are excluded, because contact about a child goes through their guardian.

Unlike the first-timer queue, this one is not cleared by logging a follow-up. Somebody leaves it when they actually join a group, which is the outcome the queue exists to produce.

### Due

Follow-ups where somebody set a **next follow-up date** that has now arrived or passed, in your branch, for people who are still active.

This is the queue that keeps promises. When you log a follow-up and set a next date, this is where it comes back to you.

## Logging a follow-up

A follow-up is either a **contact** or a **visit**, and the form changes accordingly.

**Both kinds record:**
- The channels used, up to six, so a "called, then texted, then WhatsApped" attempt is one record rather than three
- Duration in minutes
- Notes
- An optional next follow-up date, which puts the person in the Due queue when it arrives

**A contact also records:**
- Whether you actually reached them
- Their interest level: interested, not interested, or undecided

**A visit also records:**
- In person or virtual
- Whether it was announced in advance
- Arrival and departure times
- The outcome: they were present, they were not present, or it was rescheduled
- Who went with you

## The two concern flags

Any follow-up can be flagged, independently, as a **welfare concern** or a **safeguarding concern**. Both, either, or neither.

They are deliberately separate things, not two levels of the same thing:

- **Welfare** is pastoral care. Somebody is struggling, bereaved, unwell, in difficulty.
- **Safeguarding** is protection. Something raises a child-protection or vulnerable-adult concern.

Flagging either one sends the follow-up to the matching tab of the [Concerns](/platform/concerns) inbox, which different people read. A follow-up can carry both flags and will appear in both inboxes.

::: warning Flag it rather than describing it
A safeguarding matter written only into the notes of an unflagged follow-up reaches nobody. The flag is what routes it.
:::

## Who can work the queues

| Action | Member | Leader | Pastor | Admin |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Open the Follow-ups page | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| First-timer, no-group, and due queues | No | No | Own branch | Any branch |
| Log a branch-context follow-up | No | No | Own branch | Yes |
| Log a fellowship or department follow-up | No | Own group | Yes | Yes |
| Read the welfare inbox | No | No | Branch System Admin only | Yes |
| Read the safeguarding inbox | No | No | Main Pastor or Safeguarding Lead only | Yes |

The three queues are branch-level work, so they need a branch grant. A platform admin can look at any branch's queues; everybody else sees their own branch and nothing else.
